### What the images show
Basis: 43 of 43 frame sheets viewed, all codes checked.

- Explicit measurement noted on screen: over a photo of an aurora, a blue circle with a larger dashed circle around it receives the label "Hysteresis 10pt" below it, and in the next frame the dashed line disappears and a thin white arrow enters indicating the direction of the recognized gesture (sheet 0035, q0311 to q0313). The quantity appears written in the image, not only in the speech.
- Anatomy of a button tap: the Calculator's "9" button goes from gray and smaller to full white and larger than its neighbors "8" and "x", and shortly after gains an external dashed circle with a white dot on the right edge, marking the tap margin that extends beyond the button's visible circle (sheet 0034, q0304 to q0306). In sheet 0035, q0307, the dashed circle reappears around the "8" button, now with an irregular white blotch inside it in place of the dot on the edge.
- Spring adjustment tool shown in action: a panel of three sliders labeled MASS, STIFFNESS and DAMPING with values and scales (2 of 4, 40 of 150, 10 of 100) sits next to the blue ball, and the values change between frames, with STIFFNESS rising from 40 to 100 and DAMPING from 10 to 40, the ball changing position along with it (sheet 0024, q0209 to q0214).
- Visible reparameterization of the same control: the panel swaps mass, stiffness and damping for two sliders, DAMPING in percentage and RESPONSE in seconds up to 1.5s, and the "value" by "time" graph changes shape according to the pair of values: it rises to the plateau without overshooting at 100% and 0.50s, creates a spike and oscillation at 34% and 0.50s, rises more slowly without overshoot at 70% and 1.00s (sheet 0025, q0217 to q0220), closing with a dense bundle of overlapping curves (q0223 and q0224).
- Technical graph used as proof of the detection method: a curve labeled "VERTICAL FINGER ACCELERATION" appears next to the app screens, practically straight, then with a sharp drop, and finally with a spike highlighted in green marking the instant the finger pauses (sheet 0009, q0077 to q0079). Before this sequence, in a different composition, without a graph, the tilted iPhone with the hand at the base of the screen receives the label "Timer too slow" next to it (q0076).
- Conceptual model communicated by changing the arrangement of the same blocks: four colored blocks labeled Thought, Decision, Gesture and Release appear in a row with a long arrow and in the next frame reorganize into a 2x2 grid without an arrow (sheet 0007, q0057 and q0058). The same device returns later, when alternating blocks of Interaction and Animation in a line give way to three blocks of Interaction feeding a single continuous orange bar of "Dynamic Behaviors" (sheet 0018, q0160 to q0162).
- Pairs of right and wrong marked in the image: the class names UITouches and UIPanGestureRecognizer stay above and UISwipeGestureRecognizer appears struck through by a large red X (sheet 0037, q0328 and q0329); two circles at the top of the overlapping frame curve are highlighted in red with the label "Too much visual change" (sheet 0015, q0127 to q0129); and an Inbox screen receives the label "Not spatially consistent" next to it (sheet 0010, q0090).
- Three-stage diagram for hinting: a small blue square labeled "Initial" and a large one labeled "Final" connected by an arrow, and then a third intermediate square labeled "Hinting" appearing between them with a glow around it (sheet 0011, q0094 and q0095), followed by the real Now Playing card growing from small to nearly the full width of the screen (q0097 and q0098).
- Comparisons that isolate one variable at a time: four hands touching the same white circle, labeled "no lag", "50ms", "100ms" and "200ms" (sheet 0005, q0040); two small dots with different spacing labeled "30fps looks smooth" and "30fps visually strobes", with the second label later swapped for "60fps less strobing at same speed" (sheet 0015, q0131 to q0134); and the trio Normal, Motion Blur and Motion Stretch in the same shape in motion (sheets 0015 and 0016, q0135 to q0137).
- Swift code with guided reading: a single block with syntax highlighting shows the function "project", then gains lines for decelerationRate, projectedPosition, nearestCornerPosition, x.target and y.target, and a gray highlight rectangle moves across the lines, first over the decelerationRate declaration and then over the nearest corner line, before the same code appears enlarged on the giant stage screen (sheet 0032, q0282 to q0287).
- Spatial diagram of PIP destinations: a vertical rectangle divided into four corner regions in thin outline, with a yellow rectangle representing the PIP jumping between the bottom right corner, a central position in transit, the bottom left corner and the top left corner (sheet 0030, q0266 to q0270); in the sequence the corners disappear, leaving a single background rectangle and the yellow element appears floating above its boundaries, depicting a hypothetical position (sheet 0031, q0271 to q0276).
- Component decomposed in isolated rendering: the on/off toggle appears outside any app screen, first off with the white knob on the left, then with the green track and elliptical shadow under the shifted knob, and finally only the knob floating with a shadow and without the track, isolating the elevated plane (sheet 0040, q0352 to q0355).
- Anatomy of a scrollable picker: a picker with three columns of values, a larger, lighter center line and adjacent lines losing contrast as they move away, changes from February dates to June dates between frames and then only adjusts opacity and sharpness, suggesting settling after scrolling (sheet 0028, q0244 to q0246).
- Color and light used as annotation of invisible data: a green light dot marks the exact point of contact and a gradient trail extends in the direction of movement, accompanied by the list "infer trajectory with: position, velocity, speed, force" (sheet 0012, q0101 to q0103); the same green dot stays lit in the corner of the button while the screen changes content, indicating continuous pressure (sheet 0013, q0110 to q0112); and the flashlight icon appears with a progress bar filling and, later, next to a graph of green and blue force spikes (sheets 0027 and 0036, q0238, q0239 and q0324).
- Fixed grammar of the text slides: black background, bold title at the top left, white bullets entering gradually, one or two per frame and never the whole list at once, and a hierarchy by opacity with the active item in full white and the next one anticipated in dimmed gray, a pattern repeated in the four-chapter agenda and in the section summaries (sheets 0004, 0017, 0021, 0033, 0041 to 0043, for example q0150 to q0153 and q0373 to q0378). The italicized emphasis word in the bullets is only recorded in the closing, in "inseparable" and "think" (sheet 0042).
- Concrete proof on the device, with a label naming the situation: iPhone X held in hand switches from Messages to the multitasking screen in the middle of the gesture (sheet 0006, q0052 and q0053), and further on the actions gain a caption written next to the screen, "Go to multitasking while app is launching" and "Interact with app as it launches" (sheet 0008, q0068 to q0070), plus the transition from the Dock to the app labeled "Soft transition from dock to app" (sheet 0014, q0122 and q0123).

Visual proportion: by the notes, almost all of the 43 sheets record some screen element, alternating text slides, diagrams, graphs, code and captures or demonstrations on iPhone, while the frames with only the presenter work mostly as a passage between blocks and a section close.

Recorded divergences or limits: the notes mark that part of what is seen is a didactic device of the talk and not real product interface, citing the light dots and color trails over the touch (sheets 0012 and 0013), the overlap of two captures to simulate navigation (sheet 0010), the isolated 3D toggle component (sheet 0040) and the colorful closing art with three iPhones (sheet 0043). They also record that the hierarchy by opacity in the lists is an editorial slide choice, not a UI pattern, and that the progressive accumulation of bullets and the isolation of the component are not evident from the speech transcript. In a pair of frames from the old mouse example the notes note that nothing changes besides the framing (sheet 0003, q0024 and q0025).
