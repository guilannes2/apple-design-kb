### What the images show
Basis: 10 of 10 frame sheets viewed, all codes checked.

- The same system sharing popover appears with an identical layout over very different content, a store product page and a fashion photo, always with the same internal order, row of suggested people at the top, grid of AirDrop, Messages, Mail and invite by link in the middle, copy actions at the bottom (sheet 0005, q0040 to q0045). Across the screens of a notes app, a lists app and the photo essay app, what repeats is the share button always in the same corner at the top (sheet 0006, q0046 to q0048).
- The choice between collaborating and sending a copy appears as a dropdown menu with two short lines, each with an icon on the left, which then closes and leaves in its place only the permission summary line, "Everyone can make changes", working as the entry point to the settings screen (sheet 0006, q0049 to q0051).
- The collaboration popover is shown divided into three fixed vertical blocks, top with the people and three communication buttons, variable middle and bottom with a single management button (sheet 0008, q0065 to q0070). The middle block switches content between two apps in the same frame, participant list with a toggle in the photo essay app, recent activity with shortcut buttons in the notes app, which makes visible the customization that the speech only describes.
- The transition from the popover to the full management screen is done by dissolve, the popover dissolves under the new screen until it becomes sharp, and the same management screen reappears semi-transparent over another app in the background (sheet 0009, q0073 to q0077). The final screen carries a participant list with a status dot per row, a block of help text and an isolated red destructive button in the footer.
- The notification banners follow a single template, circular avatar on the left, two lines of text in the center, blue action button on the right and a dismiss X, repeated for document editing, comment, mention, creation and deletion (sheet 0010, q0079 to q0085).
- The consolidation of notifications is only evident when comparing frames, the stack of individual banners builds up, the oldest ones fade and then the whole stack is replaced by a single banner with a count of people and documents, with the button switching from "Show" to "View" (sheet 0010, q0082 to q0086).
- The complete flow is demonstrated on an iPad, document open, sharing popover with the collaborate option checked, message composition with the file card already attached and the system keyboard, switching the recipient to an existing group, notices of participants joining the document and finally the collaboration popover with the list of who is in (sheet 0003, q0019 to q0027).
- Communication without leaving the context appears in two distinct frames, a video call overlaid on the document itself with participants' initials in colored circles and a controls bar at the bottom, and an iPad in split screen where the Messages conversation alongside it receives the edit banner with a shortcut button (sheet 0004, q0030 to q0033).
- In the notes app, a small circular avatar comes to occupy the top right corner of the header, absent in the previous frame and present in the next one (sheet 0007, q0061 to q0063).
- The same block hierarchy appears on three devices, iPad, iPhone and Mac, adapting only the window frame, cursor and system menu bar (sheets 0003, 0006 and 0007). On the Mac, the conversation's permissions menu is a vertical list of three options in plain text, with no icons (sheet 0007, q0056 to q0057).
- Color carries function in the components seen, communication buttons in filled orange rounded squares, default system toggle for participant cursors, colored status dots per person and red reserved for the stop sharing button (sheets 0008 and 0009).
- The agenda slide works as a progress bar for the session, gaining a highlighted item at each topic change, from "Adopt system share sheet" to "Customize collaboration popover", and reappears at the close as a recap list (sheets 0005 to 0010, q0054, q0058, q0064, q0078, q0087 to q0089).
- The opening uses a narrative device instead of interface, the isolated email icon is replaced by an avatar with an envelope stamp, and then all the envelope stamps around an app card turn into the green Messages icon, staging the channel switch before any product screen (sheet 0001, q0008 to q0009; sheet 0002, q0010 to q0015).

Visual proportion: across the sheets, most of the frames are product mockup or agenda slide, with the presenter concentrated in the opening, in short section-change blocks and at the close.

Recorded divergences or limits: the notes point to pairs of frames with no relevant visible change, such as the pair of the Pages popover on the Mac, where only the cursor repositions (sheet 0006, q0052 to q0053), and the pair of iPhone screen triplets with no apparent change between them (sheet 0007, q0059 and q0060).
