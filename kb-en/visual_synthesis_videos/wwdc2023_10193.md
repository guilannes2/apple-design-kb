### What the images show
Basis: 9 of 9 frame sheets viewed, all codes checked.

- The Spotlight card has a constant structure across all interface sheets: the small "Top Hit" label at the top, a rounded translucent card with one to four action icons side by side and a short caption under each one, below it a "Suggestions" section with results in a list, and the search field with keyboard at the bottom of the screen (sheets 0001, 0002, 0005, 0006, 0007 and 0008). The speech describes the function of this card, the screen is what shows the layout.
- The removal of one shortcut is shown as a difference between two frames: the clock card appears with four shortcuts and, in the next frame, with three, without "Add City" (sheet 0003, q0017 to q0018).
- Phone appears in both contexts in neighboring frames: first the app with the Favorites list and the bottom tabs, then the Phone Top Hit card with the call button (sheet 0003, q0019 to q0020). In contact shortcuts, the circular profile photo carries a small phone icon overlaid in the corner (q0022 and q0023).
- The right-versus-wrong comparison is made explicit with a red X overlaid between two versions of the same four-shortcut Freeform card, one of them with the long title that repeats the app name (sheet 0005, q0039 to q0040).
- The order of the Notes list is shown by addition: the same "All iCloud" list appears first with only the note counter in the footer and then with a "Pinned" section at the top, with two thumbnail cards, and a "Yesterday" section below (sheet 0004, q0028 to q0029).
- The two blocks that make up an App Shortcut get their own icon on the slide: a yellow lightning bolt inside an outline for "Action" and a brown 3D box for "Entity", side by side with a label (sheet 0004, q0035).
- Entity icons repeat the shape the concept has inside the app, case by case: circle for contacts and for Reminders lists, square for Photos albums, with details carried along, like the small heart in the corner of the favorites album (sheets 0005 and 0006).
- The principle slides are cumulative: under the title "Ideation" the first line appears alone and the second is added in the next frame, with the title fixed at the top (sheet 0004, q0031 to q0033). The same device repeats in "Creation", which gains a second line and is then reframed into a list of three sections with the current one in bold (sheet 0007, q0061 to q0063).
- The guidance on color is made concrete as a sample: a grid of twelve colored diamonds with rounded corners, with no label, in three rows of four (sheet 0006, q0049).
- Reminders is shown in both contexts at the same time: the Top Hit with four shortcut icons and, next to it, the full screen of the app with numbered cards in a two-by-two grid, each category with its own color (sheet 0005, q0042 to q0045).
- The two examples of discovery by synonym appear as real result cards: "Scan Document", with a scanner icon, a small blue tag in the corner and an ellipsis menu, found by searches like "File Scanner" (sheet 0008, q0065 and q0066), and "Recognize Music", which uses a distinct blue background inside the card, a waveform icon and a subtitle indicating Shazam, with search suggestions by the alternative name (sheet 0008, q0070).
- The related sessions slides have their own pattern: a thin horizontal line above each item, the session name on the left and the event code aligned to the right (sheet 0007, q0057 and q0060).
- In the Shortcuts app the shortcuts appear grouped by app in two treatments: colored cards in the "All Shortcuts" list (sheet 0002, q0012) and dark rectangular cards with thumbnails that resemble the corresponding element, like the red recording circle of Voice Memos and the clock icon of Clock (sheet 0006, q0050 to q0052).

Visual proportion: per the notes, almost all frames are iPhone and iPad screens in Spotlight, Shortcuts app screens or list slides, with the presenter appearing in frames of their own as well as next to the reference slides, always in the same setting, plus the animated opening (sheet 0001) and the closing with the WWDC23 mark (sheet 0009).

Recorded divergences or limits: the notes point out frames practically identical to each other, with no perceptible change, in q0065 to q0066 and in the two closing frames (q0073 and q0074); they also record a blurred transition between screens in sheet 0002 (q0009 to q0011) and an almost blank frame with faded text before the music recognition example (sheet 0008, q0068). In sheet 0003 the numbering records the same presenter frame revisited with a different code.
