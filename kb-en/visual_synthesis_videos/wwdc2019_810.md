### What the images show
Basis: 17 of 17 frame sheets viewed, codes checked.
- The talk builds a fixed visual notation for what cannot be seen: a continuous sine wave, then a single peak labeled "Transient", then the same transient reduced to a solid blue rectangle, and finally three shapes side by side forming the sharpness spectrum, a drop "Round, Soft", a bar and a triangle "Crisp, Precise" (sheet 0003, q0019 to q0026). The complete trio returns identically in the axis chart on sheet 0013 (q0111); on sheet 0009 only the small blue triangle reappears, in the same style as the "Crisp, Precise" icon (q0079), and on sheet 0015 the blue bar and orange triangle return within the tracks. It is a notation system, not a one-off illustration.
- The two control dimensions only become quantitative on screen: the "Core Haptics Transient" slide has labeled Cartesian axes, Haptic Intensity from 0.0 to 1.0 on the vertical and Haptic Sharpness from 0.0 to 1.0 on the horizontal, with the three shapes positioned along the sharpness axis (sheet 0013, q0110 and q0111). The continuous receives the same pair of axes, but occupied by modulated waves with a short envelope and then a long one (sheet 0013, q0112 to q0114).
- The central graphic device is the alignment of two tracks on the same time axis, "Sound" above and "Haptic" below, recurring on sheets 0006, 0013, 0014 and 0015 always with the same layout. A thin red vertical line marks the temporal reference point on part of these charts (sheets 0013 and 0014). It is what makes visible what the speech describes only in words.
- The Apple Pay design iteration appears as a shape swap within that track: the same two-peak sound with a long tail is first paired with a decreasing orange triangle and, in the next frame, that triangle is replaced by two short, spaced vertical blue bars (sheet 0006, q0047 to q0052). The final result closes with the real Apple Pay capture, a blue progress circle turning into a check mark with "Done" over the Visa card (sheet 0006, q0053 and q0054).
- Anticipation is taught through displacement: the haptic triangle goes from aligned with the sound peak to sitting before it, under the title "Anticipation", and the sound line even appears tilted diagonally instead of horizontally (sheet 0014, q0118 to q0122).
- The ghost effect becomes an on-screen fact: four vertical blue bars of slightly different heights under "Number of Events" and, in the following frames, the first bar darkens and loses visual weight relative to the other three (sheet 0014, q0123 to q0125). The priming is the same diagram with a thin blue bar added before the orange triangle (sheet 0015, q0127 to q0129).
- The contrast between left and right cues is communicated through rhythmic density, not shape: in "Contrast, Left" each sound group gets a pair of blue bars; in "Contrast, Right" the bars grow progressively into an almost continuous sequence of nine thin bars, with a white diagonal line crossing the sound peaks that did not exist on the left side (sheet 0015, q0130 to q0135).
- Harmony is demonstrated with a prototype stripped down to the minimum: an iPhone frame with a single dot as the entire content, falling from the center to the bottom, flanked by two fixed columns; the "Real World" column already has Visual, Audio and Haptic complete and the "Digital World" column gains these items one by one, and the dot switches from blue to green to restart the demonstration (sheet 0007, q0057 to q0063). A fourth item, "Synchronization", is added to both columns next (sheet 0008, q0064 and q0065).
- Real hardware photography serves as an argument, not decoration: the Taptic Engine appears inside the opened iPhone with a label overlaid on the component (sheet 0002, q0011 to q0016), and macro shots of golden mechanical watchmaking gears lead to a close-up of the knurled digital crown with a red ring (sheets 0009 and 0010, q0080 to q0086). The image shows texture and material that the speech only names.
- The fit between the physical turn and the screen is recorded frame by frame: the Apple Watch app list changes from "Activity, Alarms, Breathe, Calendar" to "Alarms, Breathe, Calendar, Camera, Commute" between two frames, with a vertical green indicator bar next to the physical crown (sheet 0010, q0087 to q0090).
- The slide navigation pattern is a hierarchy by opacity: current item in full white, next topic in dimmed gray, list growing by accumulation. "Utility" gets "Moderation", then "Focus", then "Keep it simple", and "Guiding Principles" is completed with "Techniques" still dimmed below (sheet 0012, q0100 to q0105); the same pattern already appeared in the opening agenda (sheet 0001, q0007 and q0008).
- Each conceptual block closes with real material rather than a diagram: genuine screen captures of Apple Pay (sheet 0006), the Watch app list (sheet 0010) and an iMessage conversation receiving the red full-screen effect over the bubbles (sheet 0016, q0137 to q0140), plus the live-filmed video of the AR app, with a dark cube anchored over an electric pot (sheet 0011, q0094 to q0099).
- Dissolve transition between concept text and live scene is a recurring technique: the word "Synchronization" in large type overlaid in transparency on the presenter and the mockup (sheet 0008, q0066 and q0067), and the presenter's silhouette overlaid on the gear macros (sheet 0009, q0080 to q0082). The speech mentions none of this.
Visual proportion: most sheets record a slide, diagram, product photo or app capture; frames with only the presenter appear interspersed between blocks (for example q0010, q0027, q0046, q0097, q0098, q0106, q0145), and sheet 0017 ends on the closing slide with the URL and the table of two labs.
Recorded divergences or limits: the Apple Watch capture shows the partial text "laying", noted as probably "Playing" (sheet 0010, q0087 and q0088); on sheet 0004 the list of principles appears with "Causality" and "Harmony", without "Utility" in that frame (q0029); between q0073 and q0074 the green dot does not change size perceptibly (sheet 0009); the last frame of sheet 0017 is noted as probably full screen with the presenters out of frame (q0148).
