### What the images show
Basis: 34 of 34 frame sheets viewed, all codes checked.

- The slide system is the same from opening to closing: solid black background, large light-weight white title, medium gray subtitle, hyphen bullets in light gray, and the illustrative image to the right of the text block when the two coexist. The accent palette is restricted to green and the brand colors of the technology icons, with no other emphasis color (sheet 0001, q0002 to q0004; sheet 0018, q0154 to q0162; sheet 0034, q0299 to q0305). The variations recorded in the notes are of composition, not palette: centered single-sentence slides (sheet 0021, q0183 to q0185) and the list of recommended languages in centered italics (sheet 0030, q0262 to q0264).
- Each of the eleven items on the list gets a dedicated transition slide with the same anatomy: a thin hollow green circle with the number inside, centered, and the item's title in white right below (sheet 0003, q0027; sheet 0020, q0175 and q0176; sheet 0022, q0198; sheet 0029, q0253; sheet 0033, q0293). The background is flat black in all of them, except in the eleventh, where the same green circle appears over the blurred photo of the amplifier (sheet 0033, q0293).
- There is a color-based progress system that the speech never verbalizes: recap slides bring the entire numbered list, with the items already covered in muted gray and the one just completed with a circle and text in vivid green. The pattern repeats at least six times and dissolves at the end, when the final list of eleven items appears entirely in uniform white, with no highlight, signaling conclusion (sheet 0022, q0197; sheet 0027, q0236; sheet 0028, q0252; sheet 0030, q0267; sheet 0033, q0291 and q0292; sheet 0034, q0298).
- Bullet lists are built item by item across several frames, without changing slides and with the image alongside staying still: the "Key takeaways" list for the core loop grows from one to five items across nine frames, and the sixth only comes in on the following sheet (sheet 0011, q0091 to q0099; sheet 0012, q0100 to q0102). The same mechanism appears in the NSURLSession bullets (sheet 0022, q0192 to q0193 and q0194 to q0195) and in the three lines of "State of the Art" (sheet 0031, q0271 to q0277).
- The core loop diagram is a fixed graphic object reapplied with swapped labels: three blue circles with white icons (a bolt for Action, a star for Reward, layers for Expansion) connected by arrows in a cycle. The same drawing receives "Fantasy/RPG" (q0083 and q0084), then "Platformer" (q0085 to q0087), and finally duplicates side by side in "Multiple loops" with two sets of labels (q0088 to q0090, sheet 0010). The speech cites the genres and the overlap of more than one loop; what only the image delivers is that it is the same drawing reused, with only the labels swapped.
- This same diagram later gains a dashed outer ring with three more labels, Interaction, Progression, and Expansion mechanic (sheet 0013, q0113 and q0114), and comes to function as a fixed anchor for the topic: on sheet 0018 it remains identical across nine frames while the bullet block alongside it takes on three different contents, in two swaps (q0154 to q0162). The template is also reused for another topic, with sensory labels Touch, See, Feel, Hear (sheet 0016, q0141 and q0142).
- There is a single explicit right-and-wrong pair in the video, and it is marked graphically: a red circular icon with an X in the top right corner of the iPhone frame signals the bad example, a simple game with a virtual D-pad on the left and A, B, X, Y buttons on the right overlaid on the scene (sheet 0013, q0116 and q0117; sheet 0014, q0118 to q0121). The examples considered correct that follow receive no marking at all, not even a positive one (sheet 0014, q0125 and q0126; sheet 0015, q0127 to q0136).
- The device frame appears in different uses, and the notes do not support a single rule of meaning: it frames the fictional app as if it were running on the player's device (sheets 0004 to 0006; sheet 0020, q0177 to q0180), it frames the bad example of virtual controls (sheet 0013, q0116 and q0117; sheet 0014, q0118 to q0121), and it also frames captures of already published games and of the App Store itself (sheet 0014, q0126; sheet 0015, q0127 to q0131; sheet 0027, q0240 to q0243). Other captures of a published game occupy the entire frame, with no device frame at all (sheet 0007, q0063; sheet 0008, q0064 to q0072; sheet 0012, q0104 to q0108; sheet 0016, q0136 and q0137).
- Native iOS components are reproduced faithfully within the slides, with action hierarchy signaled by color: a registration modal with an orange title, black body on white, and a green button for "Register" and gray for "No Thanks" (sheet 0005, q0041 and q0042); a settings screen in a table view with a label on the left, a value on the right, and a disclosure arrow per row (sheet 0006, q0048 to q0052); a system alert in a light box with rounded corners, text centered on two lines, and two text buttons separated by a thin dividing line, Cancel and OK (sheet 0021, q0181 and q0182).
- In the onboarding case study, the hints appear in handwritten font overlaid on the game scene itself, close to the element they explain, with a hand icon indicating the gesture, never in a separate dialog box; the top HUD with stars, energy, and menu stays fixed on every screen, and each new mechanic comes in with its own hint while the previous one leaves (sheet 0007, q0063; sheet 0008, q0064 to q0072).
- The real tools appear on screen with readable numbers that the speech only approximates. Xcode's Graphics panel shows an FPS gauge with a pointer over a red, yellow, and green band, two purple Utilization bars, two green Frame Time bars with 12 ms and 23 ms, and a Program Performance table per shader with frame percentage and a horizontal bar (sheet 0024, q0214 to q0216; sheet 0025, q0217 to q0221). The following drill down shows the shader inspector with the cost annotated to the right of each GLSL line, with 59.3% being the highest percentage recorded in the notes (sheet 0025, q0223 to q0225).
- Real admin panel used as proof: the iTunes Connect retention screen fills the width of the window, with columns numbered 1 to 12, rows of dates, an installs column and cells in a blue gradient working as a heat map of the drop-off over the days (sheet 0019, q0171; sheet 0020, q0172 and q0173).
- To explain project configuration the presentation shifts register and uses a dry technical diagram, with no decoration at all: stacked boxes with 8, 7, 6.1, 6, 5.1 and 5, a thin yellow frame outlining only the top two, and two arrows labeled Base SDK and Deployment Target pointing at them. Between two consecutive frames the older versions become even more faded and the arrow shifts to point more precisely at the 7 (sheet 0031, q0278 and q0279).
- The editing avoids a hard cut in the passage between concept and proof: the old content becomes semitransparent for one or two frames while the new one emerges underneath, especially when a conceptual slide gives way to a capture of a real tool (sheet 0023, q0202; sheet 0024, q0213; sheet 0025, q0222; sheet 0026, q0229; sheet 0030, q0265). By contrast, the switch between text slides tends to be a hard cut (sheet 0021, q0183 to q0185).
- Two emphasis-by-contrast techniques appear in isolation. In the optimization recommendations grid, all the short phrases stay in dull gray and the word "Optimize" enters large and white overlaid at the center, creating layers of reading (sheet 0024, q0209 to q0212). And the section-opening word clouds use the same principle, larger central term in solid white, peripheral ones in translucent gray (sheet 0003, q0024 to q0026; sheet 0017, q0145 and q0146).
- The only two photographs of real physical objects in the whole video carry the argument alone: the chip with the Apple logo and the A7 engraving on the metal casing (sheet 0030, q0270) and the gold panel of a vintage amplifier with four brushed metal potentiometers, labels GAIN, VOLUME, TREBLE and MID, and a scale numbered up to 11 instead of 10 (sheet 0033, q0294 and q0295). The speech states the expression, the image delivers the reference.

Visual proportion: according to the notes, practically the whole video is full-screen slides, device frames, game captures and captures of real tools (Xcode, iTunes Connect, App Store, internationalization site); no sheet records a frame with the presenter on screen, and the only frame with no content is the initial black one (sheet 0001, q0001).

Recorded divergences or limits: sheets 0018 to 0034 were recorded twice, in two independent passes, and they do not match in every detail. The optimization recommendations grid is described as fifteen items in one pass and as fourteen in the other, with the literal enumeration bringing fourteen phrases, while the speech refers to fifteen recommendations. The two passes also diverge on the recap slide for items 1 to 10: one records the earlier items in dull gray, the other in full contrast, with no dimming (sheet 0033, q0291 and q0292). Some games are identified in the notes by visual style and not by on-screen label, the case of Monument Valley (sheet 0014, q0126) and of the dot pattern attributed to Blek (sheet 0015, q0128). Frame q0136 appears counted both at the end of sheet 0015 and at the start of sheet 0016.
