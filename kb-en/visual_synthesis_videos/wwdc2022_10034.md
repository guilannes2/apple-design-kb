### What the images show
Basis: 16 of 16 frame sheets viewed, all codes checked.

- The wireframe appears as a real intermediate step, not as a figure of speech, the same three App Store screens are reduced to gray blocks for title, image, text, pagination dots and tab bar, and only afterward come back as real screens in Arabic, with the composition mirrored (sheet 0003, q0021 to q0027).
- The carousel inversion is proven with numbering and arrows under each device, the printed order changes from 1, 2, 3 left to right with arrows on the right, to 3, 2, 1 with arrows on the left, in the same set of three screens (sheet 0004, q0028 to q0032).
- There is a fixed pattern of annotation over the interface itself, always a small label with a thin line connecting to the element, used for every new component discussed, "Temperature Scale", "Pagination", "Dates Progress", "Time Charts" and "Image" (sheets 0004 to 0006, q0036 to q0049).
- The side-by-side comparison of screens in English and in Arabic isolates what inverts and what does not invert, the temperature scale bar swaps ends between the two versions, while the sky background with sun is shown enlarged as the same element in both (sheet 0004, q0034 to q0036; sheet 0005, q0038 to q0039).
- A cultural layer is flagged only by a colored mark, thin red lines under certain day numbers on the calendar, with no explanatory text inside the screen (sheet 0005, q0043 to q0044).
- The typographic study takes the letter out of context and breaks it down, four forms of the same letter in a row with position labels under each one, then an entire grid of glyph variations in gray on black, an image the speech uses to talk about the size of an Arabic font's library (sheet 0007, q0055 to q0058).
- The pattern of comparing two versions with a measurement label repeats in at least three sections, a word in English in an orange box against a word in Arabic in a purple box, with the label "Concise" and later "Taller" when the purple box grows in height (sheet 0007, q0059 to q0061), and later the same typographic studies with spacing and opacity labels (sheet 0011, q0091 to q0096).
- The type family is presented as a weight table, the same word repeated in rows from thinnest to thickest with a label at each end, always next to a screenshot of a real app using those weights in title, body and numerals (sheet 0008, q0064 to q0069; sheet 0009, q0078 to q0081).
- Optical size gets two figures, a vertical stack of the same word in decreasing sizes and a large version with small circles marking points on the shape of the letters, next to the title style and text style labels (sheet 0008, q0070 to q0072; sheet 0009, q0073).
- The spacing adjustment is quantified on screen, the same text appears with labels of 0, 10 and 20 percent, and at the higher value a thin lilac bar appears joining two letters, a graphic representation that does not repeat in any other annotation in the video (sheet 0011, q0091 to q0094).
- The transparency problem is shown directly, the word at reduced opacity lets the joins between overlapping letters show through, with the value noted below the example (sheet 0011, q0095 to q0096).
- The uppercase question is resolved with a comparative pair and an arrow, text in normal case with a downward arrow and the same text in uppercase with an upward arrow, always next to the Arabic translation, and later shown in real store screens with that header in uppercase in both languages (sheet 0010, q0086 to q0090).
- The icon adaptation is presented as a grid of pairs, one row for each language, with purple highlighting only on the symbol that changed and the rest in white, making clear that the inversion is selective (sheet 0012, q0102 to q0107). In the final set, the icons in the Arabic version are not just mirrored, they carry Arabic letters and numerals drawn inside the glyph itself (sheet 0012, q0108).
- The real tool appears on screen, the symbols app on the Mac with a sidebar of categories, an icon grid and a details panel showing a list of localizations by script inside the symbol itself (sheet 0013, q0109 to q0110).
- The two numeral systems are separated by a simple caption below each number and then appear applied in four app contexts, settings with a checked item, calculator with a mirrored keypad, calendar and watch face, the last with quite distinct shapes and colors between the two versions (sheet 0014, q0118 to q0124).
- The closing combines documentation and visual recap, the guidelines page on reading direction open on a MacBook, with two example mockups at the bottom, and then a grid collage gathering thumbnails of almost all the examples already shown (sheet 0015, q0127 to q0132).

Visual proportion: across the sheets, product screens, isolated typographic studies and annotated comparisons predominate, with the presenter appearing in short blocks between sections, and the last sheet carries only the closing logo.

Recorded divergences or limits: several sheets record practically identical frames, such as the statistics card repeated at the opening (sheet 0001, q0006 and q0007), the reminders screen (sheet 0009, q0080 and q0081) and the presenter's last two frames, which change only in expression (sheet 0014, q0125 and q0126).
