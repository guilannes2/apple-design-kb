### What the images show
Basis: 20 of 20 frame sheets viewed, all codes checked.
- The translucent capsule with fully rounded corners is the default shape of almost every control shown: messages toolbar (sheet 0002, q0016 to q0018), bar with three icons over flowers and sand (sheet 0004, q0028 and q0029), media controls in individual circles (sheet 0004, q0030), navigation bar and switch (sheet 0006, q0047 to q0050).
- The growth of a button into a menu is shown frame by frame, not just stated: the circular share button grows as the finger approaches and opens into a vertical list of options with geometric icons on the left (sheet 0005, q0039 to q0045); the same logic reappears in the attachments bar that expands into a list of seven items (sheet 0009, q0076 and q0077).
- The same component, with fixed shape and icons, changes tone depending on the content behind it: the capsule with three icons over yellow flowers and then over a dune (sheet 0004, q0028 and q0029), and the three video control circles changing color with each animation scene (sheet 0015, q0127 to q0129).
- Many frames show the component outside any app, as a study piece on a neutral background: thick circular lens (sheet 0002, q0011), isolated capsule with an elongated shadow cast below (sheet 0002, q0018), two translucent blocks on a bar seen from above and then in perspective (sheet 0009, q0079 and q0080), rounded corner with two circles and a bar rotating in 3D (sheet 0014, q0120 and q0121).
- There is a pair of frames of a component library over a perspective grid, with two sliders (one blue, one in a colored gradient), a list of options, a green switch, an empty capsule and a circle with a plus sign, rearranged and resized from one frame to the other (sheet 0008, q0065 and q0066). The speech does not describe this inventory; the screen documents it.
- The tinting counterexample is built as a visible progression: on the checkout screen, the translucent cart button turns solid red, then the solid red takes up the entire width of the bottom bar, and finally the whole screen turns opaque yellow (sheet 0018, q0158 to q0162).
- The color variable is isolated in a controlled test: the same circular photo icon over the same wavy wood background appears tinted magenta and then blue (sheet 0018, q0155 and q0156).
- Typography is treated as material, not just as text: the "9:41" clock receives glass distortion over foliage, with brightness varying between two frames in the same position (sheet 0012, q0104 and q0105), and changes color (pink, green, wine) depending on the photo of each lock screen (sheet 0017, q0146 to q0148).
- The comparison of variants appears as an assembled pair: the same translucent white capsule over a background that is half light and half dark (sheet 0016, q0138 and q0139), followed by a solid dark block with rounded corners alone on black (sheet 0016, q0140), contrasting with the earlier translucent shapes.
- In a real product, the navigation layer is visibly separated from the content: the music sidebar with a list of items, active item highlighted in pink and a collapsible section appears next to the album grid in one frame (sheet 0010, q0083) and alone, narrow, in the others (sheet 0010, q0082, q0085 and q0086).
- Responsive resizing is demonstrated in practice on the Mac: the Finder window with a fixed translucent toolbar and columns for name, date, size and type appears narrower and then wider, preserving the structure and the same item selected in blue (sheet 0011, q0093, q0094 and q0096).
- Floating buttons in the bottom corners change in content and shape: in the vases app they show profile and action over the product grid (sheet 0007, q0056 and q0061), and in isolated frames over a gray background the pair becomes magnifying glass and cart, the latter expanded into a group with a numeric counter, plus and bag inside the same capsule (sheet 0007, q0058 and q0059).
- The same element switches function without switching identity: the bar with three icons over the photo becomes a larger play button with a label (sheet 0013, q0109 to q0111), and the action bar follows the tilt of the background frame photographed from the side (sheet 0013, q0116 and q0117).
- The video intercuts physical metaphors between the screens: a drop falling onto an oval surface with an engraved symbol, a lens held between fingers against the light, water being poured into a glass (sheet 0003, q0019 to q0023).
- The visual closing is a multiplatform composition with MacBook, iPad, iPhone, Apple Watch and Apple TV displaying the same gradient wallpaper and the same clock, repeated in two nearly identical frames (sheet 0019, q0170 and q0171).
Visual proportion: most of the sheets alternate between presenter and interface, but sheets 0005, 0006, 0015, 0017 and 0018 are only material, components and screens, while sheet 0020 is exclusively presenter.
