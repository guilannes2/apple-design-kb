### What the images show
Basis: 15 of 15 frame sheets viewed, all codes checked.

- The video opens and closes with the same showcase of the three components side by side, each with a caption below (menu with Cut, Copy, Paste, Delete; date picker with the June 2020 calendar; color picker with tabs and palette), varying only which one is in focus or dimmed (sheet 0001, q0006; sheet 0013, where the color picker is in focus; sheet 0014, q0121).
- The comparison between the old action sheet and the new context menu is made with two iPhones paired on the same Reminders screen: in one the menu takes up the full width, with the Cancel button set apart by a line, in the other the menu is narrow and anchored near the top, without Cancel, with the delete item isolated in red with a trash icon (sheet 0002, q0016 to q0018; repeated on sheet 0003).
- The menu's anatomy is taught through a thin-line wireframe on a black background, with captions pointing to "Label" and "Icon" in a list of four items and a second example with a "Title" header above the items, also showing the hierarchy separator (sheet 0003, q0022).
- The selection state inside the menu appears as a checkmark next to the active item, and the focused item gets a color highlight covering the entire row, two distinct visual treatments seen in the same Music sort menu (sheet 0004, q0030 to q0033).
- The menu's opening is shown frame by frame as an opacity transition, first translucent over the screen, then fully opaque, and the cancellation is indicated by symbolic icons of an X and a green checkmark overlaid on two devices side by side, signage that does not belong to the app's interface (sheet 0004, q0029 to q0032).
- Disambiguation menus are born anchored near the button that triggered them: below the add button in Photos, above the keyboard's annotation bar at the camera icon, and next to the video editing controls with the two save options (sheet 0005, q0041 to q0045).
- The Files options menu is divided into two sections by a line, actions at the top and display criteria at the bottom, with a simultaneous checkmark on two independent axes, icon mode and sorting by name (sheet 0007, q0056 to q0057).
- The rule of not hiding the primary action has a direct visual translation in Messages: the compose icon stays isolated in the top right corner, outside the menu, while select, pin and edit name and photo are grouped inside the secondary menu (sheet 0007, q0059 to q0062).
- The confirmation of a destructive action appears as a two-step sequence in two apps, first the menu with the delete option, in red, in Reminders, and then, in its place, a bottom sheet with the warning in text and the destructive button in red above the neutral button, never beside it (Reminders, sheet 0008, q0068 to q0072; Mail, sheet 0009, q0076 to q0077).
- Parity with the Mac is shown literally: the same list of a website's pages appears in sequence on an iPhone, an iPad and a Mac window, keeping the same item structure (sheet 0009, q0079 to q0081).
- The contrast between the old picker and the new one is built in a single frame with three elements: a spinning wheel of month, day and year on the left, the Reminders grid calendar in the middle with day 22 highlighted, and another wheel on the right (sheet 0010, q0086 to q0088).
- Direct typing of the time appears as its own numeric keypad with large square keys below the "5:00 PM" field, in place of the hour wheel (sheet 0011, q0094), and the month and year picker becomes a scrollable two-column list with the center item larger and bold (sheet 0011, q0091).
- Compact mode is demonstrated by the difference between frames: a simple date text over a dark background, then a calendar card appearing semi-transparent and finally fully opaque with the day and time highlighted in blue (sheet 0011, q0097 to q0099).
- The birthday field on a contact editing screen shows the editing state through a blue outline box around the value, which disappears in the next frame leaving the date as plain text, with no intermediate step on screen; it is this pair of frames that the speech associates with confirming by tapping outside (sheet 0012, q0102 to q0103).
- The color panel has its structure named by captions pointing to "Currently selected color" and "Saved colors", with a large swatch of the current color in the bottom left corner separated from the row of saved circles that ends in an add button, and the effect of moving the cursor on the grid is visible between frames, from purple to orange (sheet 0013, q0112 to q0117).

Visual proportion: per the notes, most of the frames are real screens from Apple apps in a device mockup (Reminders, Photos, Music, Podcasts, Files, Messages, Mail, Safari, contact editing) alternating with the presenter and with summary text cards; the component showcase and the Photos screen already appear in the first sheet, and only sheet 0015, with the closing laptop, shows no interface at all.

Recorded divergences or limits: on sheet 0006 the notes point out that the Safari screen was captured without the navigation menu the speech describes, and that the Files example appears only in the clean version of iOS 14, without the corresponding frame from the earlier version loaded with actions; on sheet 0012 the notes record a month and year picker frame that is small and hard to read.
