### What the images show
Basis: 9 of 9 frame sheets viewed, all codes checked.

- The architecture diagram is assembled piece by piece, not shown ready-made (sheet 0001, q0005 to q0009): first the purple AVFoundation box with a film reel icon feeding it from below, then the arrow and the yellow RealityKit box, then the wide green AVKit box above, connected by a bottom bar to four smaller blocks labeled Platform, UIKit, AVFoundation and RealityKit. The order of appearance on screen is the same dependency order described in the speech.
- The agenda card has two layers of hierarchy and grows line by line (sheets 0002, 0004 and 0007): large title on the left, presenter video on the right, the current item in bold and the rest in normal weight, with indented sub-items marked by a dot appearing one per frame (sheet 0007, q0055 to q0057, where "Exit from multiview" and then "Prepare for transitions" appear below the item already visible).
- The whole demonstration takes place in the same 3D environment, with the furniture and the wall always the same (sheets 0002 and 0003): blue armchair, light-colored sofa, coffee table with a tablet, and the video screen fixed on the same wall. Only the number and arrangement of the screens change, which communicates that the change is in the window composition and not in the space.
- The playback bar is a floating translucent material (sheet 0002): glass effect, with player buttons, content title and channel name, positioned below the screen and not attached to it.
- There is an intermediate transition frame captured between states (sheet 0002, q0015 to q0016): the single screen appears unfolding into two semi-transparent rectangular screens side by side, a state that neither the speech nor a final frame would show.
- The progression from two to four screens is visible frame by frame (sheet 0003, q0019 to q0027): each screen gains its own X close button and volume icon, the arrangement goes from symmetric to asymmetric (one large screen with smaller ones), the layout reorganizes between q0025 and q0026 and in q0027 an extra screen appears overlaid in the top right corner.
- The content thumbnail panel responds to the choices (sheet 0003, q0019 to q0024): a row of three to four thumbnails inside a curved translucent panel, each item with the match name and broadcaster, and a selection circle with a check on the items already on screen.
- The anatomy of the components is drawn before the code (sheets 0004 and 0005): AVPlayerViewController boxes connected by an arrow to lilac screens, then AVExperienceController in green connected to the player and to a single lilac monitor with a play button at the base, then the allowedExperiences property plus multiView giving way to a Browser View Controller box with a stack-of-cards icon labeled "video item" below it, and finally AVMultiViewManager entering on the left (q0037 to q0040).
- The colors of the diagram boxes reappear as syntax colors in the Swift code (sheets 0005 and 0006): green for the experience controller and manager, purple for the player, dark blue for the browser, creating an association between concept and implementation that the speech does not verbalize.
- The code has its own editorial convention for highlighting and removal (sheet 0006): light blue background over the two lines in focus (q0048 to q0049, when obtaining the experienceController), strikethrough text over the line being replaced when the comment changes to "remove content" (q0050), and in the next frame the struck-through lines disappear, leaving only the final, shorter version (q0051).
- The right and wrong pairs use round green seals with a check and red ones with an X over small mockups (sheet 0008, q0069 to q0072): in "Use title and thumbnail", a card with a film icon and title field is approved and the variant with an extra heart icon below the title is disapproved; in "Stylize appropriately", the row of four thumbnails in uniform neutral tone is approved and the row with colored borders is disapproved; and in "Reflect the current selection" a row shows one thumbnail in a lighter tone than the others.
- The closing swaps the screen for a photograph of a physical object (sheet 0009, q0073 to q0078): light blue over-ear headphones on wood with a blurred cup in the background, black text on the left, the two closing lines appearing one per frame, and the presenter closing with a thumbs-up gesture and then smiling. The notes record this same closing composition pattern in another video from the same batch.

Visual proportion: of the nine sheets, the presenter appears alone in short stretches (opening of sheet 0001, sheet 0002 at q0009 to q0010, when the agenda disappears, the frame of sheet 0004 before the first diagram, and the last frames of sheet 0009); for the rest, the screen shows a technical diagram, the agenda card, Swift code or the demonstration in the 3D environment.
