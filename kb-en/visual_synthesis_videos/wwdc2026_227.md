### What the images show
Basis: 16 of 16 frame sheets opened and viewed, all codes checked.

- The prompt given to the agent is always shown in full on screen, inside a text bubble with a translucent background and a pink, blue and orange gradient border, the most recurring graphic element in the video (sheets 0004, 0005, 0006, 0008 and 0014). The speech reads the prompt aloud, but only the image exposes the literal structure of the text, with sentences separated by purpose: number of variations, requested features and the requirement of its own name and preview for each one (sheet 0005, q0040; sheet 0006, q0047).
- The pair of vague prompt and specific prompt is shown as a direct contrast. The short prompt "Create a UI for managing a book club that meets regularly" produces a screen with a weekly poll, a reading sequence card and a photo gallery, elements the designer did not ask for (sheet 0004, q0031); next to it grows a list of critiques with a severity code by icon, red X for "Arbitrary layout" and "Features chosen by AI, not by you", orange exclamation point for "Getting stuck" (sheet 0004, q0031 to q0033). The distinction in severity by icon color is not spoken aloud.
- The "Prompting tips" lists grow item by item at four separate moments in the video, always in black text on a light gray background, with the new item entering at the exact moment the speech says it (sheets 0004 and 0005, with the first item entering at q0036 and q0037; sheet 0008, q0068; sheets 0013 and 0014).
- In Xcode, the ten requested variations appear as a vertical stack of named #Preview blocks in the code panel (Cozy Club, Reading Race, Terminal Feed, Blueprint Atelier, Archive Cabinet, Editorial), and the preview switch on the right is shown in action: the preview goes from a light app "Book Club Name" to a dark, monospaced, green, terminal-style theme (sheet 0005, q0041 to q0042).
- The style variations arrive on screen as complete iPhone prototypes, not as wireframes: "Cozy" with a beige palette, serif typography and colored progress indicators, "Editorial" in black on white with condensed newspaper typography and thin rules separating items (sheet 0005, q0043 to q0045).
- The remix is shown as a grid of thumbnails of hybrid results, each card with its own style (green terminal on black "DISCUSSION_FEED.LOG", concentric rings "Progress Rings", "Radial Burst", "Dot Matrix", table "PACE_LEADERBOARD.OUT"), which then converges into a single consolidated screen bringing together cover, progress, race track and ranking (sheet 0006, q0051 to q0054).
- The transition from placeholder to real content is visible frame by frame: the book cover in solid purple color with no image (sheet 0007, q0055) gives way to real book covers, people's names, text comments with relative time, a "Show all" button and an "Add a comment" field in the footer (sheet 0008, q0067).
- The edge cases thought through by the designer appear named in Xcode's own preview list: Long Content, No Meeting Scheduled, Empty Club, Crowded Club (sheet 0009).
- The corrections born from observing the real content are marked by a thin horizontal line annotation connecting a label to a point on the screenshot: "Account management" pointing to the profile icon and to the empty state "No books logged yet" with a blue "Log a Book" button, and "Expand control" pointing to the control that expands the leaderboard and comments list (sheet 0009, q0075 and q0077 to q0078).
- The animation block is taught through a visual glossary of black linear icons on a light background that accumulate from left to right, one per concept: Ease, then Spring, then Friction and inertia, then Device motion, then Haptics (sheet 0010, q0088 to q0090; sheet 0011, q0092 to q0099; sheet 0012, q0103). The Ease icon combines an S-curve, a solid start point and an empty end circle; the Spring one is a forked arrow with wavy lines.
- Each abstract concept is immediately grounded in a real product photo, a physical iPhone in hand on a wooden table, never an isolated screenshot: the Music app for perceived weight, the Apple Cash card in Wallet for response to device motion, Find My for haptics (sheet 0011, q0094 and q0097; sheet 0012, q0100).
- The difference between two frames of Find My shows how proximity is communicated through color area: a radar with scattered dots and "11 ft" turns into a large solid green circle filling almost the entire screen with "3 ft, nearby" (sheet 0012, q0100 to q0101).
- The animation tuning panel is shown first in the bad state and then in the good state. In a narrow window it enters as a dark overlaid layer, occupying the bottom half of the screen and obstructing the interface; when resized, the same UI reorganizes side by side with the app preview, with the label "Resize control" pointing to the exact button that solves the problem (sheet 0014, q0124 to q0126; sheet 0015, q0127 to q0129).
- The open panel shows the control anatomy used: horizontal sliders labeled "Spring Damping" and "Spring Dampening" with a numeric value on the right (4.25, 0.32, 0.94, 0.46), toggles labeled "Custom" and a green "Double options" toggle turned on, and a blue text button "Reset options" in the top right corner; between two frames the slider values change position, recording the live adjustment (sheet 0015, q0130 to q0131).
- The real Swift code repeatedly appears framed inside the silhouette of a MacBook or iPad, never as a standalone code image, with readable animation parameters (damping, response, delay) and a state enum named "Hero Transition" and "Leaderboard Expand" on the left side of the editor (sheet 0012, q0105 and q0106; sheet 0014, q0124). The Xcode screenshots also carry an overlaid elapsed-time timer (00m 02s, 00m 05s, 00m 33s), indicating a live process recording (sheet 0002, q0014 to q0016).

Visual proportion: sheets 0002 to 0015 are screen-dense, alternating Xcode screenshots, iPhone prototypes, prompt bubbles, tip slides and product photos, with the presenter appearing in short cuts between blocks; only sheet 0016 is essentially the presenter on screen, with a single "Next steps" slide over a still-life photo of the wooden table.

Recorded divergences or limits: in sheet 0014 the tuning panel controls are described as apparently sliders, not yet readable in detail at that point, and only become readable in sheet 0015; in sheet 0002 the block highlighted in green in the code is recorded as probably a diff or preview, without confirmation.
