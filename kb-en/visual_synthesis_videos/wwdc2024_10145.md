Basis: 10 of 10 frame sheets viewed, all codes checked.

- A single demo app serves as the throughline across almost every sheet (sheet 0001, q0004 to q0007): the "Bracelets" app on iPad, with a three-column grid of rectangular cards with rounded corners, each containing a thumbnail of a beaded bracelet on a light gray background, a title in the top left corner and plus and "Edit" icons in the top right corner; on the editing screen the bracelet occupies the central diagonal and the bead tray sits in a rounded horizontal bar at the bottom, with one bead marked by a white star.
