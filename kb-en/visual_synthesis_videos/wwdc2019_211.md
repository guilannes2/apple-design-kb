### What the images show
Basis: 40 of 40 frame sheets viewed, all codes checked.
- Measurements annotated on top of diagrams, not in running text: a TV drawn in outline with a diagonal line and the value "1920 x 1080" in the center (sheet 0018, q0162), two safe area frames with the margin numbers written on each edge, 60 at the top and bottom and 90 on the sides for the standard tvOS interface, and 125, 365 and 90 for the Carousel (sheet 0019, q0165 to q0168), and two checkered grids comparing 1x and 2x resolution, the second visibly finer (sheet 0019, q0163, q0164). The growth of the inner rectangle between one diagram and the other shows at a glance that the Carousel's safe area is more generous at the bottom, a comparison that the numbers alone don't deliver.
- Anatomy of the Carousel's metadata panel in three horizontal blocks anchored in the bottom third, over the video still playing: solid white "Play" button with a triangle and gray "More Info" stacked on the left, multi-line synopsis in the center, and the Starring and Director label and value columns on the right (sheet 0009, q0078 to q0081). In the app demonstration the same arrangement reappears next to the image, with a test synopsis, the named attributes Starring and Directors and a compact line of genre, year and duration (sheet 0026, q0234). There is never a separate opaque panel, the background is the image itself partly darkened.
- Direct comparison between the two Carousel styles built in the same composition: first just title, Play and More Info over the character's silhouette, labeled "Actions Carousel" (sheet 0016, q0142 to q0144), then the same frame gains the synopsis and spec sheet on the right under the label "Details Carousel" (sheet 0017, q0149 to q0153). Hollow cyan outline boxes appear as didactic markup only around what is new, first on the synopsis block (sheet 0017, q0152, q0153) and then on the Starring and Director column (sheet 0018, q0154 to q0156).
- Apple TV Home drawn in three vertical layers: full-screen content occupying most of the space, a dock of five square icons with very rounded corners, and a thin row of colored bars right below, each bar in the color of the corresponding icon (sheet 0004, q0028 to q0034). The row of bars and the precise design of the icons are not described in the speech, only on screen.
- Screen hierarchy taught as an abstract block diagram, with no text or image inside the shapes: a large green rectangle for the content, a row of small rectangles below for the dock and the picking row, and white outlines entering as annotation, first marking a subarea and then wrapping the whole set (sheet 0008, q0069 to q0071). The diagram fixes the relative proportion between the areas, which the speech treats only as "priority" and "bottom third".
- Focus states recorded through the difference between two consecutive frames: the chevron on the right edge, a thin neutral arrow, gains a translucent blue background circle when it comes into focus (sheet 0010, q0088 to q0089; sheet 0011, q0091, q0092). In the same family of signals, the row of pagination dots at the bottom swaps one of the gray circles for a blue rectangular shape to mark the current position (sheet 0010, q0085, q0086).
- Transition between items captured as an intermediate state, not as a cut: two posters appear simultaneously on the same screen, one leaving on the left and the other entering on the right (sheet 0011, q0096, q0098; sheet 0012, q0100 to q0102), and in the transitions of sheet 0012 the synopsis text of the two items blends during the movement (q0100 to q0102). The same mechanism reappears in the FullScreenLayout as a wipe with a clear vertical band separating the two photos, with the proportion between them changing frame by frame until the new image dominates (sheet 0039, q0345 to q0349).
- Xcode demonstration recorded live and captured mid-work: the template selection dialog with the Top Shelf extension option highlighted (sheet 0021, q0182), the target creation modal with stacked fields and Cancel and Finish buttons (sheet 0022, q0193), code growing one or two lines per frame, blue autocomplete popups over the text being typed, yellow warnings in the margin while the switch is still incomplete and a red error after both cases are filled in (sheet 0022, q0195 to q0198; sheet 0024, q0212 to q0216). Compiler warnings and autocomplete popups captured mid-typing indicate a live-recorded demo, not a post-produced one.
- API class hierarchy presented in three stages over the same diagram, using color and opacity instead of new text: first only the provider's green box lit and the rest darkened, then the three orange boxes in the middle lighting up connected by lines, and finally the blue item boxes at the bottom (sheet 0021, q0183 to q0189), each stage with a green circular new-feature badge in the corner.
- Fixed template for the technical slides, repeated throughout the engineering half: black background, title aligned to the left at the top, green "new feature" badge on the right, bullets that accumulate frame by frame without erasing the previous ones, and the API snippet inside a rectangular dark blue background box below the corresponding bullet (sheets 0029 to 0033, 0038 to 0040). The exact spelling of the Swift signatures only exists in the image.
- Best practice lists built through progressive reveal, one criterion per frame: "Content discovery", then plus "Updated regularly", then plus the limit of five to ten items (sheet 0018, q0158 to q0161); and the video list growing from 1080p to HTTP Live Streaming, then duration of two to five minutes, then audio (sheets 0019 and 0020, q0170 to q0174).
- Two distinct visual themes coexisting in the talk: the Sectioned-style Top Shelf prototype appears in a light theme, with a dark gray section header and a row of cards of similar proportions over a light background (sheet 0014, q0120 to q0123; sheet 0020, q0177), while the real Home and the Carousel always appear dark. One of the cards expands until it occupies the full width of the row between two frames (sheet 0014, q0122 to q0123), and a double exposure effect is used to suggest the transition state (sheet 0014, q0120).
- Tab Bar behavior explained first in a wireframe of pure color blocks, an orange row at the top for the tabs and a large yellow block for the content, with the blocks changing vertical position between frames to simulate the bar scrolling along (sheet 0032, q0281, q0282). Then the mockup shows solid red rectangles occupying both ends of the bar, marking where the brand accessory areas go (sheet 0032, q0287; sheet 0033, q0289).
- Profile system screens with light translucent material over the content: Control Center panel anchored in the top right corner with clock and date, colored circular avatars with a selection ring on the active one, stacked rectangular buttons and two square icons at the bottom (sheet 0028, q0246 to q0249); and the dialog sequence, first the profile choice with stacked colored bars, then the confirmation in a pastel gradient with two buttons, then the management list with the name on the left and profile plus chevron on the right, first row highlighted in white (sheet 0028, q0251; sheet 0030, q0264 to q0269).
- Graphic metaphors give shape to the problem before any real interface appears: a dense grid of identical turquoise-blue rectangles, with no content inside, first head-on and then in perspective like a tilted mural (sheets 0002 and 0003, q0017 to q0021), colored circles scattered like confetti to represent overload (sheet 0007, q0057), a row of five rectangles of unequal widths to illustrate the grid break (sheet 0007, q0062), and a word cloud arranged hierarchically by font size recapping all the principles (sheet 0012, q0104).
Visual proportion: from sheet 0004 on, almost every sheet carries a real interface, prototype, diagram, slide or Xcode on screen, and the presenter-alone frames concentrate at the opening (sheets 0001 to 0003), reappear in part of the design section (sheets 0007, 0008, 0010 to 0012) and return at the speaker changes (sheets 0013, 0027, 0028, 0033); in most of the remaining sheets the presenter appears small next to the projection, not in its place.
Recorded divergences or limits: the notes point out that the sheets capture still frames, so the continuous movement of the transitions only appears as varying proportions between two overlaid images, never as the movement itself; they also note that several dissolves between app and code are a video editing effect and not a software function (sheet 0024, q0211; sheet 0026, q0228 to q0233), that some frames repeat with no visual change at all (sheet 0029, q0257 to q0260; sheet 0021, q0183 to q0184), and that the opening credits text was only partly read (sheet 0001, q0002).
