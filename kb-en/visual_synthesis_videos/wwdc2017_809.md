### What the images show
Basis: 8 of 8 frame sheets viewed, all codes checked.
- Repeated didactic pattern of "candidate terms plus official term": a vertical list of alternative names appears next to the actual capture of the component and, in most cases, closes on the UIKit class name. Maps app alert with the "Settings" and "OK" buttons resolved into UIAlertController (sheet 0002, q0010 to q0014); Mail's top bar with "Header / Top Tool Bar / Navigation Bar", here with no class name on screen (q0016); bottom bar with "Drawer / Tool Bar / Bottom Navigation Bar" closing on UIToolBar (q0017 to q0018); Accessibility control with "Switch / Toggle / On/Off Slider" closing on UISwitch (sheet 0003, q0020 to q0021). The talk only asks the audience the question; it's the screen that shows which term wins and the exact class name.
- Animation spec presented as a fixed pair of chart and code next to the screen where the transition happens: "CUSTOM SPRING ANIMATION VALUES" panel with Mass, Stiffness, Damping, Initial Velocity and Duration, and a cubic curve panel with the CAMediaTimingFunction call and its controlPoints, next to an iPad running the Notes app (sheet 0006, q0053 to q0054). The two frames carry different curves for the same transition, allowing two specifications to be compared.
- The same pair of panels reappears with the caption "Use Xcode" overlaid on the iPad screen in drawing mode (sheet 0007, q0055 to q0056), tying the spec to the environment where it becomes code.
- Actual Xcode screens document the flow that the talk only names: project template grid (sheet 0007, q0058), storyboard with a transition arrow between two blank screens (q0059), semi-transparent overlay of another capture on the storyboard (q0060) and the Attributes Inspector of a Table View Cell with fields like Style, Selection and Accessory (q0061). This turns what the talk calls object library and attributes into concrete field names and panel layout.
- Difference between frames showing the Notes app transition: the iPad goes from the "Yosemite Trip" note with a small drawing of three overlapping circles to full-screen drawing mode, with larger circles and the pencil, pen, ruler and palette toolbar at the bottom (sheet 0006, q0053 to q0054).
- Visual humor used as an argument with no explanatory caption: an actual Finder window in list mode grows from four versioned PSD files to nine, each name more desperate than the last, with the hashtag "#DesignerLife" below (sheet 0004, q0031 to q0032). This is how the lack of a single source of truth is demonstrated.
- Standard system component shown as an alternative to customization: the iOS Settings screen under General, a simple list with a colored icon on the left and a navigation arrow on the right, appears by dissolve from the wok photo, first translucent over the photo and then at full opacity (sheet 0005, q0040 to q0041).
- Photographic metaphors outside the app domain support abstract decisions before the screen returns to the system: a red disposable cup next to a white ceramic mug for common vocabulary (sheet 0003, q0023), a wok with vegetables and skewers on the barbecue for choosing between a ready-made standard and specialized equipment (sheet 0005, q0038 and q0039).
- Transition slides on a dark background with a short centered word or phrase mark each section and fix the exact label to remember: "Reality" and "Same Terminology" (sheet 0001, q0005 and q0008), "Worth the Effort?" (sheet 0005, q0043), "Show > Tell" and "Demonstrate" (sheet 0006, q0048 and q0051), "Focus" with the Xcode icon before it (sheet 0004, q0034 to q0035), "Communicate!" at the close (sheet 0008, q0068).
- The suggested practical activities are recorded in short list slides, one per block: postmortem, pictionary, show and react and report (sheet 0003, q0027); use cases, free resources and accessibility (sheet 0006, q0046); face to face, tweak/explore and check ins (sheet 0008, q0064); and the final summary with terminology, single source and focus (q0067).
Visual proportion: the talk alternates blocks of actual screen capture, iPhone, iPad, Finder, Settings and Xcode, with list slides and many frames of just the presenter on stage, and all of sheet 0001 goes by with no interface component.
Recorded divergences or limits: in sheet 0008 the last three positions of the grid stay empty because the video ends before the next cut.
