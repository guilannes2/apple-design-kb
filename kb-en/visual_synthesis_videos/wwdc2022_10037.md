Basis: 17 of 17 frame sheets viewed, all codes checked.

- The session's visual backbone is a list of four words that reappears at every topic change, with the current item in bold and the other three faded in gray, with no box or icon, only weight and color guiding the eye (sheet 0003, q0021 to q0022; sheet 0006, q0053; sheet 0009, q0078; sheet 0013, q0115). - Each section closes with a recap of the same design, bold title with smaller, indented subitems below (sheet 0006, q0052; sheet 0009, q0077; sheet 0013, q0114; sheet 0016, q0138 to q0139). Only on sheet 0016 do the notes record the progressive reveal by color, with the subitems appearing in light gray and then turning to full white (q0137 to q0139).
- Design critique is annotated directly on the screens with two graphic signs, a gray X next to the rejected version and a green check next to the approved one, with no text caption explaining the convention (sheet 0004, q0034 to q0036; sheet 0011, q0094 to q0099; sheet 0012, q0103 to q0106). On sheet 0013 the notes record only the X, on the rejected screen (q0111).
- Before-and-after pair of a system warning: the rejected version is a dense wireframe with a title, a triangular alert icon, two paragraphs and a large red emergency-call button; the approved one reduces everything to a thermometer icon, a short title, one sentence and a discreet capsule-shaped button, on a dark greenish background (sheet 0004, q0033 to q0036).
- Before-and-after pair of a subscription alert shown stacked on the same screen: the rejected alert remains visible with the X while the corrected version appears just below with the check, allowing the two titles and the two button pairs to be compared in the same frame (sheet 0011, q0096 to q0099). - Before-and-after pair of an error alert: the rejected one shows a raw numeric code and two side-by-side buttons that sound equivalent; the revised one names the problem, gives a direct instruction and stacks the buttons vertically, with the primary action highlighted above (sheet 0012, q0101 to q0106). The change in button arrangement, from side-by-side to stacked, appears only in the image.
- Demonstration that text carries the hierarchy: the same Apple Music library screen is shown first with only the colored icons, with no label at all, and then complete, with a name next to each icon and a title at the top (sheet 0002, q0013 to q0015).

- Empty states of two different apps share the same skeleton, simple gray central icon, bold title, smaller explanatory text and the app's tab bar still visible below, which isolates the text difference between them (sheet 0013, q0109 to q0112).
