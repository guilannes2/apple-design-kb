### What the images show
Basis: 20 of 20 frame sheets viewed, all codes checked.

- The App Clip icon appears on screen as the app's normal icon with a dashed circular border added around it, shown in a side-by-side comparison labeled "App" and "App clip" (sheet 0002, q0017 to q0018), and the same graphic device returns on the final App Store icon at the close (sheet 0019, q0170), signaling a system rule and not a brand choice.
- The App Clip Card's anatomy is taught through an annotated diagram, with a straight line and a white-box label pointing to each zone: "Title" on the GoodSongs Radio card, "Subtitle" on the Spin card (sheet 0010, q0087 to q0089), "Header" over the cover photo and then over a cup illustration on the 6am Coffee card, "Action" on the blue button (sheet 0011, q0092 to q0098) and "App" on the footer's attribution line (sheet 0012, q0103).
- Two header examples in the same diagram show that the cover image can be a real photograph (two people on a scooter, Spin card) or simple line art (steaming cup, 6am Coffee card), with a bold title and a smaller gray subtitle keeping the same typographic hierarchy in both cases (sheet 0011, q0094 to q0099).
- The card's action button changes label depending on the type of experience, "Open" on the scooter service card (sheet 0011, q0098) and "Play" on the radio card (sheet 0012, q0102), keeping the same filled blue pill shape.
- The App Clip Code is broken down on screen: first the whole symbol, dotted concentric rings with a central square, then split into two side-by-side instances labeled "NFC" and "Visual code", and finally printed on a white triangular totem on the table of a real coffee shop (sheet 0005, q0040 to q0045).
- The granularity of tagging by location and by table is shown through a color selection state: four store icons go from all white to only "Cupertino" white with the rest grayed out, and then a dark card appears with stacked buttons "Table 1", "Table 2", "Table 3" connected to the code by an arrow (sheet 0006, q0049 to q0051).
- The system's entry points appear as real, distinct screens: the App Clip card anchored to the bottom of the lock screen (sheet 0003, q0020 to q0021), the Siri suggestion widget on the Today screen (sheet 0007, q0057), the Smart App Banner fixed at the top of the Etsy page in Safari (sheet 0007, q0063) and a rich link card in a Messages conversation with an "OPEN" button (sheet 0009, q0076).
- App screens are presented through a semi-transparent iPhone mockup overlaid on a photograph of the physical context, a device repeated at a coffee shop, a parking lot, a clothing store, a restaurant and a mountain overlook (sheets 0014 to 0016, q0119 to q0139), in several of these cases starting from the plain photo and bringing the interface on top in the next frame (q0119 to q0120, q0124 to q0125, q0129 to q0130, q0138 to q0139).
- The Apple Pay checkout reappears with an almost identical composition in different contexts: a tax line, a tip selector with a percentage option highlighted in a dark button (20 percent on sheets 0014 and 0018), a bold total and a black Apple Pay button spanning the footer's width (sheet 0014, q0121; sheet 0015, q0134; sheet 0018, q0162).
- The prompt to convert to the full app is a stable visual component, a bold question above a horizontal card with icon, name, subtitle and a button on the right, coming right after the payment screen in the coffee shop and restaurant examples, with "GET" in both and "Sign up with Apple" on a black background in the clothing store (sheet 0014, q0122; sheet 0015, q0131 and q0135).
- What is left out of the App Clip is shown through exclusion cards with an "X" icon in a gray circle next to a label, each paired with a capture of the screen to be cut: "Introductions" over the brand story screen with pagination dots, "Login and accounts" over an email and password form, "Navigation or tabs" over the menu with a tab bar (sheet 0017, q0147 to q0151).
- The contrast between App Clip and the full app is made explicit in the image: the same 6am Coffee menu grid appears with a bottom navigation bar of four tabs only when it represents the whole app, absent from the App Clip screens shown before (sheet 0016, q0144; sheet 0017, q0146).
- The notification window is annotated graphically: over a standard lock screen notification a clock icon with a circular arrow appears along with the text "Eight hours default per use", then isolated full screen on a black background (sheet 0018, q0154 to q0157).
- Summary lists grow line by line in sync with the speech, four items at the close of the first part (sheet 0012, q0104 to q0107) and four at the close of the session over a blurred sky background (sheet 0019, q0167 to q0170), never exceeding four simultaneous sentences on screen.
- The video's authorial split is marked by setting: the first presenter is in a white studio with armchairs and an orange pouf (sheets 0001 to 0012), the second presenter appears in a glass setting with natural light and a view of trees, opened by a black title card with named credit and a video thumbnail in the corner (sheet 0013, q0109 to q0110).

Visual proportion: according to the notes, almost every sheet has a product screen, text card or diagram alternating with the presenter in a medium shot; only the opening (sheet 0001) and the close (sheet 0020) are practically without interface, and the greatest density of real UI is in sheets 0010 to 0018.

Recorded divergences or limits: on sheet 0002 the notes make a caveat about frame q0017, which would still show the "What Are App Clips?" card, with a reference to a supplementary note that is not in the file.
