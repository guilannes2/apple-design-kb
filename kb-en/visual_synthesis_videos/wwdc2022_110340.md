Basis: 20 of 20 frame sheets viewed, all codes checked.

- A fixed list of five words works as a navigation summary for the entire video, always in the same order, with the current item in bold white and the rest in faded gray, often on a split screen next to the presenter (sheet 0003, q0022 to q0023; sheet 0007; sheet 0010, q0085; sheet 0013; sheet 0016; sheet 0019, q0165 to q0167).
- Abstract concepts get their own iconography: a grid of three columns by two rows of squares with monochromatic linear icons and a small caption below, built in two stages, three icons first and three more afterward (sheet 0002, q0014 to q0017). The same icons return later anchored next to the charts as a marker for the topic in focus (sheets 0004, 0009, 0010).
- The test of mark types against irregular data is shown as a sequence of states of the same axis: dots in a regular wave, then scattered dots, then dots in an upward trend, then a line with moderate peaks and valleys and, finally, a line with deep, repeated drops (sheet 0004, q0032 to q0036). The final switch to bars is shown as a direct replacement in the same data range (sheet 0005, q0037 to q0038).
- The study charts appear in white on a black background, with no color, while the mark-type icons are blue, which visually separates the example under construction from the symbol of the concept (sheet 0004).
- Fixed range versus dynamic range is demonstrated by an overlapping transition, not by separate slides: the steps chart, with a dynamic axis, appears semitransparent over the battery chart, with a fixed axis of 0 to 100%, and then the sales chart appears overlapping the steps chart and gradually lightens, first two bars on the right and then all of them, until they turn white, already with 0 at the base and 60 at the top (sheet 0008, q0066 to q0072).
- Grid density tested live on the same chart: few lines, then six horizontal lines close together, then an intermediate state with fewer, more spaced-out lines (sheet 0009, q0079 to q0081). Then the grid appears at regular intervals and the horizontal axis gets date labels at weekly intervals, each aligned under its implicit vertical line (sheet 0010, q0083 to q0084).
- Evolution of the text above the chart in three visible states: a small, discreet label next to the axis, then a large bold title spanning the width of the screen, and finally the numeric total taking the highlighted spot above the chart, in place of the generic title text (sheet 0011, q0091 to q0097). The typographic hierarchy difference between the axis label and the context title is only clear from the image.
- Interaction demonstrated with a real hand holding a physical iPhone and touching the chart, with the finger approaching, pressing a bar and being withdrawn across successive frames, and a small dark badge appearing under the touch point (sheet 0006, q0046 to q0051; sheet 0012, q0101 to q0103).

- Input types presented as a grid of six dark rounded squares with a white symbol and caption, built in two stages, two icons first and four more afterward, overlapping semitransparently on the chart itself (sheet 0014, q0118 to q0119).
