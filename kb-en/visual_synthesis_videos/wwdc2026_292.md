### What the images show
Basis: 14 of 14 frame sheets opened and viewed, all codes checked.

- The search field's anatomy is shown isolated on a textured light gray background, in three successive states: empty with "Search" placeholder and magnifying glass on the left, focused with the magnifying glass no longer visible, and filled with the text "Design" gaining a circular clear button on the right with the magnifying glass reappearing (sheet 0002, q0016 to q0018). This same pill pattern with magnifying glass on the left, gray placeholder and clear button present only when there is text repeats in all the apps shown afterward.
- The structural assembly of an iOS screen appears in wireframe, three devices side by side evolving from left to right: blank screen, screen with bottom tab bar and search icon, complete screen with large title and inline search field below the title (sheet 0003, q0025 and q0026).
- The field's position determines where it animates to, and this is shown in action: in Mail, the difference between two frames shows the system keyboard rising and the search field settling right above it, with the email list remaining visible behind it (sheet 0004, q0035 to q0036). A "Bottom toolbar" label pointing to the lower area between two duplicated mockups marks the position under discussion (sheet 0004, q0034 and q0035).
- The reason a position gets discarded appears labeled in the screenshot itself: in Stocks, the bottom "Top Stories" section receives the "Sheet" label pointing to it, showing that the bottom of the screen is already occupied (sheet 0005, q0040 to q0041).
- The pair of standard and prominent search tabs is shown first in wireframe, with the magnifying glass icon isolated, enlarged and shifted outside the bar in the prominent version (sheet 0006, q0047 to q0048), and then in real apps: Apple TV opens a landing page with a uniform three-column grid of genre cards before any typing (sheet 0006, q0050), while in Phone activating search removes favorites and the tab bar and brings up the keyboard immediately (sheet 0006, q0052 to q0053).
- The local scope is reinforced by the placeholder text itself: on the "Albums" screen in Music, the inline field below the title reads "Search Albums" instead of a generic label (sheet 0007, q0055).
- For large screens, the layout variations are compared in grids of abstract desktop window wireframes, with sidebar, list column with the selected item in blue and detail column, at different proportions and field positions (sheet 0007, q0060 to q0062).
- Search in the toolbar's final position is demonstrated in a functional sequence in Mail for Mac: the field goes from compact to expanded and focused, the typed term evolves from "josh" to "joshua tree", and the list opens into two sections, "Top Results" and "All Results", with the detail panel on the right showing the selected email or "No Message Selected" (sheet 0008, q0064 to q0068).
- The toolbar's collapse behavior is shown by the difference between two wireframes: the item groups reorganize and move closer together as space decreases, with the "Search" field on the right (sheet 0009, q0073 to q0074).
- Search at the top of the sidebar appears in two real apps, Settings on Mac, separating the searchable category list from the detail panel, and Stocks on iPad, with the selected item highlighted in blue (sheet 0009, q0076 and q0078).
- Recent searches are shown in the three recommended forms, each in a different context: inline "Recently Searched" section revealed when focusing the field on iPhone, with square cover, title, "+" button and "Clear" link in the header (sheet 0010, q0086 to q0087); popover anchored to the toolbar field in Mail, listing terms with the descriptive prefix "From: Christine Huang" (sheet 0010, q0088); and a list in the Podcasts sidebar for iPad (sheet 0010, q0089).
- The predictive suggestions use a visual device that the speech doesn't detail: within the same line, the part already typed by the person appears in darker black and the part predicted by the system in lighter gray, in the example of "mel robb" completing to "the mel robbins podcast" and variants, in a compact three-line list above the actual results (sheet 0011, q0095 and q0096).
- The filtering controls appear in two distinct weights: Mail's scope bar as a pair of segmented pills right below the field, with the active one on a dark background (sheet 0012, q0103), and Maps' horizontal row of pills with a dropdown arrow, allowing several filters side by side above the results list (sheet 0012, q0107).
- Tokens are visually distinct from free text and the transformation is shown frame by frame: in Mail, the free text "Brian" turns into a "Brian Carey" chip inside the field itself, next to the "Sender contains: Brian" chip, with person suggestions bringing a circular avatar; in Photos, two colored tokens coexist in the same field, "Joshua Tree" and "2021" (sheet 0013, q0109 to q0110 and q0114). A generic avatar placeholder labeled "Person" precedes the token's application, showing the base component (sheet 0013, q0112).
- The searched term is highlighted in a yellow background inside the text results preview, a recurring feature that the narration does not describe verbally (sheet 0005, q0037, in one of the search results mockups shown fanned out; sheet 0012, q0103, in Mail).
- The empty state is shown in full: large central magnifying glass, title with the term in quotes including an intentional typo, "No Results for "Dsign"", instructive subtitle below, and a generic "Subtitle" label pointing from outside the frame to that line, identifying the component (sheet 0014, q0119).

Visual proportion: the video alternates in an almost regular way between mockups and captures of real apps with cuts to the presenter at the table, and on several sheets the presenter occupies three or four frames in a row between one example and another (sheets 0012 and 0014); the screens appear mostly as device mockups, screen captures and abstract wireframes.

Recorded divergences or limits: several sheets record apparently static frames between cuts, with no change of interface (the "WONDLA" banner on sheet 0005, the Stocks screen on iPad on sheet 0009, the recents list on sheet 0011, and the entire sheets 0012 and 0014, where each mockup appears isolated between lines of narration); on sheet 0002 the field's focus state is described as a focus appearance, with the magnifying glass no longer visible, with no confirmation of which state the frame represents.
