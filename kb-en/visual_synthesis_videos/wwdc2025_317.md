### What the images show
Basis: 30 of 30 frame sheets viewed, all codes checked.
- The session agenda is a typographic slide with no borders, icons or bullets: a single left-aligned column, the current item in bold black and all others in light gray, and the same layout is reused to mark progress throughout the entire video (sheet 0002, q0012 to q0016; sheet 0012, q0106 and q0107; sheet 0019, q0164; sheet 0024, q0214 and q0215; sheet 0027, q0240 and q0241).
- The API teaching alternates between two screen formats: the two-column split, with monospaced code on the left and the 3D result on the right (sheet 0003, q0022 to q0025), and the code screen taking up the whole page (sheet 0004, q0034 and q0035; sheet 0006, q0048 to q0051). Syntax highlighting is constant (comment in gray, keyword in purple, type in light blue), and in the full code screens a light blue background block marks the line in focus (sheets 0004 and 0006).
- Between identical code frames, the only thing that changes is the position of the blue highlight, which shows which line the speech is explaining at that moment: from q0048 to q0049 the highlight moves from the GestureComponent lines to the components.set lines, and from q0094 to q0096 it starts on one modifier and then comes to cover two (sheet 0006 and sheet 0011).
- Feature lists almost never appear ready-made: they generally grow line by line across consecutive frames, without removing earlier ones (sheet 0006, q0053 and q0054; sheet 0014, q0119 to q0122; sheet 0015, q0128 to q0131), although some cards appear complete all at once (sheet 0012, q0104 and q0105; sheet 0030, q0265 and q0266). Several lists close with a session citation footer, with the title on the left and "WWDC25" on the right, reaching up to three citation lines at once (sheet 0003, q0026; sheet 0009, q0076; sheet 0014, q0122; sheet 0015, q0131). In one frame the new sentence was captured halfway through the fade, still dimmed, before appearing opaque in the following frame (sheet 0022, q0193 to q0194).
- Abstract concepts turn into diagrams of simple geometric icons with a label underneath: the three spatial containers are a flattened rectangle, a translucent cube and a glowing silhouette, with the current item in bold (sheet 0003, q0027), and spatial audio turns into two identical blue screens emitting colored waves, with the wave color of one of them changing from green to orange, which the notes read as marking a distinct sound source, without asserting it (sheet 0007, q0059 to q0062).
- The anatomy of a presentation card over a volume appears in full: a glass-like translucent surface, rounded corners, bold title, smaller body text, an action button with a highlighted background and a small X in the top corner to close, floating over the Grand Canyon-like 3D terrain (sheet 0004, q0028 and q0029).
- Widgets are first shown as pieces hung on real surfaces, with different visual treatment in each case (album poster, square clock, rounded blue calendar, weather panel, scoreboard panel), and only afterward comes the formal slide with the properties and their values in light blue code blocks, each property in bold with the options aligned below (sheet 0008, q0064; sheet 0010, q0090; sheet 0011, q0098; sheet 0012, q0100 to q0105).
- The rendering quality slide is the segment with the most explicit annotation and measurement: a checkered grid with a central blue blotch controlled by a vertical slider marked 0.0, 0.4 and 1.0, plus magnifying glass circles connected by thin yellow or orange lines to points in the code, labeled as low and high quality; the blue blotch grows as the slider rises from 0.0 to 0.4 and then to 0.8 (sheet 0016, q0139 to q0144; sheet 0017, q0145 and q0146).
- The media format infographic encodes immersion through size and color: growing human silhouettes over a gradient bar that goes from gray to gold, with a two-line caption bringing the technical specification of each format, and the list grows from four to six and then seven categories, with the new ones inserted in the middle of the sequence (sheet 0021, q0184 to q0187).
- There is a literal before-and-after pair in the media conversion: a photo with barrel distortion connected by a thin green arrow to the same photo with the lines rectified, and then the same source connected to a dark 3D render with a textured sphere and a white figure serving as a scale reference (sheet 0022, q0196 to q0198).
- When the API has two ways to adopt it, the screen shows the two snippets stacked in a single panel, separated by comments that name each framework, with the keyword in purple and the enumerated value in green (sheet 0027, SwiftUI and UIKit panel; the notes describe the panel without recording the frame codes).
- Accessories first appear as an isolated product photograph on a neutral white background, with no setting, and only afterward in real use, with a translucent scoreboard with a dark header and numeric columns floating above the player and the physical controller anchored in the bottom corner of the first-person view (sheet 0013, q0111 to q0116; sheet 0014, q0118).
- Occlusion and progressive immersion are demonstrated through the difference between frames, before and beyond the text slide that names them: colored shapes appear partially hidden behind real furniture and plants, and in two consecutive frames of the same game scene the proportion of transparency changes, with the real room becoming sharper around it in the second (sheet 0008, q0066 and q0067; sheet 0014, q0125 and q0126; sheet 0015, q0127, with real ceiling and wall visible at the edges).
- The spatial web is shown as a sequence of states: the full browser window turns into a translucent gray rectangle with just the minimal player, then the scene turns into a reconstructed dark environment with suggestion cards on the right; and on an article page the text and photo disappear leaving only the highlighted 3D model of the concrete block, which corresponds to dragging it out of the page (sheet 0025, q0217 to q0219; sheet 0026, q0229 and q0230).
- Real production tools appear on screen without stylization: the dark video editing interface with a circular fisheye preview, timeline tracks, green audio waveforms, color wheel and vectorscope, switching tabs between cut, color correction and render settings across three consecutive frames (sheet 0024, q0208 to q0210).
Visual proportion: according to the notes, most of the sheets are text slides, code blocks or demonstrations in a real environment, with the presenter alone in frame in specific stretches (sheet 0001, and five consecutive frames of just the presenter in sheet 0023, q0200 to q0204).
Recorded divergences or limits: the notes mark points of uncertain reading, such as white text over the crystalline shapes described as illegible from a distance (sheet 0015, q0134), the identification of the three framework icons made by assumption from their shape (sheet 0005, q0041 and q0042), and a small corner frame described as a selection mark or annotation, without certainty (sheet 0001, q0006).
