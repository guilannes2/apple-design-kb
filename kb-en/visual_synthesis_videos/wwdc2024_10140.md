### What the images show
Basis: 15 of 15 frame sheets viewed, all codes checked.

- The "tone spectrum" exists as an interface component on the screen, not only as a spoken idea: four horizontal bars labeled (Clarity, Simplicity, Friendliness, Helpfulness), each with a dotted track and a blue rectangular marker whose position indicates the intensity, stacked inside a card with rounded corners and a light shadow (sheet 0012, q0102 to q0104). The same component reappears in two other scenarios, "Connectivity needed" (sheet 0013, q0109 to q0111) and "New feature" (sheet 0013, q0116 and q0117; sheet 0014, q0118), which allows comparing the marker positions between the three cases. Each panel enters in two steps: in sheet 0013 the controls appear first without a defined colored marker and then with the blue markers positioned (q0109 to q0110, q0116 to q0117); in sheet 0012 the first frame brings discrete markers, still without a highlighted position, and only the next frame moves them to distinct points on the track (q0102 to q0103). The adjustment appears in the difference between frames, not in the still bar.
- The example sentences are edited live with a fixed color code: black for the already established text, blue for the newly inserted piece, plus a blinking cursor simulating typing (sheets 0009, 0010 and 0011). The speech narrates only the result of each sentence; the screen shows exactly which piece changed.
- Before and after pair in the rewrite of the investment app: q0075 shows the generic sentence, q0076 erases the verb and leaves the cursor in its place, q0077 and q0078 insert the new verb in blue, q0079 swaps the ending, q0080 fixes the result and q0081 returns to the initial sentence, placing the two ends side by side in time (sheet 0009). The same piece-by-piece editing mechanic repeats for the children's app, with a vertical dividing line at q0086 suggesting the comparison between the old sentence and the new one (sheet 0010, q0085 to q0090), and then in a third energy adjustment (sheet 0011, q0096 and q0097).
- The post-it exercise has visual stages that the speech sums up in one sentence: title with no notes at all, two notes, about sixteen scattered, regrouped into loose clusters, and finally organized into columns with two blue header post-its ("Smart" and "Motivating") standing out from the yellow ones (sheet 0006, q0049 to q0054). Next, the columns turn into compact stacks with a slight offset between notes, suggesting paper thickness (sheet 0007, q0055 and q0056).
- The comparison between the voices of the two fictional apps is resolved as layout, not as text: two lines labeled on the left ("Investment app", "Savings app for kids") align the resulting stacks at the same height, allowing a direct reading (sheet 0007, q0062 and q0063).
- Agenda and progress slides use the same graphic convention: a short vertical list aligned to the left, the active item in bold black, the rest in light gray (sheet 0001, q0004; sheet 0005, q0039 to q0041; sheet 0008, q0065 and q0066; sheet 0011, q0098). It is the "where we are" indicator, with no progress bar. The "Tone exercise steps" frame from sheet 0014 (q0124 and q0125) does not follow this convention: the notes describe it as four lines of plain text, with no active item highlighted.
- Anatomy of the iOS notification card, repeated in every example: dark translucent material with a blurred colored glow behind it, a colored circular icon on the left, bold title, smaller secondary text and the timestamp in the top right corner, well rounded corners (sheets 0003, 0004, 0005). In the stack, the card behind is offset diagonally and with reduced opacity (sheet 0004, q0032 to q0036), and the order even reverses at q0036.
- Anatomy of the Stocks welcome screen: black background, two-line title with the second line in blue, three feature blocks each with an icon on the left and two lines of text (bold title and smaller description), small legal paragraph in the footer and an almost full-width blue button (sheet 0008, q0067 to q0070). The progressive highlight lights up the title word along with the "Market Data" block while the rest loses opacity (q0068) and, in the last frame, keeps only those two elements sharp while "Business News", "On All Your Devices", the footer and the button fade (q0070), following the screen's reading order.
- Anatomy of the system alert: dark card with well rounded corners, Wi-Fi icon to the left of the title, centered secondary text and two buttons separated by a horizontal divider, one neutral and one blue (sheet 0013, q0112 to q0114). The blue button appears faded first and then solid while the background lightens, recording the alert's entrance as an animation.
- The Apple Watch pairing screen shows the complete recipe of a setup screen: black background, large white greeting at the top, smaller paragraph, colored text link, central image of two watches, almost full-width button in the footer and a tab bar at the base (sheet 0003, q0019 to q0022). From q0019 to q0020 and q0021 the whole screen loses contrast and returns to normal opacity at q0022; the notes record the fading without assigning it a function.
- The four words that define the voice appear with no hierarchy among them: large typography, centered alignment, the same weight and size across the four lines, regular vertical spacing, black on white (sheet 0002, q0016).
- Where the notes record the device frame, it frames the capture: an iPhone frame around the Falstaff app screen (sheet 0001, q0009) and, on the Apple Watch, the Digital Crown and band visible around the watch face with the notification (sheet 0012, q0105 to q0107).
- The Work Focus screen shows the structure of a feature introduction: a padlock icon at the top, colored illustration occupying the top third, bold title, smaller explanatory paragraph and an almost full-width teal button in the footer, with a gesture indicator at the base (sheet 0014, q0119 and q0120).
- The final list of best practices is not running text: they are blue rectangles with rounded corners and centered white text, stacked and appearing one by one, and the fourth one enters in a lighter blue than the others (sheet 0015, q0128 to q0130). The closing shifts register to a still-life photograph of a mug and an iPhone on a light table, with the title in black in the top left corner (q0131).
- Completely blank frames separate the presenter's speech from the next example screen, always in pairs around a presenter frame (sheet 0001, q0006 and q0008; sheet 0002, q0013 and q0015; sheet 0003, q0024 and q0026). It is an editing device that only appears in the image sequence.

Visual proportion: according to the notes, almost every sheet alternates presenter frames with exercise slides and real system screen captures; sheets 0004, 0009 and 0010 are almost entirely screen (sheet 0004 has nine frames with no presenter at all), and there is no live tool demonstration, only exercise frames put together as a slide.

Recorded divergences or limits: the notes point out two reading issues with no explanation in the speech. At q0053 (sheet 0006) the frame shows fewer post-its than the previous frame, with four notes missing from the composition. At q0062 and q0063 (sheet 0007) the two frames repeat the same comparative composition with almost no perceptible difference between them.
