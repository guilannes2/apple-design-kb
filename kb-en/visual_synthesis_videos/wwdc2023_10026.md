### What the images show
Basis: 9 of 9 frame sheets viewed, codes checked.
- The layout system appears drawn on top of the interface itself: grid lines overlaid on the Activity rings watch face make visible the mesh derived from the screen's shape that the speech only describes in words (sheet 0004, q0028).
- The three fundamental layouts are presented as a side-by-side comparison, with a text label under each watch, fixing the Dial, Infographic and List nomenclature alongside the corresponding app example, Activity rings, World Clock and email inbox (sheet 0004, q0029).
- Background color used as state, not as decoration: the same trio of watches moves from semitransparent pastel backgrounds in pink, green and aqua to saturated, opaque backgrounds, marking focus and non-focus, with the background color following the Activity ring in focus in each one (sheet 0004, q0032 to q0033).
- Color annotation directly on the slide, with a label and a guide line: the text "System Green" points to the primary button of an alert dialog in dark mode, naming the exact color where the speech only talks about the system's semantic colors (sheet 0005, q0039).
- The concept of object permanence is demonstrated as the transformation of the same element: the Stopwatch moves from an analog dial with a hand to a digital numeric readout while keeping the same buttons in the same positions, with the "Object permanence" annotation naming the pattern right on the screen (sheet 0006, q0049 to q0050).
- The grid of exactly nine circular contact avatars appears as a fixed Messages page, repeated in two watch case finishes, which matches the limit of nine pinned conversations stated in the speech (sheet 0006, q0053 and q0054).
- The location switch in World Clock becomes a before-and-after pair: "New York" with 1:09 becomes "Cupertino" with 10:09, preserving layout and background map, changing only the time and the indicator's position, an effect that the speech attributes to turning the Digital Crown (sheet 0007, q0059 to q0062).
- Text slides are built by accumulation, from the title or the first item to the complete list, always in the same template of bold title, simple list and light gray background in a split composition with the presenter (sheet 0003, q0023 to q0024; sheet 0007, q0056 to q0057; sheet 0008, q0069 to q0070).
- A slide literally lists the vertical pagination recommendations the speech states, including using scrollable pages sparingly and placing them only after fixed-height pages (sheet 0007, q0057).
- Contact card with a large photo and message and phone icons at the base, next to the "Paginate and scroll" label (sheet 0007, q0055).
- Dark mode predominates in the demonstrated app screens, Messages, the alert dialog, the Mail "All Inboxes" list and the Settings list with icons colored by category, while full watch faces and World Clock appear on a light or colored background, a distinction the speech does not comment on (sheets 0005 and 0008, q0038, q0039, q0044, q0065).
- Color associated with health data: a hybrid stopwatch and heart rate watch face with a radial red glow around the dial, displaying the reading "Current 72" alongside the elapsed time (sheet 0008, q0068).
- Hardware enters as a visual argument: a macro shot of the metal Digital Crown next to a grid of iPhone app icons, with a translucent dissolve of a weather screen over the crown into the final composition of an iPhone and two watches side by side (sheet 0002, q0013 to q0015).
- Product photography frames the video, loose watch pieces and bands in a white studio still at the opening and a whole watch with a leather band on a wooden table at the close, the latter receiving the list of four related sessions with the "WWDC23" label aligned to the right (sheet 0001, q0008 and q0009; sheet 0008, q0071 to q0072).
