### What the images show
Basis: 15 of 15 frame sheets viewed, all codes checked.

- The access point appears anchored in the top left corner of the game screen as a small circular avatar, which can gain a translucent blue pill next to it with an icon and numeric data in two lines, and the frames show the alternation between the two forms, avatar alone and avatar with a progress badge (sheet 0002, q0011 to q0013).
- The color variation of the badge is demonstrated by two iPhones side by side with the same composition and the same typography in two different palettes, pink and green, changing only the color (sheet 0002, q0018).
- The access point measurements are drawn over the game's own interface, not in a separate caption: horizontal color bands cross the screen, pink on top and green at the bottom, and the numbers appear overlaid, first 62, 114 and 335, then 280 and 91, with the image zooming in to add these last two (sheet 0003, q0019 to q0020).
- In the sequence of screens the access point does not reappear during gameplay: after the clean title screen and the map and settings screens, the game screen shows only the score in large typography in one corner and the gear icon in the other, without the avatar (sheet 0003, q0023 to q0027).
- Three icon variants appear side by side in the same frame, solid gray circle, colored gradient version and white version, these last two with the same overlapping bubble shape (sheet 0004, q0028).
- The right and wrong naming is taught through a format repeated across four sheets: a vertical list with a green checkmark badge on the approved term and a gray X on the rejected ones, applied to "Game Center" against "Game Info" and "Game Zone" (sheet 0004, q0031), to the profile (sheet 0006, q0054, with the same slide repeated on sheet 0007, q0055) and to leaderboards against "Rankings" and "Scores" (sheet 0013, q0109).
- The dashboard's entrance is shown frame by frame as a layer over the game: the three cards appear semi-transparent over the title screen and in the next frame the dashboard takes up the whole screen with a gradient background and a close button in the corner (sheet 0005, q0037 to q0039).
- The same three-card structure is repeated on phone, tablet, laptop and television within the same sheet, and in the TV version the first card gains a focus highlight, a remote control convention (sheet 0005, q0041 to q0042).
- The file specifications always appear in the same visual format, three lines of text over a blurred video background with the 2x dimension in a heavier weight than the rest: TIFF or PNG at 1846 by 300 (sheet 0006, q0048), JPEG, TIFF or PNG at 512 by 512 at 1x and 1024 by 1024 at 2x (sheet 0009, q0075) and PNG at 659 by 371 at 1x and 1318 by 742 at 2x (sheet 0011, q0098).
- The three achievement types are placed side by side in a single frame and the difference lies entirely inside the card's central circle, solid padlock, percentage ring with "20%" and question mark, while the title and description follow the same template (sheet 0008, q0067).
- The 512 pixel markings in height and width are drawn around the circle over the card's own artwork, and the next frame zooms in on the crop while keeping the same markings (sheet 0008, q0070 to q0072).
- The anatomy of the cards is diagrammed with callout lines labeled "Title" and "Description" pointing to each part, and the same card reappears with the text switched to German while keeping the identical layout, visual proof that the structure survives localization (sheet 0009, q0076 to q0079).
- The in-game achievement notification is demonstrated by the difference between two frames of the same scene: the plain game screen and, in the next one, a compact banner in the top right corner with a circular icon and two lines of text, without interrupting the scene (sheet 0009, q0080 to q0081).
- The leaderboard embedded in the game itself appears as a compact side panel with a map thumbnail and a numbered list, with the player's own row highlighted at the end of the list even while outside the top of the ranking (sheet 0012, q0105 to q0107), while the full screen version uses Friends, Recent and Global tabs with a period selector (sheet 0012, q0102).
- The multiplayer lobby is shown in two successive states, first with the player themself in one slot, the other three slots with an add button and "Start Game" at the bottom, then with two more slots filled by avatars and the button switching its label to invite and start (sheet 0013, q0116; sheet 0014, q0118).
- Social information enters the App Store as its own component, a horizontal row of app icons under the friends playing label in the listing and, on the product page, a compact badge of friends' avatars with a numeric count near the game's icon (sheet 0014, q0123 to q0124).
- The closing is a mind map style diagram that grows from nearly invisible icons and resolves by connecting dashboard, profile, leaderboards, achievement cards, friends, multiplayer and App Store to a colored central icon, an information architecture that no single screen had shown before (sheet 0015, q0127 to q0128).

Visual proportion: per the notes, the video alternates screens from the demo game and specification slides with clips of the two presenters in close-up, and there are several sheets with b-roll frames with no interface at all (sheets 0004, 0007, 0010 and 0013 record frames only of the presenter or of outside shots).

Recorded divergences or limits: on sheet 0003 the notes carry two different readings of the same number noted on the screen, 91 in the frame description and 291 in the list of overlaid numbers; on sheet 0011 the notes record as an uncertain interpretation that the frames with solid green fill and pink border (q0096 and q0097) serve as an area diagram or crop frame, since the final illustration does not appear in them.
