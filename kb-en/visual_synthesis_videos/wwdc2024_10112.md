### What the images show
Basis: 14 of 14 frame sheets opened, all codes checked.

- The system is presented first as a schematic and only afterward as a product: a thin, unfilled blue outline wireframe shows two circular shapes inside a rounded bar, the cluster, next to a rectangle with a grid of app icons, and in a following frame the rounded bar becomes wider and the rectangle beside it gains a gradient background (sheet 0002, q0011 to q0013).
- Next come photorealistic renderings of steering wheels with panels that differ from each other, without repeating appearance or content: digital speedometer with a content band in a violet gradient, speedometer in a partial arc with a statistics block in dark theme, analog dial with a side air conditioning screen in a grid, appointments panel in a wine tone and a dial that shows only a large number (sheets 0002 and 0003, q0014 to q0021).
- The variable font is demonstrated with the same isolated glyph: the number "35" centered on a plain white background appears in a thin weight, then in a much heavier weight, then back to thin, with no other element on the screen (sheet 0003, q0025 to q0027).
- The gauge study varies one attribute at a time, always with the component alone on a neutral background and the same data at the center, "60" with "mph" below and a range of 450 miles in the footer: the ring starts thin and thickens in black, with an extreme zoom frame along the way in which only a black band and a light band are visible, it continues thickening until it covers most of the circle, and then changes from black to a petrol blue gradient (sheet 0004, q0029 to q0036).
- The analog alternative is shown through transformation of the same component: a dark capsule shape serves as a transition frame, then the thin red needle appears coming out of the center with ticks around it keeping "60" at the center, then the set moves to dark theme with the scale numbered from 0 to 120 in red around the ring, and finally the needle disappears and only the digital number remains (sheet 0005, q0038 to q0045).
- The breadth of the system is communicated through a visual catalog: a mosaic with about twenty thumbnails of gauges and widgets in a grid, each with its own palette and function, circular dials, gear indicator, range bar, compass, thermometer, equalizer and analog speedometer (sheet 0006, q0048). The dynamic content section repeats the feature with ten dark cards of the same size in two rows of five, labeled by category, among them tire pressure with the car seen from above, door alert, ADAS, calendar with color blocks, weather, trip, charging with a green bar, map, clock and current playback (sheet 0012, q0100, q0101).
- Each function gets a pair of contrasting appearances in the same frame, light card with linear gauge on the left and dark circular gauge on the right, and the title changes from pair to pair: "Cruise control", "Speed limiter", "Other states", "Combustion" and "Electric" (sheet 0007, q0055 to q0063).
- The cruise control behavior is legible in the difference between frames, not only in the label: the speed drops from 60 to 31 and then to 30 while the linear bar gains an orange section and the green arc at the top of the circular gauge grows (sheet 0007, q0055 to q0057).
- The warning states appear as a recoloring of the same geometry: a square badge with a fixed speed limit in the upper corner of the gauge, and the fuel bar and text turn red in the critical state, with the layout then changing to the driving mode dial with a comfort-to-sport scale and a temperature gauge (sheet 0007, q0059 to q0063).
- The hybrid gauge divides the ring into two colors, blue and orange red, for the two propulsion systems, and between two frames the proportion between them changes, indicating another energy state (sheet 0008, q0064, q0065).
- The modularity of the layout is shown through successive recomposition of the same panel: three modules with direction, speedometer and driving mode, then five smaller modules with compass, two small dials, a larger central speedometer and tachometer, then back to three, now with an album cover in place of driving mode, each module bounded by a thin line inside a rounded-corner container (sheet 0009, q0076 to q0078).
- The wallpaper appears as an explicit layer: in one frame the modules disappear and only the hill landscape remains, filling the entire panel, in the next frame the three modules come back overlaid on the image, now as a thin horizontal band and semitransparent (sheet 0009, q0079 to q0081).
- Piece-by-piece editing is signaled by individual blue selection frames around each mini component inside the composite cluster, and an empty curved silhouette in a turquoise line on an almost black background shows only the physical shape of a display, with no content inside (sheet 0010, q0083, q0085).
- The dynamic content area is demonstrated by alternating between empty and filled: a plain teal card disappears and the equivalent card in the group starts showing numeric trip data, and in the rendered steering wheel panel a translucent rectangular wiper fluid notification covers part of the central statistics block, which reappears whole in the next frame (sheet 0011, q0092 to q0099).
- The structure of the presentation is the agenda itself growing: the list of sections starts at one item and reaches five, with the current one in black and bold and the rest in light gray, and the section cards receive their sub-items one by one, "Gauge styles, Typography, Geometries" under customization, five functions under gauges, "Modular" and "Flexible" under layout (sheets 0001, 0006, 0008, 0010, 0012).

Visual proportion: the fourteen sheets are divided among isolated components on a neutral background, catalog mosaics, composite clusters on screen and photorealistic steering wheel renderings, with the presenter appearing at section openings and at the close; the notes do not record any code or API slide on any sheet.

Recorded divergences or limits: the notes flag repeated frames with no perceptible change, such as the pair of dynamic content mosaics and the steering wheel panel that repeats almost identically, and passages where the reading is stated as an assumption, for example the darkened frame described as a probable scene cut; some frames do not appear described in the sheet's sequence, among them q0030 on sheet 0004 and q0084 on sheet 0010.
