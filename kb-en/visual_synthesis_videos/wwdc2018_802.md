### What the images show
Basis: 28 of 28 frame sheets viewed, all codes checked.
- The dominant visual device is the card with an isolated word or phrase, large centered typography in white on a black background, with no frame, icon or supporting text; each card retains only the final label of the reasoning while the speech develops the argument (sheet 0001, q0007 to q0009; sheet 0013, q0111 to q0113; sheet 0028, q0244 to q0246).
- Each case study opens with the same arrangement: the app icon at large size with the name in typography beside it, on a black background, a pattern the notes record as repeated for each example (sheet 0003, q0020 to q0022, iTranslate Converse, with the icon on the left; sheet 0006, Vanido; sheet 0008, q0071, Streaks Workout; sheet 0025, q0217, Tinycards; sheet 0026, q0226, Gorogoa).
- A numbered list of the elements functions as a progress index that reappears throughout the talk, sometimes as a miniature in the background of the stage, sometimes as a full slide, with the current item in full white and the previous ones grayed out, accumulating from two to three, four and five items (sheet 0008, q0070; sheet 0012, q0106; sheet 0017, q0145 and q0146; sheet 0019, q0166; sheet 0024, q0215; sheet 0027, q0241).
- The removal of steps from a flow appears as a graphic mark over the text itself: the list of four workout steps is shown in full and in the following frame items 1 and 2 get a strikethrough, keeping the original numbering visible (sheet 0010, q0085 to q0086).
- Component anatomy in Streaks Workout: the selection screen uses a three-column grid of circular buttons with a white pictogram on a dark background, a label below each icon and a confirmation button in the top right corner (sheet 0009, q0079). The in-progress workout screen appears in two forms in the notes: one almost empty, with a large counter in the center, a small timer below and a row of exercise icons at the bottom (sheet 0009, q0081); and another with a circular progress ring with the repetition number inside, the exercise name in capitals below, three small icons in a row (previous, current and next with a question mark) and a linear bar at the top with the counter advancing from 2/20 to 3/20 between frames (sheet 0010, q0082 and q0083).
- Radical simplification appears literally in the composition: solid-color screens filling the entire frame (orange, olive green, yellow), large centered typography, a single circular button as the interactive element and small instruction text at the bottom (sheet 0004, q0028 to q0036).
- The screenshots appear inside the physical frame of an iPhone when the scene is one of use or demonstration (sheet 0003, q0020, map inside the device; sheet 0004, device frames visible on the translation app's screens; sheet 0011, q0097, photo of hands with the back of a card), while the workout app's screens are described without a frame, filling the frame (sheets 0009 and 0010).
- Progressive zoom simulated by stills: the same Vanido screenshot grows across seven consecutive frames, revealing in stages a floating label, the musical note labels in the side bar (F3, A2), a pause icon and a dotted bar at the top, which the notes only assume to be a progress indicator, a detail the speech does not describe (sheet 0007, q0055 to q0061).
- Schematic diagrams replace the real interface when the subject is the concept of interaction: two black-and-white human silhouettes with a phone icon, sound waves and a speech bubble (sheet 0004, q0031); and four white playing cards with a suit and exercise label, which gain a red repetition number and then a pink highlight on one card while the others darken, a technique for directing the eye to the item under discussion (sheet 0011, q0094 to q0096).
- Old artifacts enter as concrete images: a photo of an iPhone in hands showing the printed back of a playing card, which the notes read as the app's initial visual prototype (sheet 0011, q0097), and a small legacy software window, looking like a low-resolution screenshot, on a black background (sheet 0013, q0116 and q0117).
- A semitransparent overlay links a technical image or app screen to the physical world at three distinct moments: the app screen blended with a street photo, the suitcase's patent drawing over photos of an overhead bin and a cockpit, and the Apple Watch screen over a photo of a hand with rosary beads (sheet 0005, q0045; sheet 0015, q0130 to q0132; sheet 0027, q0240).
- Technical documentation enters as evidence: an official American patent form with the inventor's name gaining bold between two frames, and thin-line patent drawings with parts numbered from 14 to 33, shown in exploded and profile views (sheet 0014, q0121 to q0122; sheet 0015, q0127 to q0129).
- Market numbers become images: a slide with the isolated number and a small caption in gray, a dense grid of human pictograms with a single one highlighted in white, and a fraction slide with a caption about potential customers (sheet 0016, q0136 and q0138 to q0140). The video also uses dictionary-definition slides to fix two terms: one with the word in bold, phonetic transcription in italics, part of speech and numbered meanings aligned to the left (sheet 0016, q0141), and another in which the isolated word gains phonetic transcription and a definition in two numbered lines (sheet 0009, q0077).
- Personality intensity control shown as a state: the slider on the settings screen changes position and color between frames, from a blue indicator on the left to a red one on the right, with the list of options below unchanged (sheet 0020, q0172 to q0173). The weather app's layout repeats identically between cities, varying only the background color and the pixel-art scene at the bottom according to weather and time (sheet 0018, q0155 and q0158 to q0160).
- Comparison pairs support the argument about labels: the same house icon appears labeled as a shortcut on the iPhone home screen and in the Finder sidebar, and then the same Finder is labeled as a container (sheet 0022, q0190 to q0193); next, the generic tab bar of a meditation app and the specific tab bar of the WWDC app trade the foreground through a blurred overlay (sheet 0023, q0199 to q0201), and the criticized metaphor is staged on stage with the phone in front of a building facade and then a warehouse with a roll-up door (sheet 0024, q0212 and q0213).
- The physical metaphors appear with dimensional cues in the UI itself: a card with rounded corners, a subtle shadow and vertical proportion, a thin progress bar at the top, an answer field and the system keyboard appearing when typing (sheet 0025, q0219 to q0222); a fixed two-by-two grid of panels that swap content between frames, with the central dividing line highlighted in blue in one frame and not highlighted in the next (sheet 0026, q0227 to q0231); and on the Apple Watch an arc of circles imitating beads, one filled in yellow, whose highlighted position advances from one frame to the next (sheet 0027, q0236 to q0237).
Visual proportion: in most of the frames described there is a typographic slide, diagram or app screenshot, and the presenter alone appears in short stretches, mostly as a transition between blocks and at the close.
Recorded divergences or limits: some details were illegible in the frames, such as the text of the green button in the Vanido screenshot (sheet 0006), the caption overlaid on the cards only partially legible (sheet 0012) and background text cut off by the edge of the frame (sheet 0011); in the close-ups of hands with the iPhone the app screen does not appear clearly (sheet 0005, q0042 and q0043). The notes also contradict themselves about the translation app's screens: sheet 0004 describes a visible device frame, and the notes' own synthesis classifies those same screens as full screen without a frame.
