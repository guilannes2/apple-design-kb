### What the images show
Basis: 18 of 18 frame sheets viewed, all codes checked.

- The navigation hierarchy is translated into a recurring geometric diagram, rectangles connected by lines with the active path painted blue, which first appears as an overview of each tab as the root of a tree, then with the blue branch descending one level per frame to represent the depth of the push (sheet 0002, q0017; sheet 0011, q0092 to q0094).
- The diagram is paired with the real screen by label and guide line, the highlighted node changes from a city to a route and the screenshot next to it changes in the same proportion, tying abstraction and product together in the same frame (sheet 0012, q0100 to q0101).
- Good and bad practices receive an explicit graphic marker, a tab bar with two items gets a red X underneath and the reorganized version with three items gets a green check, a comparison the speech does not mention (sheet 0006, q0049 to q0051).
- The duplication of content between tabs is drawn out, four tab columns with their items underneath and, in the following frame, the same item names repeated under more than one column inside dashed boxes (sheet 0007, q0062 to q0063), while the corresponding initial screen appears with three simultaneous annotations pointing to actions scattered across the same screen (sheet 0007, q0055 to q0061).
- The fictional cycling app is rebuilt step by step with the same content screens reused, from the version with everything gathered in a single tab to the structure split into places, itineraries and profile, passing through route detail, city detail and city list (sheets 0003 to 0008, q0025 to q0071).
- The detail screens have a consistent anatomy, a cover image at the top, a title and short description, a two-by-two grid of metrics with a small label and large value, and scrollable blocks below, including an elevation profile chart with a marked point and a stop list with the time on the right (sheet 0004, q0035 to q0036; sheet 0005, q0037 to q0038).
- Almost every mockup carries a text label connected by a straight line to the element discussed, and this vocabulary names the interface piece by piece throughout the video, from the screen name to terms like push, back, navigation bar, preferred action, inactive action, dismiss, and selection and input (sheets 0003 to 0017).
- The transition appears frozen midway, a new screen covering almost all of the previous one coming from the right, and further on two screens overlapping diagonally between list and detail, which shows that the sheets record frames of animation in progress (sheet 0010, q0082 to q0083; sheet 0012, q0104).
- The disclosure indicator is shown in the list, each row with a square photo on the left, text in the center and an arrow on the right, along with the annotations that separate back from going deeper on the same screen (sheet 0012, q0103, q0105).
- The right-to-left language inversion is shown in fact, the same screen reappears entirely mirrored in Hebrew, with the back button, action icons, text alignment and the annotations themselves swapped to the other side (sheet 0012, q0105 to q0106).
- The three types of modal task get real screens from Apple apps, creating a reminder with the keyboard open, adding a transit card with a large value and its own numeric keypad, and full-screen content with a background video and a full-width primary button (sheet 0014, q0122 to q0125; sheet 0015, q0127 to q0128).
- The modal's navigation bar is broken down annotation by annotation over the same form screen, first the whole bar, then just the right button accompanied by a list of possible alternative labels, then that button's grayed-out state with an incomplete form and finally the cancel button (sheet 0015, q0130 to q0135).
- Dismissing the form appears as an action sheet anchored at the bottom, a confirmation question and two stacked buttons, the destructive one in red (sheet 0016, q0136 to q0137).
- The contrast around the close symbol is built with two cases on screen, an editorial article with no data entry with an X in the corner, and a filter screen with the same X but with items that receive a selection mark between one frame and the next (sheet 0016, q0138 to q0142).
- Nesting is demonstrated within the same form, a push annotation over a guest's row, a modal annotation covering the entire screen, then the photo picker in a grid with a numbered indicator and the return to the form already with the new thumbnail marked (sheet 0017, q0145 to q0152).
- The summary of the three sections reappears throughout the video with the current item in strong white and the rest dimmed, functioning as a progress indicator, and the recommendation lists are recapped with one item in bold at a time (sheets 0002, 0009, 0013 and 0018, q0010 to q0014, q0077 to q0078, q0113 to q0117, q0154).

Visual proportion: by the sheets, mockups of the fictional app, screenshots of real apps and text cards take up most of the frames, with the presenter appearing interspersed between these blocks and at the close.

Recorded divergences or limits: the notes record frames with no visible change between them, such as the repeated new itinerary form (sheet 0010, q0088 and q0089), and an ambiguous description on sheet 0003 about whether the two music tab bar frames are the same app with swapped labels or two different media apps being compared (q0019 to q0020).
