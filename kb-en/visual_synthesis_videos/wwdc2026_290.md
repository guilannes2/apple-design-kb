### What the images show
Basis: 11 of 11 frame sheets opened and viewed, all codes checked.

- The video opens by cataloging where naming happens, with generic components on a light gray background: a context menu with items "Label", "Action", "Disabled Action", "Destructive Action" in red and "Section Title" in a submenu; a tab bar with five abstract geometric icons labeled "Tab 1" through "Tab 5"; and a card with three colored circles labeled "Label" plus a "Title/Subtitle" block (sheet 0001, q0005 and q0006). The red reserved for destructive action and the hierarchy by font weight and size already appear before any real app enters the scene.
- Name tests are never done as a loose list, always inside the real component where the name will live. The Apple Cash card keeps an identical layout (small gray label, large bold value, black pill-shaped "Send or Request" button) and only the label text changes between frames: "Spending Power" with $49.36, then "Spending Power" with $0, then "Current Funds", then "Balance" (sheet 0002, q0018; sheet 0003, q0019 through q0024).
- The change in value to $0 while keeping the same label is a frame dedicated to testing the name's tone in the worst case, not just the name itself (sheet 0003, q0019 to q0020). The winner "Balance" then returns inside the full iPhone mockup, closing out the test (sheet 0003, q0025).
- The same method repeats on the subscription screen of a gym app: purple gradient background, two stacked buttons with plan name and price on two lines, and between two frames only the names change, from "Basic Access" and "All Access" to "Lightweight" and "Heavyweight", with the prices fixed in each position (sheet 0003, q0027; sheet 0004, q0028 to q0029).
- The name discovery exercise is visualized as a board of post-its with color coding by category, a feature the narration does not describe: blue for "Think" ("Easy", "Helpful", "Clever idea", "It remembers for me"), peach for "Feel" ("Fun surprise", "Secure") and green for "Do" ("Opt in", "Find it", "Share places"), with the columns filling in one at a time (sheet 0005, q0042 through q0045; sheet 0006, q0047).
- The grouping by theme is shown as a visual migration: the same idea post-its are replaced by post-its under theme labels that appear progressively, "Ease", "Excitement" and "Security" (sheet 0006, q0048 and q0049).
- The elimination of candidates has its own visual mechanism, also absent from the narration: nine names in solid orange blocks distributed by theme ("Rewind", "Breadcrumbs", "Visited Places", "Wanderings", "Discoveries", "Ventures", "Private Footprints", "Place Vault", "Private Memories") and, in the next frame, six of them crossed out by a diagonal line, leaving three (sheet 0006, q0050 to q0051).
- The chosen name is shown applied in the product right after: the bottom panel of Maps displays "Visited Places" as a highlighted label with a light green background, above the categories "By Category" and "By City" (sheet 0006, q0054; sheet 0007, q0055).
- In Photos, the difference between two frames shows the feature getting a name inside the "Collections" screen: a section labeled "Memories" with a navigation arrow appears above the "Pinned" section, grouping the same two large cards that were already there without a label (sheet 0007, q0062 to q0063).
- In the dark podcast player, the list of candidates is eliminated one by one next to the very speed control where the feature will live: "Vocal Isolation" enters as a bubble, turns into an orange block, then is crossed out along with "Isolate Vocals", then "Clarify Speech" enters and is crossed out, then "Enhance Playback", until "Enhance Dialogue" remains in solid orange (sheet 0008, q0069 through q0072; sheet 0009, q0073).
- The winner is shown applied inside the speed selector's own bubble, with a check icon (sheet 0009, q0073 to q0074), and the next scene reveals a second device behind the iPhone, an Apple TV with the audio menu of "For All Mankind" showing the same "Enhance Dialogue" next to "Original Sound"; the narration mentions this consistency across platforms, and it is the image that shows the second screen (sheet 0009, q0075 to q0076).
- The invented name is broken down on screen: the "Song Transitions" settings screen in Apple Music shows "AutoMix" with a check and a secondary gray description next to "Crossfade", and a following slide reduces the name to the formula "Auto + Mix = AutoMix" in large black text (sheet 0009, q0078 through q0080).
- The slides follow a single, consistent system, light gray background, black text, bold title at the top left, and a list growing one item per line as the narration advances: the criteria "Belongs", "+ Sets expectations", "+ Works everywhere" (sheet 0002, q0010 through q0012), the titles "Think. Feel. Do." and "Say it out loud" (sheets 0005 and 0006) and the takeaways "Naming is a design decision", "+ Name for the person and the product", "+ Good names set the pattern" (sheet 0010, q0086 through q0089).
- The presentation convention is stable: all app screens appear in an iPhone mockup with a full frame, notch and 9:41 status bar, and the frame is only dropped when the camera zooms in on an isolated card to focus on the copy test (sheets 0002 and 0003).

Visual proportion: the video splits its time between structure slides, app mockups with name tests applied, and cuts of the presenter at the table, with the presenter occupying long blocks of consecutive frames in some sheets (seven consecutive frames in sheet 0004 and three in sheet 0010) and no interface in the final closing sheet.

Recorded divergences or limits: in sheet 0011 the presenter appears with a different outfit in the last frame, brown or maroon instead of the black used in the rest of the video, which the notes record as an indication of a second recording used only for the farewell; in sheets 0002 and 0003 some frames are recorded as apparently static between cuts, with no change in interface.
