### What the images show
Basis: 19 of 19 frame sheets opened, all codes checked.

- The app icon appears disassembled in a diagram: the Safari icon enlarged and blurred at the edges, then the three layers separated and labeled "Background", "Foreground 1", "Foreground 2", with the two foreground ones over a checkered background indicating transparency, and finally the finished circular icon with glow and shadow, repeated side by side in variations (sheet 0002, q0013 to q0017).
- The touch target measurements are annotated on top of the real component, not in loose text: a heart icon inside a gray circle first gets a yellow "60pt" rectangle around it, then gets "8pt" on the four inner margins, breaking the total area down into visual element plus empty space; then a row of circular buttons gets yellow spacing marks between them, which the notes read as apparently 16pt, and the music app grid carries the 16pt annotation written on the screen (sheet 0009, q0077 to q0081).
- The small button gets its own treatment: a disclosure control next to "Playlists" is measured at "28pt" with an arrow pointing to it, and on the Photos screen the cell made up of image plus text "Mia, June 29" is surrounded by a dashed yellow rectangle, showing that the touch area was defined for the whole set and not for the visible arrow next to it (sheet 0010, q0082 to q0086).
- List spacing appears as a wrong and right pair: two identical settings panels side by side, one with a red x badge at the top and the other with a green check, the latter with the "4pt" annotation between the Wi-Fi and Bluetooth items, so the hover effect doesn't intrude on the neighboring item (sheet 0011, q0093, q0094).
- The concentric corner rule is turned into a diagram over a real component: the "Family Dance Party" album cell gets a rounded yellow outline and a small circle in the top left corner marking the radius, and in the next frame the label "inner corner radius + padding" is overlaid on the same cell (sheet 0011, q0097, q0098).
- In the same album grid, the frames first show the cells without an outline and then each one outlined in yellow, revealing that the hover shape follows the whole card, cover plus text, as a single element (sheet 0011, q0095, q0096).
- The fit of the ornament into the window is taught through comparison and measurement: the Notes toolbar appears isolated in two states, one pulled away from the edge with a red x and the other overlapping the bottom edge with a green check, and in the playlist panel the "20pt" annotation marks exactly the distance of that overlap (sheet 0015, q0128 to q0134). The ornament icons appear circular and isolated, with no solid bar around them.
- Typography comes as a comparative diagram between platforms: columns with a rectangle icon for iPhone and a glasses-shaped icon for the spatial device, "Body" and "Title" samples, and in the next frame the spatial device's line becomes visibly thicker and gets the labels "Medium" and "Bold" below it (sheet 0005, q0040 to q0042).
- Vibrancy is demonstrated through the difference between frames, not through explanation: the same translucent "Astonishing" information panel, with tabs, thumbnail, text and two buttons, stays in the same position and composition while the video behind it changes scene completely, ocean, elephant, aquatic creature, animal in a green field, dinosaurs and foliage, changing tone with each background (sheets 0006 and 0007, q0050 to q0059).
- The glass material is shown in several lighting conditions and with an explicit counterpoint: the translucent window appears at different angles and framings in a home setting, part of the frames empty and part with the music app interface inside, first in bright light and then in a dark setting, against the frame with the text "Avoid opaque windows" over solid blue gray panels with no interface at all (sheets 0003 and 0004, q0022 to q0032).
- The green and red badge pair becomes the didactic convention of the entire video, applied to different components: e-book cards, FaceTime notifications with a grayed out action button against a bright green "Join" button, settings lists and toolbars (sheets 0004, 0007, 0008, 0011, 0015).
- Ergonomics appears in a 3D mockup, with the app window scaled against the furniture: a large screen floating on the wall of a room with a sofa, armchairs and a bookshelf, seen from two angles, and the Freeform canvas with the note "California Super Bloom" occupying a wide horizontal screen on the wall (sheets 0008 and 0009, q0071 to q0074).
- The translation from iOS to spatial is shown literally in a sequence of frames: a smartphone with the screen off, then the same device with the vertical strip of photos by year, and in the next frame the same list of years jumping to a large panel on the room's wall, which then appears with reduced opacity and afterward returns to the device (sheet 0013, q0113 to q0117).
- The ornament that appears and disappears is seen frame by frame: a video window with minimal controls crosses several documentary scenes keeping the same border, then the "Astonishing" panel appears overlaid at the bottom, and afterward the whole window fades until almost invisible (sheet 0016, q0136 to q0143).
- Stacked modals and push navigation appear as a sequence of windows: the Apple Music window receives a secondary Apple TV+ account window, which then switches content to the list of active subscriptions with a family sharing toggle; and the "Expand View" button in a media window is followed, in the very next frame, by the same scene filling the frame with no window border (sheet 0018, q0155 to q0159). The frame with the text "Avoid white-background buttons unless selected" sits over a real Apple Music window in a room, and the red subscription banner appears in the following two frames, now without the text (sheet 0017, q0151 to q0153).

Visual proportion: across the 19 sheets the presenter frames concentrate on topic transitions, with title screens and the list "UI Foundations, Layout, From screen to spatial" marking each change, while the body of the sheets is occupied by app interface in a rendered 3D environment, annotated measurement diagrams and right-and-wrong comparison screens; the last sheet is only farewell and a final card.

Recorded divergences or limits: on sheets 0004, 0007 and 0010 the notes record that the speech in that passage does not directly describe what the screen shows, the "Books" cards with green and red indicators, the FaceTime cards and the "Memories" example, classifying the relation as apparent and not asserted; on sheet 0009 the spacing between buttons in the row is recorded as "apparently 16pt"; on sheet 0018 the description of the change between frames starts from q0154, which does not appear in that sheet's list of viewed frames.
