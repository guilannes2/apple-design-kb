### What the images show
Basis: 23 of 23 frame sheets viewed, all codes checked.
- A single case study sustains almost the entire talk: the fictional "Kitchen Toaster" app in an iPhone mockup over a dark background, with a centered toaster icon and a solid orange rectangular button, slightly rounded corners and centered white text, as the screen's only action element (sheet 0002, q0014).
- The state diagram works as a throughline and is visually edited to direct attention: six nodes linked in a cycle (Toaster Ready, Request Toast, Making Toast, Toast Done, plus Cancel and Eat Toast) and, in the next frame, three of them faded out to leave only "Request Toast" in strong white (sheet 0003, q0021 to q0026); the same move of fading out nodes returns in sheet 0009 (q0076 to q0078). The notes record that the speech does not describe this feature, it only exists in the image.
- The label choice appears as a sequence of alternatives on the same screen: the word "Toast" alone in orange, then a bread slice icon in its place, then two screens side by side comparing icon against text inside a button (sheet 0007, q0055 to q0060).
- The control's shape is compared in a simultaneous grid: nine button variations in three shapes (pill, rounded corner rectangle, nearly straight rectangle) by three treatments (gradient, solid, outline only) all shown at the same time (sheet 0007, q0062).
- Proximity and grouping are tested by moving the button: in one frame three screens with the orange bar stuck to the bottom of the screen, in the next frame two screens with the bar right below the toaster icon (sheet 0008, q0067 to q0068).
- A translucent white circle overlaid on the screen marks where the finger touches, and it always appears next to the button's edge, never in the center, visually supporting the discussion of a touch area larger than the button's drawn shape (sheet 0009, q0080; sheet 0010, q0082 to q0084 and q0088 to q0090; sheet 0011, q0091 and q0092; sheet 0012, q0103 to q0105). In q0087 the same screen appears without the circle.
- The pressed state is made by darkening the same color: the frames alternate the button between vivid orange and darkened/brownish orange with the touch point at the edges (sheet 0010, q0087 to q0090).
- The control's state transition is visible frame by frame: the toaster icon gains a golden gradient rising from the base, the status label "Toasting..." appears above the button, and the button itself switches from darkened "Make Toast" to vivid orange "Stop Toasting" (sheet 0012, q0103 to q0108).
- In some sheets the screen shows two buttons stacked vertically, the top one faded and the bottom one vivid, showing at the same time the available action and the active action instead of cutting between screens (sheets 0013, 0014 and 0018; q0126, q0156 and q0159). In q0161 the same composition appears with only one button.
- The prototype is photographed outside the slide: a hand holding a physical iPhone with the app screen against a light, blurred background (sheet 0008, q0069 and q0070) and, on stage, the same screen enlarged on the big screen while a hand in the foreground holds the real device (sheet 0011, q0094 and q0095; sheet 0013, q0113 and q0114).
- The slides follow a single template, fixed title on the left and stacked bullets with no graphic markers, with the text accumulating one line per frame (sheet 0004, q0028 to q0033; sheet 0005, q0037 to q0041; sheet 0014, q0118 to q0120; sheet 0023, q0199 to q0202).
- Progress within the talk is signaled by text color, the current item in strong white and the rest in faded gray, repeated in the "Inspiration" and "Building Blocks" slides (sheet 0015, q0132; sheet 0020, q0176; sheet 0022). The three phase model also has its own graphic form: three orange discs seen in profile, shown together and with no emphasis in the first appearance (sheet 0005, q0045) and then with the current block's disc highlighted, "Before" in sheet 0006, "During" in sheet 0008 (q0071) and "After" in sheet 0011 (q0097).
- The sound comparison gets a visual interface: a column of circles A, B and C beside an app screen that does not change, with only one circle in vivid orange at a time (sheet 0018, q0156 to q0159), and then a green circle with a checkmark next to the "C", confirming the choice (sheet 0019). An orange dot marker below the diagram line moves from left to right across the sheets until stopping under "Toast Done" (sheet 0017, q0151 and q0152; sheet 0019, q0166 to q0171; sheet 0020, q0172 and q0173).
- Abstract sound concepts turn into literal images: the same toaster icon in tiny size and then giant for low versus high pitch, and a thin horizontal line with the word "Duration" above it (sheet 0021, q0182 to q0187). The physical world enters as a reference: a 3D model of a white toaster with an orange button labeled "Eject Toast" (sheet 0004, q0030), a collage of three photos of real buttons from a keyboard, pen and doorbell (sheet 0015, q0135), a real metal object touched in extreme close up (sheet 0017, q0146 and q0147) and a piano keyboard projected on the big screen (sheet 0020, q0178).
- The talk breaks its own pattern once, showing system captures instead of the fictional app: an incoming call on the lock screen with "Remind Me", "Message" and the slide control, and a conversation in the Messages app with the iOS keyboard visible (sheet 0022, q0192 to q0194).
Visual proportion: almost the entire video has content on screen, text slides, app mockups, diagrams or reference photos, and only the opening sheet is limited to stage, audience and title screen, with occasional close ups of the presenter between blocks (sheets 0005, 0009, 0011, 0013, 0014, 0015, 0020, 0022 and 0023).
