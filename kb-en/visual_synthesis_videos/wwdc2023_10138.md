### What the images show
Basis: 15 of 15 frame sheets viewed, codes checked.
- The structure of the video is signaled by an overlaid list of topics, "Design principles", "Navigation", "Layout" and "Color and materials", with the active item in bold black and the rest in light gray, reappearing at each change of subject (sheet 0001, q0007 to q0009; sheet 0004, q0035; sheet 0009, q0078; sheet 0012, q0102).
- The navigation patterns get a permanent technical caption: a small text label centered under each watch naming "NavigationSplitView", "TabView" and "NavigationStack", always in the same position while the content of the watch faces changes (sheet 0005, q0037 and q0039; sheet 0007, q0055; sheet 0008, q0070).
- The inheritance of the two-column layout is shown through resizing, the same Weather app appears on iPad in landscape mode with list and detail, then compared simultaneously across three screen sizes down to the watch (sheet 0005, q0040 to q0043).
- SwiftUI code appears in monospaced text with syntax highlighting always next to the equivalent result on the watch face, and a specific snippet is highlighted with a light blue background exactly when the watch screen changes to reflect that snippet (sheet 0006, q0053; sheet 0007, q0060 to q0062; sheet 0008, q0066 to q0069; sheet 0011, q0097 to q0098).
- The element animation between tabs is shown at the API level: the blue highlight moves from a snippet with "isSource: selection == 0" to another with "isSource: selection != 0", linking the Activity ring's position to the TabView's selection variable (sheet 0008, q0068 to q0069).
- The basis of the layout system is justified by a hardware diagram, columns labeled "Series 0-3", "Series 4-6", "Series 7-8" and "Ultra" with proportional rectangles and measurements from 38MM to 49MM under each one, with a physical watch superimposed in the center (sheet 0009, q0079 to q0080).
- The reference grid appears drawn over the watch face itself, forming a cross and concentric circles, and then isolated in green lines with no app content at all, exposing the mesh that the speech only describes as mathematics derived from the curvature (sheet 0009, q0081; sheet 0010, q0082 and q0090; sheet 0011, q0091).
- The three fundamental layouts appear labeled over real apps, "Dial" on the rings with grid, "Infographic" on World Clock with a map and "List" on the message inbox, followed by pairs of watches changing content through a translucent transition as a sampling of the variety within each category (sheet 0010, q0083 to q0089).
- Toolbar placement is taught by an anchored annotation: the ".topBarTrailing" label connected by a straight line to the cloud icon in the top right corner of the weather screen (sheet 0011, q0095).
- Modifier effect verifiable between frames: with ".controlSize(.large)" highlighted in the code, the Now Playing pause button appears larger than in the previous frame (sheet 0011, q0097 to q0098).
- Materials and background colors are shown first as a pure sample, three watches with only solid gradient backgrounds and no icon or text, and in the next frame two of them receive Move and Exercise content, keeping the third as a clean background (sheet 0012, q0107 to q0108).
- Accent color differentiating screens of the same type: pink in Move, green in Sleep Stages and teal in Stand, each with its corresponding chart (sheet 0013, q0109).
- The typographic hierarchy is presented outside of any app, as rounded pills labeled "Primary", "Secondary" and "Tertiary" in decreasing opacity over a dark background, and next to it as text blocks in decreasing sizes (sheet 0013, q0112 to q0114).
- Code annotation anchored by a straight line to the screen background: ".containerBackground(.green.gradient, for: .navigation)" pointing to the background of the decibel meter (sheet 0013, q0117).
- In the next block the same screen receives two simultaneous annotations, the one for the background and ".foregroundStyle(.secondary)" pointing to a graphic element on the right, and shortly after it reappears with no annotation at all (sheet 0014, q0118 and q0120).
- The supporting material mixes render and reality, the same World Clock screen appears first as a product still and then in a real photo of a wrist, and the closing brings the "Wrap-up" block over a still life of a wooden table with three related sessions labeled "WWDC23" (sheet 0006, q0047 to q0049; sheet 0015, q0127 and q0128).
Visual proportion: practically all fifteen sheets show watches, code or diagrams, with the two presenters appearing in interspersed frames and in transitions in which they share the frame with a watch; no recorded sheet is made up only of a presenter, and groups of watches side by side, each with a different app, are the most recurring device in the entire video.
