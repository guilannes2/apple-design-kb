### What the images show
Basis: 29 of 29 frame sheets viewed, all codes checked.

- The list of six practices works as the video's progress marker, stacked vertically next to the presenter's face, with the active item in bold and the rest in faded gray, with no extra progress graphic (sheet 0004, q0031; sheet 0007, q0062; sheet 0017, q0147; sheet 0021, q0183; sheet 0028, q0248 to q0250). The contrast of the active item follows the background: the notes record black bold on a light background in sheets 0004 and 0007 and white in sheets 0017 and 0028. The notes record that the speech only states the names of the practices once, so this orientation device exists only on screen.
- Abstract concepts turn into an icon grid with a label below: the diversity axes appear in a five-column grid, in light gray on black, with the cited item turning pure white while the rest darken (sheet 0002, q0016 to q0018, where the notes count five rows; sheet 0027, q0237 and q0238, where they count twenty items in four rows of five). The same pattern reappears in four symmetrical columns for the "Content" screen, with a large icon, bold label and smaller subitems (sheet 0003, q0026 and q0027).
- The before-and-after pair is made by keeping the layout exactly the same and swapping only the content: the "To-Do" list keeps the circular checkbox and colored category dots while each item is rewritten, with the white highlight following the swap item by item (sheet 0006, q0047 to q0053); the food app keeps the two-column grid per meal and swaps only the photos and dish names (sheet 0007, q0056 to q0060).
- The structural change to the form is shown field by field: three fixed fields labeled "Father", "Mother", "Child" with an add-child button give way to repeatable "Family member" fields, each with a "Role" selector next to the name and an "Add member" button (sheet 0011, q0092 and q0093 to q0097 and q0098).
- Dynamic Type is demonstrated by several iPhones lined up in the same image, from two to four devices in increasing sizes, with arrows and annotation labels pointing to the corresponding typographic elements (sheet 0014, q0119 and q0120). Next, three frames compare the checkbox circles and status indicators of the same list, which the notes describe as apparently thinner in the first and thicker in the following two, under the font-weight caption (q0121 to q0123).
- VoiceOver is explained by white speech bubbles with a triangular point overlaid on the app's actual screenshot, pointing to the element and quoting what would be announced, first on the post's star icon and then on the profile header (sheet 0015, q0132 and q0135). In the second bubble, the generic avatar switches to showing a specific photo.
- Contrast measurements are annotated directly on the product's interface: the same game dialog box appears in two overlaid versions, one labeled as standard colors and the other as high-contrast mode, then with two large ratio numbers and the minimum-ratio caption (sheet 0023, q0205 to q0207). On the next sheet the same box only changes the fill color of the main button, from orange to blue, with the ratio annotated alongside by a thin line in both versions and a third frame that repeats the blue version now without the number (sheet 0024, q0208, q0210 and q0211).
- Color alone as a carrier of state is shown and then corrected on the same screen: two dots, one blue and one orange, with no shape or icon to distinguish them, receive text labels connected by leader lines (sheet 0023, q0199 to q0201).
- Error states appear as part of the component, not as an exception: the same name field alternates between a red outline with a specific message right below and a neutral outline with no message, with the same values typed in (sheet 0025, q0218 to q0219 and q0221). The final solution reduces two fields to a single full-name field, keeping the same button and the same keyboard (sheet 0026, q0226).
- Localization appears at three distinct levels of depth. Full layout mirroring for Arabic, with navigation and alignment reversed on the same icon-grid screen (sheet 0017, q0149) and a caption in German on an iPad player (q0150 and q0151). Phrase rewriting that preserves the composition, swapping the idiomatic expression for direct language on the same featured card (sheet 0019, q0165 to q0166). And content adaptation in the recipe app in Hindi, where the cards move from translated Western dishes to Indian dishes and a festival section that did not exist before appears, with an explanatory label connected by a thin line to that section (sheet 0020, q0173 to q0178).
- System material is shown in layers: in Control Center, the translucent accessibility-shortcuts panel gets a second semi-transparent text-size panel on top, with a slider and a two-button toggle in the footer, glass on glass, with a visible finger trail indicating the gesture (sheet 0016, q0138 to q0140). On the keyboard, a curved line traced over the keys records the trail of the swipe gesture while the three suggested words change between frames (q0143 and q0144).
- Two palettes of the same onboarding screen are shown side by side, varying only the saturation of the four decorative circles and the fill of the main button, from vibrant blue to grayish tones (sheet 0009, q0080 and q0081).
- The game's character-creation screen reuses a single frame for completely different categories: a colored header with the panel name, a grid area of square blocks, a highlight outline on the chosen option and a back button in the corner, serving equally for skin tone, hairstyle and voice language (sheet 0027, q0241 to q0243).
- The closing shows documentation as an interface product: the inclusion page in the Human Interface Guidelines with a text-item sidebar, a title and running paragraph, and a horizontal row of avatars with different appearances at the top of the content area (sheet 0029, q0255).
- Gender is handled through typography before it becomes a component: a list of terms in large type, each word in a different color in a gradient from green to magenta, suggesting a spectrum (sheet 0008, q0068 and q0069); then the comparison between three fixed single-choice buttons and a free-text field with a clear button and a privacy toggle below (sheet 0026, q0231 to q0233).

Visual proportion: almost all 29 sheets record studio shots of one of the two presenters, but most of them alternate these shots with an app screen, an infographic slide or illustrative photography; the sheets where there is only the presenter and photography, with no interface, are few, like the opening one and the one that introduces the color topic.

Recorded divergences or limits: on sheet 0010 the notes point out that the two frames of the onboarding screen show the same text with no change between them, that is, the copy correction the speech proposes does not appear on the screen within the frames viewed. On sheet 0003 the two frames of the "Content" screen are recorded as identical, with no difference in state. On sheet 0018 the two frames of the travel app card are also recorded with no visible difference.
