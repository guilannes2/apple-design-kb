### What the images show
Basis: 16 of 16 frame sheets viewed, all codes checked.
- The spacing rule appears as a yellow graphic annotation overlaid on the real interface, not as a loose number: yellow boxes around three circular icons, each box larger than the icon and with "60" written below it, and a horizontal measurement line labeled "60 pt" above a sphere floating over a wooden table (sheet 0002, q0010 and q0011). The same concept is demonstrated twice, once in a flat interface and once on a three-dimensional object.
- The animation curves are shown as technical graphs with labeled axes: two line graphs with the vertical axis at 100% and 20% and the horizontal axis at 0.8 s and 1.2 s, a curve that is almost flat at the start and rises steeply near the end, each with a face avatar above it, one unnamed and the other labeled "Nathan" (sheet 0006, q0050 and q0051). The notes link these graphs to the curve the speech recommends for the "Ramp" category; the frames themselves do not carry this label.
- The default versus highlight pair is taught through static images side by side, with a transformation arrow between them: an outlined heart in a gray circle on the left and the same heart filled in gold inside a gold circle on the right (sheet 0004, q0029 and q0030). An earlier comparison, separate from this one, shows two outlined hearts side by side labeled "Standard" and "Custom" (sheet 0002, q0017).
- The environment icon's scale effect appears as a difference between two frames of the same grid: in q0046 twelve circular landscape icons in a regular grid with a caption below, in q0047 the central "Mount Hood" icon grows and changes from a circle to an elongated oval, revealing a wide photo, while the neighboring icons remain at their original size (sheet 0006).
- The same content-reveal mechanism appears in a text card: the small "California Coast" card with two cut-off lines grows and shows the full description paragraph, keeping the same image-on-the-left, text-on-the-right layout at both sizes (sheet 0006, q0048 and q0049).
- The highlight in a row of content is done through a difference in color saturation: three "The Rainbow / Apple Park" cards with identical layout in which the card the notes read as being looked at appears with vivid color, a legible title and a large rainbow illustration, and the other two are faded, desaturated and with only a trace of the illustration (sheet 0013, q0109). In an earlier sheet the same row appears at two moments, first with one card more vivid than the other two and then with all three at similar saturation to each other (sheet 0012, q0107 and q0108).
- The three animation category names appear as a progress diagram within the explanation, with the active item on a solid black background and the inactive ones in light gray, a caption below each one: "Instant", "Delayed" and "Ramp", with "Delayed" highlighted (sheet 0005, q0043 and q0045).
- A time label is recorded as a real sequence, not as a frozen state: a bubble above the progress bar of a video player, with values that advance from "0:43 -0:41" to "0:45 -0:39" and then "0:48 -0:36" across three consecutive frames, while the background video changes (sheet 0005, q0040 to q0042). The notes associate this sequence with the instant animation example given in the speech.
- Persistent controls have both states recorded in the same framing: a floating video call window with no visible controls and, in the next frame, the same window with a full bar below the video (green camera, microphone, ellipsis, red end button), with no change to the video content (sheet 0015, q0127 and q0128).
- Right and wrong marking with color appears as a teaching device in two situations. In a horizontal strip of destination cards, a green right circle appears below the "Yellowstone" card and red wrong signs appear further along the strip, near the "Petrified Forest / Arizona" and "Derest" with "Death Valley" cards; the strip mixes complete cards, with photo and title, and gray cards with no photo and a cut-off word, and the notes read the set as an example of right and wrong (sheet 0007, q0055 to q0057). In the other, a circular confirmation button in the bottom right corner of an editing toolbar goes from a red "x" to a green mark between two frames, and in the second frame the toolbar's table icon also appears highlighted in blue (sheet 0008, q0065 and q0066).
- The screens the speech uses to decide when to enable Look to Scroll appear in successive frames, all as real system captures: a long note with text and a photo in Notes in three columns (sheet 0011, q0093), an article with a large photo on the "surfacemag.com" site (q0095), an episode carousel with duration in the TV app (q0096) and the "General" panel of Settings with a list of categories and a green toggle, which the notes record as the counterexample dominated by controls (q0097 and q0098).
- In Notes, the visible change between two frames is only in the sidebar list of notes, which goes from a neutral background to a darker, bluish background while the open note on the right stays the same; the notes read this as a highlight state change in the list (sheet 0012, q0100 and q0101).
- The three-row grid of pills shows increasing fill stages of the same button, from a thin, faded outline to a solid white background and then to solid blue with a white icon (sheet 0010, q0085).
- The visual vocabulary repeats throughout the 16 sheets and the speech does not name it: practically all controls, context menus, toolbars and content cards use a dark or light translucent background, well-rounded corners and simple monochrome icons.
- The closing card carries, in writing, references the speech does not cite: "Next steps" with two short sentences and a list of two earlier sessions with the year, separated by thin horizontal lines (sheet 0016, q0136).
Visual proportion: by the notes' count, most of the described frames carry interface, annotation over interface or slide, with about two dozen frames showing only the presenter and a few blank frames or frames of a blurred home environment.
Recorded divergences or limits: in sheets 0003 and 0008 the notes point out that the frames capture static moments and not the opening animation, growth or the highlight effect that appears and then disappears; in sheet 0009 the notes say it is not possible to state whether the small icon that appears in the corner of some tab thumbnails is the close button or another element; in sheet 0011, q0097 and q0098 show no visible difference in state between them; in sheet 0010 the notes record that the pill grid does not correspond to any example cited in the matching stretch of speech, and in sheet 0012 the saturation effect of the cards is not described by the speech.
