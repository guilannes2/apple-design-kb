### What the images show
Basis: 8 of 8 frame sheets viewed, all codes checked.
- The boundary between icon and glyph is drawn on the slide, not just stated: four iPhone icons in a single row progress from the smooth, monochrome silhouette to the colored version with texture, gradient and shadow, and from q0008 to q0009 a bracket labeled "Glyphs" appears covering only the two simplest ones (sheet 0001).
- The contrast returns shortly after in isolated objects: the wallet glyph in a thin white outline on a dark background and, in the next frame, the Phone app icon, rounded green with the white symbol inside (sheet 0002, q0012 and q0013).
- The conceptualization exercise is built as a controlled iteration: the composition does not change (a centered white line glyph above the word "Delicious", same size and same vertical alignment), and only the symbol is swapped, going through hamburger, ice cream cone, smiling face and heart; the word appears alone before any symbol, the hamburger occupies one frame and the other three symbols appear in two frames each (sheet 0003, q0020 to q0027).
- The universal symbol is validated with real photography and a progressive close-up: the blue German highway sign with written destinations and an airplane symbol enters first behind the slide text, then without the text, and finally in a tight zoom on the square with the airplane (sheet 0002, q0016 to q0018).
- Optical weight is shown with overlaid measurement guides: four glyphs of unequal sizes (large speech bubble, smaller phone, video camera, envelope) receive two parallel diagonal lines touching the top of each one, which did not exist in the previous frame (sheet 0004, q0035 and q0036), and in sheet 0005 the same four reappear with sizes already adjusted and a green approval stamp (q0037 and q0039).
- Line weight is corrected as a before and after in the same set: edit, trash, information and search first appear with visibly different strokes from each other and then with a uniform stroke, and only then do they receive the green stamp (sheet 0005, q0041 to q0043).
- The optical centering adjustment gets its own instrument: two share glyphs appear side by side, one of them marked with a red X, then the red glyph is isolated in the center and, in the next frame, appears inside a dashed circular crosshair that works as a center guide (sheet 0006, q0047 to q0049). The play glyph receives similar treatment, first between rewind and fast forward, then isolated and enlarged inside the circle (sheet 0005, q0044 and q0045).
- The talk uses a constant color convention for verdicts, green with a check for the correct state and red with an X for the incorrect one, applied to different examples, the media icons and the share glyph (sheets 0005 and 0006).
- The relationship between typography weight and glyph weight is reduced to two options labeled "Light" and "Heavy" with a checkmark next to each one (sheet 0006, q0054), and then this overlay is removed from on top of a real profile screen, revealing the list of items with a glyph to the right of each line (sheet 0007, q0055 and q0056).
- System spaces appear as usage captures: MacBook Touch Bar with the thin control bar, the iOS quick action menu in a vertical list with an icon to the left of each item (Take Selfie, Record Video, Record Slo mo, Take Photo) over the Mail app, and a grid of white monochrome glyphs described as the set used in toolbars and sidebars (sheet 0004, q0029 to q0033).
- The context menu of a third-party app is shown with the hand still touching the screen and then without it, and then placed side by side with the profile screen of the same app, leaving both screens in the same image (sheet 0007, q0059 to q0061).
- The work tool appears on screen: a Photoshop capture with the "Home Screen Quick Action Glyphs iOS 11" panel, standard glyphs in red labeled by function on the left and empty boxes labeled "Custom Glyphs" on the right, and the next frame reveals more rows with labels like Confirmation, Contact and Date (sheet 0007, q0062 and q0063).
- The structure slides follow a single pattern, a short title at the top and stacked items in a lighter weight, seen in "Effective Glyphs" (sheet 0002), "Design Considerations" (sheet 0004), "Crafting Glyphs" (sheet 0006) and in the final "Recap" (sheet 0008). The staged reveal is recorded in two of them: "Effective Glyphs" gains the list of three items from q0014 to q0015, and "Recap" goes from one item to three from q0064 to q0065.
Visual proportion: almost all frames feature a slide, an isolated glyph or an app screen capture; the presenter alone appears in short stretches of sheets 0005, 0006, 0007 and in the two final frames of sheet 0008, which close with an empty stage.
