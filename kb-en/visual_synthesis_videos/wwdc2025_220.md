### What the images show
Basis: 10 of 10 frame sheets viewed, all codes checked.
- The agenda appears as a side list next to the presenter, with the current item in bold black and the rest in light gray, and the bold advances from subtopic to subtopic as the speech progresses within the icon-design block (sheet 0001, q0003; sheet 0002, q0017 and q0018; sheet 0006, q0046 and q0047; sheet 0007, q0058 and q0063; sheet 0009, q0078).
- The iPhone dock is used as a single sample to move through the appearance modes in sequence: original colors, dark monochrome and dark red tint, always inside a rounded-corner capsule that lets the floral wallpaper show through behind it; only in tint mode does a second row of icons appear below the capsule (sheet 0002, q0011 to q0014).
- The construction grid appears explicitly on screen in two ways: solid-color canvas blocks with a rounded corner, with the camera zooming in on the corner to show the curvature, and the calculator icon overlaid on a grid of visible guide lines (sheet 0003, q0023 to q0026).
- The circular grid is shown alone first, as concentric circles plus radial guide lines, then with the music icon fitted and aligned to the center, and finally the icon without the grid, a sequence of three frames that separates structure, alignment and result (sheet 0005, q0038 to q0040).
- Real drawing tools enter the screen: the same icon template open in two design software programs, one with a light interface and the other dark, with the circular grid in the center and the list of named layers on the left; the properties panel on the right is only recorded in the light-interface one (sheet 0005, q0042 and q0043).
- The before-and-after comparison is the most repeated device in the video, always with the two icons side by side and the new one generally marked with a short label: it appears in the case of the contacts book against the new circular icon, in chess in 3D perspective against the flatter, more frontal version, in the house with shadow and bevel against the simplified version, and in the mail envelope with arrows indicating the direction of the change (sheet 0004, q0028 to q0030; sheet 0007, q0055 and q0056; sheet 0008, q0069 to q0072; sheet 0009, q0079 and q0080).
- The idea of layers is shown literally taken apart: in one frame the translucent white speech bubble and the rounded green base float separately over a black background, and in the next frame they are combined into the finished icon (sheet 0006, q0048 and q0049).
- The vector construction of an element appears in two stages: first the gear as a white line outline on black with the anchor points visible on the teeth and curves, then the same gear rendered in metal with volume and light, keeping the same points overlaid (sheet 0009, q0074 and q0075).
- The two standard backgrounds are presented in isolation, as two squares with no icon on top, one light and one dark, each with its label written on screen, fixing the naming that the speech does not spell out (sheet 0009, q0081).
- The material research enters as photography of physical objects on a wooden table, with translucent discs, a green bubble, a blue envelope and a wavy line, cutting straight from the presenter to this still life before the first icons (sheet 0001, q0007).
- The limits of automatic adaptation are shown through camera movement and through state, not through text: a progressive zoom on the square icon of a third-party app down to an extreme close-up, without the artwork changing, and the photo booth icon going from semi-transparent to opaque and then duplicated in overlap with a slight offset (sheet 0004, q0031 to q0036).
- The material is demonstrated through variation of the same icon: the cloud with sun appears in close-up, then complete next to the dark monochrome version, and then gains a third variation with floral texture appearing behind it, which shows the translucency instead of describing it (sheet 0007, q0060 to q0062).
- The icons are almost always presented in isolation over a plain black or gray background, without a device frame and without home screen context, which leaves shape and edge as the frame's only subject (sheet 0001, q0008 and q0009; sheet 0006, q0049 and q0052; sheet 0007, q0060).
- The closing moves from the isolated icon to the whole set: a complete home screen grid with all icons in the new style, and between two consecutive frames the focus of sharpness moves down from the upper rows to the lower ones (sheet 0010, q0084 and q0085).
Visual proportion: according to the notes, the presenter appears in nine of the ten sheets, absent only in sheet 0004, sometimes accompanied by the side agenda list or an icon beside her, sometimes alone in frame; the rest of the time is occupied by isolated icons, pair comparisons, construction grids and captures of design tools. No sheet shows a code screen, and the only software interface recorded is that of the two drawing programs (sheet 0005).
Recorded divergences or limits: the notes mark identifications by resemblance rather than certainty, such as the two design software programs described by approximation, and a comparison label noted as present or absent without a definite conclusion (sheet 0005, q0042 and q0043; sheet 0008, q0070 to q0072). They also record a black frame with the number "28" large and centered, without linking it to any point in the speech (sheet 0009, q0077).
