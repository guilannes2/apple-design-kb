- The grouping of items is shown with the intermediate state visible: four distinct icons side by side in one frame, a single "+" button in the next (sheet 0004, q0033 to q0035).
- The "Customize" panel appears as a modal grid of icons in rounded squares, some with a yellow dashed outline indicating they are draggable, and the body of the document stays still while only the bar changes above it (sheet 0004, q0029 to q0032).
- The rule separating the document menu and Share is shown as two physically distinct panels: the document menu carries a header with thumbnail, name and file size plus a list of actions with an icon to the right of each item, and the sharing panel carries circular contact avatars in a row, a grid of app icons and secondary actions below; one of the frames still overlays the menu with the text that states the rule, and the next frame shows the same menu already clean (sheet 0006, q0049 to q0053).
- The edit menu appears in two variants with the same internal order of items, one horizontal and compact next to the selected text for touch and another vertical in a list with an icon to the right of each action for pointer, and in one of the frames the item "Format" appears highlighted in white and in the previous one it does not (sheet 0007, q0056 to q0060).
- The context menu over a canvas object expands horizontally and reveals options that were not visible before, such as Select Objects, Add to Shape and Comment, next to the standard editing actions (sheet 0007, q0061 to q0062).
- Find and replace appears in two forms: a small dropdown menu with advanced options anchored above the iPad's virtual keyboard, and a compact bar with a field, a results counter and up and down arrows positioned in the footer when there is a physical keyboard (sheet 0008, q0064 to q0066).
- The navigation contrast is direct and visual: the Files screen carries back and forward arrows before the title, while the Photos sidebar, denser and with collapsible sections, appears without any control of that kind (sheet 0009, q0076 against q0079).
- Search is presented in four linked steps: an activated field in the top right corner showing context tabs, partially typed text evolving into different suggestions, a schematic diagram of three blocks on a white background for recents, recommendations and filters, and finally the literal application of that same grouping on the real Books screen (sheet 0010, q0082 to q0086).
- Multiple selection without edit mode is shown with the title replaced by a count, cards highlighted in yellow and an actions menu that repeats the number in each label, and before that a destination folders menu appearing during the drag (sheet 0011, q0091 to q0096).
- The selection features are reviewed in a checklist slide where the blue circles with a check appear one by one, in the same order as spoken, with the item still unmarked left for last (sheet 0012, q0100 to q0103).
- The submenu opens to the side from the parent item, with a context icon to the right of each action, and right after that a compact detail popover appears with the priority field open (sheet 0013, q0109 to q0116).
- Editing a property without leaving the place is shown as a small floating card, not as a new screen, and the value changes between consecutive frames, from Weekly to Custom in the repeat field; in the side detail panel, a toggle goes from gray to blue between two nearly identical frames (sheet 0014, q0118 to q0124).

- The closing repeats the accumulation pattern: the still life of a toy dinosaur and bowl stays fixed on the right while the bullets enter one per cut, until completing three items plus the footer referencing another session (sheet 0016, q0140 to q0142).
