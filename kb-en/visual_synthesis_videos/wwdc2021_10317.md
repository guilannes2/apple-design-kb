### What the images show
Basis: 22 of 22 frame sheets viewed, all codes checked.

- The sample has almost no software interface: the overwhelming majority of the 191 frames are NASA archival footage, cinematic 3D renderings of rockets and capsules, or documentary-style interview shots (synthesis of sheets 0001 to 0022).
- The opening shows a regular grid of hundreds of circular avatars with horizontal pills overlaid, and in the next frame two confetti bursts appear over the same unchanged grid, an event production element that the speech does not mention (sheet 0001, q0001 to q0002).
- The opening title is understated: thin white text left-aligned in the lower third, with a small speech bubble icon beside it, over a black background (sheet 0001, q0003).
- The richest piece of interface in the video is a tracking screen in thin white strokes on black, with antenna identification in uppercase, numbering, a mission counter in the bottom left corner and a three-view segmented control with the active item filled in solid green, the only active-versus-inactive state pattern in the whole sample (sheet 0006, q0050).
- A split screen uses the same caption layout in both halves, in the bottom left corner, with the mission name in uppercase and date and location in a lighter weight below, building a side-by-side comparison (sheet 0006, q0051).
- The engine test carries real telemetry overlaid on the video in small white monospaced font, with no box or background, with a time counter that advances between frames and camera identification and tower position labels that appear only in the second of them (sheet 0013, q0110 and q0112).
- A blue rectangular indicator in the upper left corner shows the countdown in hours, minutes and seconds format before launch, and the value drops two seconds from one frame to the next, being the only HUD element with visible numeric updating in the sample (sheet 0008, q0071 to q0072).
- The sample's only segment title follows a minimal scheme: a word in white uppercase with a short red horizontal line right below it functioning as an emphasis underline, over a darkened image (sheet 0008, q0069).
- An archival image is introduced inside a frame that mimics an old carousel slide viewer, with curved dark borders and an orange side marker, reinforcing the historical tone without a single word (sheet 0004, q0033).
- Attribution captions always stay in the same position, bottom right corner in small white text, keeping consistency across frames from different sources, from concept art to instrument imagery (sheet 0007, q0057 to q0060).
- Stage separation is communicated through an exploded-view diagram: two components isolated and spaced apart over a neutral black background, with no supporting text or caption at all (sheet 0012, q0105).
- The logo of the organization founded by the interviewee is the sample's most complete example of typographic hierarchy: an illustrated icon on the left, a text block on the right with two font weights in the two-line name and an uppercase tagline with increased tracking below, a limited palette and left alignment over a white background (sheet 0020, q0176).
- The final card reduces the same lockup principle to a minimum: the apple icon and the event text forming a single unit, centered in the upper third, monochromatic and surrounded by plenty of negative space (sheet 0022, q0190 and q0191).
- The montage builds meaning through progressive camera approach, going from a wide shot to an extreme close-up within the same sequence (sheet 0011, q0091 to q0093; sheet 0013, q0115 to q0117; sheet 0016, q0136 to q0139), through the reverse movement, moving from an extreme close-up of the hull to the whole rocket against the sky (sheet 0005, q0037 to q0039), and through minimal variation between nearly identical frames, such as the five reentry frames in which only the intensity and shape of the fire halo change (sheet 0017, q0147 to q0151).
- The few on-screen elements that appear are consistent with each other despite their distinct origins, NASA operations panel, event motion graphics and organization logo: text always small, a single accent color per element, green for active state and red for underline or mark, and a dark or neutral background under the text (synthesis of sheets 0001 to 0022).

Visual proportion: no sheet shows app interface or talk slides; the video alternates NASA footage, 3D rendering and fixed interview shots, and the on-screen elements are concentrated in about a dozen isolated frames (sheets 0001, 0006, 0008, 0012, 0013, 0020 and 0022).

Recorded divergences or limits: the notes flag uncertain readings, such as the concept art caption attributed to a neighboring frame (sheet 0007, q0055 to q0057), the loose numeric value in the corner of the engine test noted as a probable real-time measurement and the slight change in counter format between frames (sheet 0013, q0110 and q0112), and the opening avatar grid noted as a probable event screen, with no confirmation from the speech (sheet 0001). The final card was read in the notes with the text "WDC21" (sheet 0022).
