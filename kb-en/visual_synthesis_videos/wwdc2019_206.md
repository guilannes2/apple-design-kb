### What the images show
Basis: 28 of 28 frame sheets viewed, all codes checked.
- A magenta measurement-annotation layer is overlaid on the same test layout (a circled icon plus two lines of text over tea and chocolate) and returns several times throughout the presentation, with dimension lines and the values 18, 16, 29 and 90, plus labels identifying the symbol and the font, "star.circle, Large" and "System Font, 19 points" (sheet 0007, q0061 to q0063; sheet 0008, q0065 to q0067; sheet 0011, q0094 to q0097; sheet 0013, q0114 and q0115). The same UI artifact becomes a reused test bench, instead of a new screen per concept.
- The scales get explicit dimensioning: three stacked components (chip, search bar, action bar) receive the same 17-point text label and annotated heights of 24, 36 and 44, with the names Small, Medium and Large to the left of each row (sheet 0005, q0040 to q0043). The same typographic size coexists with containers of different heights.
- The scale system appears implemented as a pre-drawn matrix, not as a real-time calculation: two-dimensional grids with weights in the columns (from Ultralight to Black) and scales in the rows (Small, Medium, Large), both in the design app and in Xcode (sheet 0008, q0068 to q0070; sheet 0009, q0078 and q0079).
- The customization of a symbol is shown cell by cell: in Sketch, the teacup's stroke propagates from one template cell to the next across the frames, with slightly different outline and thickness at each combination, until the grid is nearly complete with the SVG export panel open on the right (sheet 0009, q0073 to q0077), and then the same grid already whole inside Xcode (q0078).
- A pair of twin screens labeled by system version, with a single variable: the same phrase "Rehydrate!" and the same layout, changing only the icon, one screen marked iOS 12 or earlier with a simpler gray teacup and the other iOS 13 or later with a sharper teacup (sheet 0013, q0110 to q0113).
- The argument that a typographic point is not a screen dimension is proven by dimensioning, not by words: a slide opposes the words "Typographical" and "Dimensional" and then the icon next to "Favorite" receives annotations of 33pt width by 32pt height, with the font label 28 points below (sheet 0014, q0122 to q0126). Here the annotation changes language, dimensions in black over a dark background instead of the usual magenta.
- When the content becomes abstract, the screen switches register and becomes a diagram of colored boxes: green for system symbol, blue for custom symbol, purple for regular image, with code call-out labels above each flow and orange numeric badges that increment from one frame to the next to show two entries with the same name (sheet 0012, q0104 to q0108).
- The baseline is drawn as a visible measurement: a white horizontal reference line, orange offset markings of minus 3.5 and plus 4.5 for two different icons, and an outline rectangle delimiting the bounds of the cloud icon; the markings come in between frames, first the icons without a number, then with a number (sheet 0019, q0165 to q0169).
- Horizontal alignment is demonstrated with a guide: a dotted yellow vertical line crossing the column of avatars in a comments list and, in the following frame, a bidirectional arrow measuring the distance to the text (sheet 0020, q0175 to q0177); right after, a spec card with pink annotations naming each symbol, the font and an angular measurement of 90 between dotted axes (q0179, q0180).
- The anatomy of the configuration appears in the Xcode panel, with fields the speech does not enumerate: Symbol Configuration with Point Size, Scale at Medium and Weight at Regular, and blue guides crossing the horizontal and vertical center of the icon to check centering (sheet 0015, q0132 to q0135); in sheet 0016 the same panel appears with Point Size at 500 (q0136 to q0138).
- The rendering mode difference is shown as a visual pair: the second rabbit enters next to the first, one in colored illustration and the other in blue tint outline, and with them the box with the rendering mode code appears (sheet 0024, q0214 and q0215; in q0213 there is only one rabbit and in q0216 only the code box); then a diagram of three cards, "Non-symbol Image", "Image" and "Symbol Image", with the center column dimmed and green highlight on the cards at the ends (sheet 0025, q0217 and q0218).
- The symbol naming is taught by the screen: five volume icons in a row with the technical name written in quotes below each one, and a grid of variants of the same person symbol in outline, filled and with a square frame, with action badges overlaid in the corner (sheet 0006, q0048 and q0049).
- The scales reach the designer as a real typographic feature: an OpenType Features panel in a design app with checkboxes for circle, square, solid, small and large variants, and then the Illustrator artboard with the moon symbol being positioned and selection handles around the shape (sheet 0006, q0051 to q0054); a glyph catalog with a name below each icon and a weight dropdown menu with "Heavy" checked complete the tools picture (sheet 0007, q0055, q0056).
- The code slides follow a fixed grammar: dark background, syntax highlighting, a green "new" badge in the corner and a blue highlight that moves line by line between consecutive frames to point out what changed, for example from the draw with a forced rectangle to the draw at natural size (sheet 0025, q0220 to q0221), and the button lines that only get a blue background in the following frame, the one creating the system button (sheet 0021, q0187 to q0188) and the one for per-state configuration (sheet 0022, q0193 to q0194).
- The effect of embedding a symbol in running text is shown as before and after in the same pop-over: the sentence loses two words and gains star and heart icons inline, at the same size as the surrounding font, plus a share icon inserted in the middle of the text (sheet 0026, q0226 to q0227); then the heart already appears in red when the slide about tinting comes in (q0234).
Visual proportion: according to the notes, most of the frames are screen, between app screens in an iPhone and iPad frame, concept and code slides, Sketch, Illustrator and Xcode; the presenter alone occupies few frames, at the opening, at the presenter change, in short cuts between demonstrations and at the close, and during the demonstrations he is reduced to a thumbnail in the bottom left corner.
Recorded divergences or limits: the notes say that sheet 0003 does not explicitly show the before and after of the iOS 13 redesign, the similarity between successive grids is inference; that in sheet 0005 the pair of screens in Arabic and English shows only the texts, without visually explaining the logic of localized numerals; and that some frames were ambiguous as to whether it was a cut, zoom or transition, such as q0021, besides the typography app in sheet 0006 having been identified only by appearance. The notes themselves contradict each other in sheet 0025, sometimes describing only the "Symbol Image" card in green, sometimes the two cards at the ends.
