### What the images show
Basis: 19 of 19 frame sheets viewed, all codes checked.

- The margin of the lock screen appears marked on the screen itself: thin green vertical guide lines appear on the left and right edges of the card stack, showing that Live Activities and notifications align on the same grid despite having different layouts (sheet 0002, q0016).
- The video uses a fixed pattern to compare two variations of the same component, with a neutral gray X and a neutral gray check below the cards. It reappears at least three times: in the two timer layouts, one simple and the other with pause and dismiss buttons embedded in the card (sheet 0002, q0017 and q0018), in the two green delivery cards (sheet 0004, q0030), and in the two car racing cards (sheet 0006, q0047). In sheets 0004 and 0006 the notes record a badge under each card; in sheet 0002 they record both badges under the same side, and in no case do they record which variation each badge approves.
- A light gray circle appears from one frame to the next to the left of the Apple TV scoreboard card, in the same background color as the card, which the notes read as the dismiss button derived from the colors of the Live Activity itself (sheet 0003, q0026 to q0027).
- Brand identity through color over identical structure: the same flight card, with the same text and the same layout, appears in blue as "Horizon" and in yellow as "AirJet", changing only the palette (sheet 0004, q0028 to q0029).
- Layout collapse by relevance is shown in successive states: the black soccer scoreboard with a highlight band on the play loses the play's text line, becomes dimmed gray, and replaces the clock with "Halftime", reducing height and information (sheet 0005, q0038 to q0040).
- In StandBy the extension of the background appears in progressive zoom: the yellow that belonged to the card comes to fill the whole screen of the device resting on a metal base, revealing a black pill-shaped cutout reserved for the sensor region that the content does not invade (sheet 0007, q0058 to q0063).
- The alternative without a background is shown as a direct pair: the same "AirJet" card with yellow fill and then with a fully black background, leaving the plane, text and route line in yellow floating over the black of the device (sheet 0008, q0064 to q0065).
- StandBy's night mode appears as a palette swap without a structure swap: the tide card, with a curved line chart and high and low markers, goes from grayish blue to dark red while keeping the text, chart and markers in the same place (sheet 0008, q0067 to q0068).
- The metamorphosis of the Dynamic Island is shown frame by frame in the same black pill: empty, then an incoming call with a circular photo and two round buttons, then an almost square shape with a green circular icon, then an elongated bar with a waveform, a timer and a red recording button, back to empty, and finally four small dots at the base of a wider dark shape (sheet 0009, q0074 to q0080).
- Concentricity gets its own diagram on the screen: a simple white circle is positioned inside a gray pill shape in two different positions to compare the resulting margin, and then fitted into the rounded end of a real black pill with an equal distance from the edge (sheet 0013, q0110, q0111 and q0113). On the next sheet, a thin cyan outline traces the inner perimeter of the rounded shape alongside content blocks indented from the edge (sheet 0014, q0118 to q0121).
- Size classes get a proportion reference: three mockups in sequence show the same sensor area as a narrow pill, then a wider oval, then a wide rectangle labeled "Expanded" next to it (sheet 0014, q0124 to q0126).
- The empty gap inside the compact view appears literally: frames show a considerable space between the icon on the left and the text on the right of the same pill, for example a heart with "87" on one side and a silhouette on the other, or a bicycle on one side and "Lap 5" on the other (sheet 0015, q0129 and q0131).
- The progression of detail between sizes is shown in three states: a green train icon with "2m", then a card with the line name and station below it, then the full layout with a time badge and a progress bar with the stops named (sheet 0016, q0137 to q0139).
- The alternation between simultaneous sessions is drawn as a shape transformation: an isolated black circle stretches sideways until it becomes two circular ends connected by a thin band, one of them with a green truck icon, and then it separates into two independent circles (sheet 0017, q0151 to q0153).
- An entire app condensed into the pill: the "Cycling Workout" in full screen, with a yellow route over a dark map, time, distance and a waveform, turns into a compact pill that keeps only the bicycle icon and the distance (sheet 0011, q0094 to q0095).
- In the final scenes the expanded cards are rendered in 3D perspective with the central graphic element floating above the flat surface, whether the satellite's arc, the outline of the racetrack or the coffee timer button (sheet 0018, q0156 to q0159). It is purely visual emphasis, with no literal match in the speech.

The animated opening occupies the first frames of sheet 0001, which also already brings iPhone and iPad mockups with the lock screen, and only sheet 0019 is left without interface, with the WWDC23 mark over a black background.

Recorded divergences or limits: in the opening, the title text appears partially legible and the notes record the reading as probable, not confirmed (sheet 0001). Still in sheet 0001, the iPhone and iPad pair repeats between frames with no real visible transition, that is, it looks like duplication rather than a change of state.
