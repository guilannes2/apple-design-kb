### What the images show
Basis: 12 of 12 frame sheets viewed, all codes checked.

- The rendering modes comparison table is built up in stages in front of the viewer: first only the Monochrome and Hierarchical rows, then the same table gains Palette and Multicolor, always with the mode label aligned to the left and the same seven symbols repeated in fixed columns, which lets you read the difference piece by piece on the same icon (sheet 0004, q0028 to q0033). Next the composition gets a green circular new badge in the corner and then disappears to make way for the isolated title of the automatic feature (q0034 to q0036).
- The SF Symbols app appears in real screens with a three-column layout, category navigation on the left, a central grid of symbols in square cards with the name below and a properties panel on the right with rendering, color and background controls, and switching category in the sidebar replaces the central grid entirely (sheet 0003, q0023 and q0024). The mode-selection dropdown menu opens over that panel, with the active item highlighted in blue (sheet 0005, q0038 and q0039).
- Context-based judgment is shown as a right-and-wrong pair: two horizontal sliders side by side, one labeled Hierarchical with a yellow warning icon above and the other labeled Monochrome with a green check above (sheet 0006, q0046 and q0047). The yellow warning had already appeared before, in the corner of the frame that shows the symbol in Hierarchical (sheet 0005, q0045).
- The layer panel is the video's central graphic device: diamonds stacked in light isometric perspective, numbered from the base to the top, each one containing the vector outline of that layer. It reappears with the same grammar in every example, from the phone with waves to the speaker, to the cupcake and the timer (sheet 0006, q0052; sheet 0007, q0058, q0061 and q0062; sheet 0009, q0080; sheet 0010, q0082; sheet 0011, q0096 and q0097), and starting from sheet 0009 it comes accompanied by a vertical axis labeled z-order.
- The logic of variable color is presented as a gradation, not as a shape swap: in the speaker symbol, the panel goes from one layer to four numbered ones, and then the icon appears with only the first wave in white and the rest in dark gray, with a small percentage indicator below on the right (sheet 0007, q0061 to q0063). In the phone symbol, the waves change color side by side between frames, blue on one side and gray on the other, and then both return to gray (q0055 to q0057).
- The partial participation of layers is demonstrated with three silhouettes of people: the framing stays identical and only the third figure goes from white to dark gray, keeping its shape, which makes explicit that leaving the sequence is a change of state, not of drawing (sheet 0008, q0066 to q0068).
- Draw and erase are presented first as a concept pair with a pencil icon and an eraser icon, and then demonstrated on simple geometric shapes, an outlined square over circles, over a checkered transparency background, with a blue outline marking the selected layer and the erase label appearing beside it when the outline changes color (sheet 0009, q0077 to q0079).
- The construction of the custom symbol is shown through successive states of the same cupcake: it appears filled white beside the four-layer panel (sheet 0009, q0080 and q0081) and, on the next sheet, comes back in shades of gray, becomes entirely white, gains the multicolor mode label below and finally has its base painted dark red, with color dots appearing beside layers 1 and 2 in the panel, recording the color chosen per layer (sheet 0010, q0082 to q0087).
- Badge circles labeled with the four modes change from a thin outline to solid white and solid green fill over the course of the annotation, a reading that the notes themselves record as a hypothesis (sheet 0010, q0088 to q0090; sheet 0011, q0091 and q0092), and then go on to display the cupcake itself in miniature inside each circle, each one in a different color version (q0093).
- The same comparison method closes two different examples, showing that the layer anatomy repeats: four final versions of the cupcake side by side with the mode name below each one, and four versions of the kitchen timer in the same layout, this one with a layer scale numbered 1 to 8 in the side panel, more granular than the earlier four-layer examples (sheet 0011, q0094 to q0098).
- The new symbol categories are always presented as regular grids of white icons on a pure black background, around eight columns by four rows, with uniform visual weight and outline and filled variants coexisting within the same group, before any color is applied (sheet 0002, q0017 and q0018; sheet 0003, q0019 and q0020), with a dotted-texture screen and a numeric watermark marking the scale of the expansion (q0021).
- The talk's script stays overlaid on the presenter's video as a vertical list on the left, with hierarchy by opacity and weight, current item in white and bold over a slight background blur, and the list changes active item as the section advances, reaching four items (sheet 0002, q0014 and q0015; sheet 0006, q0048; sheet 0008, q0069 and q0072).
- The symbols appear inside a real interface only at the close, with two iPhone screens, a music app and a wireless network selection screen, which in the next frame receive an overlaid layer of colored system icons that were not visible before (sheet 0012, q0101 and q0102).
- The opening illustrates the thesis of universality through typographic composition, a grid of three columns by four rows with the word love written in several languages and scripts, flanked by white outline icons and a filled red heart as a color exception (sheet 0001, q0007 to q0009).
- References to other sessions and to the tool download always use the same footer pattern, small text aligned to the left and the event acronym to the right, and at the close the app icon appears in three-dimensional style with a purple circular beta version badge (sheet 0011, q0099; sheet 0012, q0100).

Visual proportion: the sheets are dominated by symbol slides, layer panels and captures of the SF Symbols app, and the presenter appears in an isolated shot intercut through part of them (sheets 0001 to 0003, 0006, 0008, 0010 and 0012), several times already with the script overlaid on the image.

Recorded divergences or limits: on sheet 0002 the notes say that frames q0011 and q0012 show practically the same page, with a cursor difference not clearly perceptible. On sheet 0005 the reading of the three-circle diagram is declared as an assumption, not as confirmed content. On sheet 0010 the interpretation of the badges' white fill is also recorded as a hypothesis. On sheets 0009 and 0012 there are pairs of frames with no perceptible difference in content.
