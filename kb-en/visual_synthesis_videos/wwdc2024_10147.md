### What the images show
Basis: 11 of 11 frame sheets opened, all codes checked.

- The change in position of the tab bar is shown by the difference between two consecutive frames of the Clock app in dark mode: first the cropped screen with no device frame, with a thin bar at the bottom, then the full iPad frame with the bar already at the top, as a segmented bar with the labels "World Clock", "Alarms", "Stopwatch", "Timers", the active item in a light pill, "Edit" on the left and the plus sign on the right (sheet 0001, q0004 to q0009).
- The cycle between tab bar and sidebar is documented in both directions and in two apps: the top bar of Clock opens into a vertical list with the selected item highlighted in blue, in the next frame the content switches to the TV app keeping the same side structure, with the items separated by spacing into three blocks, search and home, subscriptions and store, and the library with its children, and further on this sidebar collapses back into a compact bar of icons and short labels (sheet 0002, q0010 to q0016).
- Customization mode has its own, repeated anatomy: on entering it, the sidebar gains "Reset" and "Done" at the top and a circular selection box next to each list item, with the same controls reappearing on different sheets (sheet 0003, q0020 to q0022; sheet 0007, q0061; sheet 0008, q0065).
- The placement rules are annotated on top of the real screenshot, not in a separate diagram: in an enlargement of the top bar, a thin line ending in a horizontal bracket under the tabs carries the label "Customizable" pointing to "New" and "Favorites"; in the next frame the annotation becomes "Pinned" pointing to the magnifying glass in the right corner of the bar; then "Sidebar only" appears pointing to a snippet of code (sheet 0008, q0066 to q0069).
- The code slides follow a fixed anatomy pattern: bold title on the left, green circular "NEW" badge on the same line when the API is new, two short subtitles, and a light card with rounded corners holding the code block in colored syntax, keywords in purple, literals in orange and types in teal (sheet 0004, q0031 to q0035).
- The code is revealed incrementally, with the new snippet highlighted in light blue, and the difference between frames shows exactly which line corresponds to each phrase: the same block gains the additions for typed selection, then the search tab line, then three customization identifier calls added at once (sheet 0004, q0031, q0032; sheet 0005, q0044, q0045; sheet 0008, q0070 to q0072).
- The SwiftUI and UIKit pair is always assembled in the same order and with nearly identical titles, a feature visible in the sequence of slides and not announced as a rule: tab syntax, search tab, adding the sidebar, sidebar actions, drag and drop destination, customization and change observation (sheets 0004, 0005, 0006, 0009, 0010).
- A single slide places the two appearances of the same music app side by side, the version labeled "Tab bar" with four items at the bottom and the version labeled "Sidebar" with the same items followed by a "Library" group indented under a small caps header, the active item highlighted in red (sheet 0005, q0037).
- The complete collapse of the sidebar is shown by the difference between frames in the example game app: the side list disappears and the main content comes to occupy the full width of the device (sheet 0005, q0042, q0043).
- Drag and drop appears captured mid-gesture: in the sidebar of the Photos app with "Memories" selected in blue, a thumbnail is displaced from the grid, over a gray folder icon with a small green plus badge, while the main area keeps "Edit" and "Select" at the top (sheet 0006, q0051).

- The coherence between code and screen is staged in the same frame: when the search tab line enters the code block, the device next to it comes to display the magnifying glass icon at the top of the sidebar (sheet 0005, q0044, q0045).
- The agenda slides work as a progress indicator, with the current topic in bold black and the other two in light gray, reappearing at the three block transitions (sheet 0003, q0025, q0026; sheet 0007, q0059; sheet 0010, q0085 to q0087).
- The platforms section repeats the same sidebar structure across three distinct mockups: a macOS window with the three colored dots in the title bar and sidebar on the left, carrying the same items from the iPad example app; a floating window with rounded corners and a translucent title bar over a 3D room with a sofa and bookshelf on visionOS, where what appears is a photos app, with the example app entering afterward in a second overlapping window; and a widescreen TV screen with the sidebar as a dark, semi-transparent list overlaid on the content, with no device frame (sheet 0010, q0088 to q0090; sheet 0011, q0091 to q0093).

Visual proportion: the eleven sheets are dominated by slides, mostly of code, alternating with captures of the example app inside device mockups, and the presenter appears in short segments and at section transitions; the transitions between slides are done in crossfade with the semitransparent devices in the background.

Recorded divergences or limits: the notes flag several consecutive frames with no perceptible change, which limits the reading of transitions in those stretches, for example the UIKit slide repeated between q0033 and q0034 and the pairs q0080 and q0081 and q0082 and q0083; on sheet 0003 an agenda frame is recorded with all three items in gray and none in bold.
