### What the images show
Basis: 14 of 14 frame sheets opened, all codes checked.
- The evolution of rendering modes is told through regular grids of symbols: first in black on light gray, then the grid erased with a single symbol in blue and, in the next frame, more symbols lighting up in blue, up to a full purple screen with six large white icons (sheet 0002, q0010 to q0016).
- The layer structure starts from the folder icon with a plus sign: a blue line sketch, in isometric perspective and with the tab highlighted from the base, turns into a solid purple folder repeated side by side under the label "By layer" (sheet 0003, q0019 to q0021).
- The concept of space gets its own diagram: diamond-shaped planes, semi-transparent and stacked, labeled Front and Back, with the same folder inside each plane, smaller and lighter from front to back (sheet 0003, q0026 and q0027; repeated in sheet 0004, q0028 and q0029).
- Replace's directionality uses the same diagram: the source symbol stays to the side and, inside the plane, the shape goes from small or blurred to larger, sharp and centered (a plus turning into a check, a cloud with sun turning into a cloud with rain), followed by the play and pause pair at large size (sheet 0009, q0074 to q0079).
- The script is marked by title frames: the first has a small gray label above a larger dark title, left aligned (sheet 0001, q0007); each preset opens with a single word in a strong color over a light background, sometimes with faded icons around it and, in the case of q0031, with the label next to the presenter, and the color changes by topic, purple in Appear, Bounce and Scale, magenta and pink in Pulse, Variable Color and Replace (sheets 0004 to 0008, q0031, q0036, q0037, q0043, q0056, q0062, q0070).
- The Bounce example is a real brightness slider, with a blue track, a white circular indicator and a small sun and large sun at the ends; between frames the indicator moves from the center and reaches the right end, and the blue fill comes to cover almost the whole bar (sheet 0005, q0041 and q0042).
- The Scale example is a toolbar from a files app (folder with plus, list with Select, search field with microphone) where a rounded light blue background jumps from no item to the folder and then to the list, without changing the other buttons; then, two layer icons labeled Bounce and Scale go from faded to saturated for comparison (sheet 0006, q0049 to q0054).
- The Pulse example is the screen sharing icon, whose rectangle alternates between solid pink fill and hollow outline, and then the same feature in a dark FaceTime call, with the screen share button on a white background between gray buttons and the end call button in red (sheet 0007, q0058 to q0061).
- Cumulative and Iterative appear as a pair of labeled wi-fi icons that gain saturation layer by layer over three frames; then wi-fi shows up in the macOS menu bar, between battery and search, first highlighted in pink and then monochrome like its neighbors (sheet 0008, q0064 to q0068).
- Contextual Replace is shown on a smartphone keyboard: with the word typed, the bar above the keys carries undo, redo and paste; with the word selected in yellow and circular handles at the ends, the bar switches to scissors, copy and paste (sheets 0009 and 0010, q0080 to q0082).
- The bear's drawing for animation is shown in vector steps in black stroke: loose points, a closed outline of head and body, overlapping dashed circles for the paws and, finally, the paws isolated without the body (sheet 0011, q0091 to q0094).
- The SF Symbols app appears in three columns: categories on the left, a large preview in the center with a thumbnail in the corner, a panel on the right with Rendering, Color, Background and Layers; between frames the list of layers annotated as Draw gains a line, the mode changes from Monochrome to Multicolor with the bear going from black to green, and a Custom opacity bar with a percentage shows up on a layer (sheet 0011, q0095 to q0099).
- The recommendation to draw with erase layers comes as a right-and-wrong pair: two identical bears labeled "With erase layers" and "Without erase layers", the first with a green check badge and, in the next frame, the second getting a red X (sheet 0012, q0103 and q0104); before that, three bears labeled by preset show the Scale one shrunk and the Appear and Disappear space empty (q0100 and q0101).
- The size of the library is communicated by category grids (automotive, gaming, weather) and by large numbers over a texture of hundreds of small icons, "700+" emerging subtly and then "5,000+" in sharp black that then fades, before the app icon with a BETA badge (sheet 0013, q0109 to q0116).
