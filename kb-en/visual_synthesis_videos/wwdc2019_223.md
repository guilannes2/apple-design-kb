### What the images show
Basis: 36 of 36 frame sheets viewed, codes checked.
- Apple builds a fixed geometric vocabulary for a sense that cannot be seen: a straight bar for transient, a rounded drop labeled "Round, Soft", a pointed triangle labeled "Crisp, Precise" and a wide block for continuous. The shapes appear loose at first (sheet 0003, q0027; sheet 0004, q0028 to q0031) and are only formalized later inside a cartesian plane with axes labeled from 0.0 to 1.0 for intensity and sharpness (sheet 0013, q0115 and q0116). The speech describes the concepts; the slide fixes the exact position of each pattern on the plane.
- The single linear axis with a circular marker and a real screen thumbnail is the central metaphor for the parameters: the same diagram is reused for HapticIntensity and for HapticSharpness, first empty and then with pole labels at the ends, and it gains two markers in distinct positions anchored to tappable examples, "Flashlight button" near the right end and "App Switcher" closer to the center (sheet 0025, q0217 to q0225). The relative position of the two examples on the axis exists only in the image.
- The diagram with two horizontal tracks, "Sound" on top and "Haptic" below, aligned with a label on the left, is the recurring device for showing temporal synchronization, and returns in sheets 0006, 0007, 0014, 0015 and 0016. A red vertical line marks the reference instant, and moving the haptic triangle to before the sound peak is what makes the concept of anticipation visible (sheet 0014, q0123 to q0125).
- The explanation of the ghost effect becomes a visual fact: four vertical blue bars labeled "4 Events", of slightly different heights, repeat without change from one frame to the next and, then, only the first one changes tone, turning gray (sheet 0015, q0128 to q0130). Then the same track diagram shows priming as a thin bar followed by a larger orange triangle (sheet 0015, q0132 to q0134).
- The contrast between navigation cues is communicated by density of elements, not by shape: the slide "Contrast, Left" has few bars in simple pairs and "Contrast, Right" accumulates many more bars on the same track, increasing from q0139 to q0140 (sheet 0016, q0137 to q0141).
- Color has a semantic function not stated in the speech: light blue for transient events and for the default haptic track, orange for continuous and for ramp and highlight events, green in the abstract sequence of the point that grows and shrinks (sheet 0009). It is a convention of the presentation material, not of the spoken text.
- The harmony principle is staged through mirrored layout: two iPhones in a realistic frame, with a notch and "9:41", labeled "Real World" and "Digital World"; the left column already comes with the three layers complete and the right one gains the items one by one, Visual, then Audio, and finally a fourth item, "Synchronization" (sheet 0008, q0064 to q0070).
- A real photograph of the mechanism serves as an argument of causality: the label "Digital Crown Haptic" appears at the top left over the image of the watch (sheet 0010, q0083 and q0084) and, in sequence, gold and silver gears with red rubies, in successive camera close-ups, lead to the 3D close-up of the serrated digital crown with a red ring (sheet 0010, q0086 to q0090).
- The slide navigation pattern is sequential highlighting, never a ready-made list: the active item in white or bold, future items in gray, and the list grows by accumulation instead of substitution, as in "Utility" receiving "Moderation", then "Focus", then "Keep it simple" (sheet 0012, q0105 to q0107), and likewise in "Dynamic Parameters" and "AHAP" in the second half (sheet 0031, q0274 to q0276; sheet 0032, q0283 to q0288).
- The API comparison is built column by column: the table "Choose the Right Haptics API" stays several frames with only the "UIFeedbackGenerator" column before the "Core Haptics" column appears with its three attributes (sheet 0020, q0174 to q0180). The progressive reveal is the technique, not the content of the table.
- Direct graphic annotation over a product photo: three frames in a row, iPhone, Apple Watch and iPhone with UIDatePicker, each with a thin green circle pointing to the exact component, home button, crown and picker (sheet 0021, q0185 to q0189). The speech cites the examples; the circle shows where to look.
- The playback flow is taught as a living diagram: a numbered list growing on the left and, on the right, blocks colored by object type that change state with labels in parentheses, "(Running)", "(Stopped)", plus the green labels "Done?" and "Player is done", step by step from q0235 to q0243 and closing with the seventh step at q0244 (sheets 0027 and 0028).
- In the code, the editor shows the Swift file in blocks in the order they appear in the real file, and a semitransparent light blue rectangle highlights the line under discussion, with the visible excerpt advancing from one frame to the next (sheet 0029, q0254 to q0261; sheet 0030, q0262 to q0270).
- The AHAP is assembled key by key in JSON, with highlighting on the part just added and a one-line comment explaining that "EventDuration" is mandatory (sheet 0033, q0289 to q0295), and the same content is immediately translated into two stacked graphs of Intensity and Sharpness with a short blue bar at 0.0 and an orange block between 0.5 and 0.75 (sheet 0033, q0296 and q0297). The equivalence between the text file and the graphic form is established by the image.
- A layer of the specification appears only in the diagram: the hierarchical tree "AHAP Structure Summary", with boxes in a card-stack effect, exposes dynamic Parameter and ParameterCurve with ParameterCurveControlPoints, which had not been built in the code in the previous sheets (sheet 0034, q0299 and q0300).
Visual proportion: almost all the sheets record a slide, diagram, product screen or code; only sheet 0001 is exclusively presenter, audience and title, and there are isolated frames of the presenter alone interspersed between blocks (for example q0158, q0229, q0246, q0272, q0287, q0306).
Recorded divergences or limits: several labels appear cut off by the edge or by the camera and were noted as probable, "Hapti" for Haptics (sheet 0006, q0054), "laying" and "ges" for Playing and Messages (sheet 0011, q0092 and q0093), "...ore Haptics Lab" and "Core Haptic..." (sheet 0018, q0155 and q0156); in sheet 0018 the item "Our first haptics" does not appear highlighted in any captured frame; in sheet 0026 there is illegible gray text between the two circles of the Palette app.
