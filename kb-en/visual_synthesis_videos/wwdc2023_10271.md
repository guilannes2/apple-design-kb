### What the images show
Basis: 12 of 12 frame sheets viewed, all codes checked.

- Abstract sound concepts are drawn as a mockup: a light gray monochrome 3D rendering of a domestic room, with the human figure and the headset in solid black and a bright blue sphere as the only colored element, marking the position of a sound source in space (sheet 0001, q0009).
- Sound propagation gets a fixed notation, white concentric rings with a gradient from the center outward, used first coming out of a point on the floor of a living room (sheet 0002, q0016) and then repeated at several points in the same room to indicate simultaneous sources (sheet 0011, q0093).
- The contrast between two states of the same sound is shown as a graph: a horizontal waveform split down the middle, the left half in light gray with low, regular bars and the right in black with tall, irregular bars (sheet 0002, q0014).
- The spatial keyboard appears in constructive detail: round keys with a translucent glass effect and slight relief, QWERTY layout, word suggestion bar above and elongated space bar, floating at an angle in the air (sheet 0003, q0021 and q0026). In one of the frames two real hands touch the keys, with text already typed in the field above (q0022).
- The floating screens follow a common pattern of a translucent panel with rounded corners, a date and location header in white text on a dark background and a grid of thumbnails inside the card (sheet 0003, q0027, and sheet 0004). In sheet 0003 there is also a vertical bar of icons to the left of the scene, with one of them highlighted as the active selection.
- The dimensionality transition that the speech mentions appears in successive states: a distant panel with two albums, then the album opened into a panoramic photo with an expand button and a row of thumbnails below, then the same photo taking up almost the whole frame with the edges curving inward, then with no panel visible (sheet 0004, q0028 to q0032).
- Navigation details of the photos app appear only on screen: the horizontal scope bar with years, months, days and all photos below the panel, and a vertical single-photo card overlapping the album panel as if it were being opened on top (sheet 0005, q0038 and q0039).
- The duality of a light and dark version of the same place is shown as a photographic pair: the same composition of a lake with a snowy mountain, first in late afternoon light and then at night, with a dark sky and the mountain in silhouette (sheet 0005, q0043 to q0044).
- The capture process is documented with real field photography, not rendering: a person in silhouette operating a tripod microphone by the lake under mist and dawn light, and then a close-up of a hand holding a portable recorder with several rotary buttons, photographed with shallow depth of field (sheet 0007, q0056 to q0061).
- The mixing is annotated with emojis: first a row of frog, cricket and eagle on a white background, with two blowing faces added (sheet 0008, q0067 and q0068), and then two frog emojis positioned at the left and right margins of the night photo of the lake, to indicate where each sound comes from (q0071 to q0072).
- The construction of sound layers is shown through variation in quantity, grouping and scale of the same emojis: two frogs at the ends, then three grouped on the left plus one isolated on the right, then the same configuration with the right frog much larger, suggesting approach (sheet 0009, q0074 to q0077).
- A small mixer widget with a green vertical fader and markings appears overlaid on the corner of the night photograph, the only audio control element visible in the video (sheet 0008, q0070).
- The best-practices slide is cumulative, with the same typographic pattern as the other lists: first only randomization, then positioning added below (sheet 0010, q0083 to q0084).
- The example outside the fully immersive environment appears as a 3D scene of a meeting room with niche shelves, with the word "hello" drawn as a continuous light trace in handwritten style floating over the table, and then the same room with speakers embedded in the niches and several sound rings distributed (sheet 0010, q0089, and sheet 0011, q0092 to q0094).
- The agenda is overlaid on the presenter's real setting instead of becoming an isolated slide, with the current item in bold black and the rest in gray, reappearing at two points in the video (sheet 0003, q0020, and sheet 0011, q0097).

Visual proportion: according to the notes, the presenter appears in a good portion of the frames of almost every sheet, always in the same setting, interspersed with 3D renderings, landscape and equipment photographs, spatial screens of the keyboard and the photos app and a few list slides; there are no code blocks or editing tool demonstrations besides the small mixer widget.

Recorded divergences or limits: the notes point to frames with no usable image (q0008 fully black and q0066 fully white), a frame practically repeated due to the passage of time (q0046 to q0047) and uncertain readings: in the photos app scope bar the active option is not distinguishable in that frame (sheet 0005), and the mixer widget labels are read as apparent channel controls, without confirmation (sheet 0008). Sheet 0007 has no interface element besides the photo displayed full screen on the physical iPhone.
