### What the images show
Basis: 41 of 41 frame sheets viewed, all codes checked.

- The piece uses a standardized credit card to identify several of the apps: product name in large white typography over the scene and, in small text in the bottom left corner, the creator's name and city (sheet 0014, "NAVI", Amsterdam; sheet 0015, "MD CLOCK", Hangzhou; sheet 0017, "SLOPES", Boulder). The speech does not name these apps in these passages, only the screen identifies which product is being shown (sheets 0022, 0024, 0028, 0029 and 0033 repeat the same credit pattern).
- The prototype and product pair appears within a single frame: in q0124 the left half brings a hand-drawn wireframe with arrows labeling each element (the sender's name, where captions come in, leave the session, participants) and the right half brings the real iPhone capture with the live caption in use and the keyboard visible (sheet 0014, q0123 and q0124).
- Production style guide boards appear with named sections and reference thumbnails: one on art direction with columns of small text about rules for branches, shadow and texture (sheet 0009, q0080 and q0081) and one on character divided into shape, color, ethnicity and facial details (sheet 0010, q0082).
- A body-tracking prototype storyboard shows nine numbered sketches in a grid, with red markers and yellow stars at the capture points and the last one labeled as ready (sheet 0019, q0163). In the following frames the same interviewee appears standing performing the movements, with a neon hand icon persisting in the corner until q0166.
- The tool panels of the creative apps reveal the density of controls behind the idea of simplicity that the speech treats in the abstract: fixed footer bar with named gradient chips, color wheel, crop modal panel and sliders per color channel and per tonal range in Procreate (sheet 0023, q0202 to q0205); undo, redo, screenshot, numeric resolution field and file path in the isometric house builder (sheet 0022, q0194 and q0195); tools stacked on the left and named layer cards on the right in the vector editor (sheet 0025, q0221 to q0224).
- The difference between consecutive frames works as a demonstration of effect: under the same gradient library bar, the mountain landscape switches from a magenta and purple palette to petrol blue and green while keeping the composition identical (sheet 0023, q0203 to q0205); the same vector drawing goes from the filled colored version to the black outline under construction (sheet 0025, q0222 to q0224); an empty outline cube gains a yellow articulated object inside it (sheet 0027, q0241 and q0242).
- Internal test tool overlaid on the final art: a monospaced text box in the corner lists speed parameters, drawing mode and coordinates over the forest scene, with a mission label next to it, which is development interface and not the screen delivered to the player (sheet 0029, q0255, absent in q0254).
- Mobile game HUD with fixed positions: circular skill icons grouped in the bottom right corner, translucent circular minimap in the top left corner and mission box in the top right corner (sheet 0029, q0258 to q0261). Between q0265 and q0266 the yellow damage numbers go from a single value to an overlapping stack, showing accumulation of hits (sheet 0030).
- Information hierarchy by color and position in the transit app: line number and direction in large white text over blue at the top, a stop change warning in a yellow box just below, and alternative options in cards colored by service with the estimated time inside a circle on the right (sheet 0032, q0283 to q0285). At another moment the same screen family brings a large line number and a real-time crowding warning (sheet 0012, q0104 and q0105).
- Regular grids are the standard for showing variation and progression: walls of square cards with a character and an illustrated icon or face (sheets 0002, 0008, 0018, 0036 and 0038), a row of six characters where only the cape and mask color changes per unit (sheet 0010, q0088), and horizontal illustrated stripes with large characters serving as a learning progress map (sheet 0018, q0158 to q0160).
- Reward and habit-state screens appear cropped from the apps: cup with liquid level and volume below plus an add button (sheet 0015, q0127), character divided into color stripes with four drink icons and a challenge counter (q0128), and short-praise cards with confetti and a neon outline heart (sheet 0018, q0162).
- The physical research material is filmed as evidence of process: a still-life collage of old clocks and alarm clocks next to an insert of the interviewee (sheet 0016, q0138), a pegboard of old cameras (sheet 0006, q0053), an open watercolor notebook with palette and brush (sheet 0009, q0078) and whiteboards with hand-drawn box-and-arrow diagrams (sheet 0006, q0052; sheet 0011, q0096), plus a third whiteboard with arrows connecting shapes, noted in the notes as illegible (sheet 0038, q0338).
- The final award block uses a fixed template filled in progressively: category title, winners subtitle and columns labeled game and app, each with a rounded-corner icon and the name below. Between q0346 and q0347 a second column is added to the category, the same happens between q0350 and q0351, and between q0355 and q0356 the next category receives both winning icons at once (sheets 0039 and 0040). The close condenses everything into a mosaic of twelve icons in a four-by-three grid, with no names (sheet 0041, q0364).
- The repertoire of transitions is broad and never commented on in the speech: fragmentation of the frame into gray geometric blocks with slices of the image inside (sheet 0013, q0111 and q0112), circular border cropping the frame (sheets 0007 and 0008), irregular border of purple lines whose outline changes between q0098 and q0099, indicating animation (sheet 0011), lateral sliding revealing the next scene (sheet 0008, q0071 to q0072) and an abrupt switch from a real background to green and turquoise texture with the person in the same pose, suggesting chroma key compositing (sheet 0037, q0329 to q0330).
- The dominant composition places the interviewee and the interface in the same frame, half face and half screen or full-screen art with the portrait cropped on top, instead of showing the interface in isolation (sheets 0005, 0010, 0022, 0023, 0025 and 0026).

Visual proportion: most of the video is the interviewee in a domestic or studio setting, with product screens entering as overlaid crops or short inserts; entire sheets have no software interface at all (0034, 0036, 0037 and 0038), while the three final sheets (0039 to 0041) are only result slides with no speech.

Recorded divergences or limits: the notes flag uncertain readings, such as the error bubble over animal illustrations that could be feedback or a learning app (sheet 0011), the gems-on-clipboard scene described as a possible game or creative app (sheet 0026), the giant letters cut off by the edges that seem to spell out a larger phrase without the frames allowing confirmation (sheet 0040), and the planning whiteboard whose scribbles are not legible (sheet 0038, q0338).
