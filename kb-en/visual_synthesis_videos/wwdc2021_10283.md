### What the images show
Basis: 17 of 17 frame sheets viewed, all codes checked.

- Each of the five principles gets a fixed graphic identity, a thin outline icon in its own color with a caption below, green star, red stack, two blue balloons, orange-framed "T" and purple binoculars, first lined up and then isolated with their rules stacked when the theme is explored in depth (sheet 0004, q0028 and q0029; sheet 0006, q0051 and q0052; sheet 0010, q0085 and q0086; sheet 0013, q0113; sheet 0015, q0132 and q0133; sheet 0016, q0143). The notes record that this visual structure is not stated in the speech, which only names the principles.
- The most repeated component of the talk is the action summary block: white rectangle with rounded corners, black text and each variable parameter inside a light blue pill, present from beginning to end (sheet 0002, q0013; sheet 0004, q0033 to q0035; sheets 0007 to 0015). It is the image that gives concrete form to the "parameter summary" addressed in the speech.
- A fixed pair of evaluation badges stays attached to almost every example, a green check for the recommended one and a yellow alert or gray X for the discouraged one, always in the upper corner of the evaluated screen or centered below the block (sheet 0004, q0033 to q0035; sheet 0006, q0048 to q0050; sheet 0007, q0056 to q0060; sheet 0011, q0091 to q0093; sheet 0014, q0123 to q0126).
- Modularity is staged with colored building blocks that snap together to form shortcuts, gray, red, green and orange, next to the corresponding action blocks (sheet 0002, q0010 to q0016; sheet 0007, q0056 and q0057).
- The anatomy of the shortcut card is visible: gradient background, well-rounded corners, white title in the bottom left corner, action count in gray below the title and an ellipsis menu in the top right corner, then brought together in the two-column grid of the "All Shortcuts" screen on iPhone and reduced to a compact vertical list on Apple Watch (sheet 0002, q0012 to q0018).
- The translucent material appears at several entry points in the system: the action list in the Mac menu bar with a frosted glass background, the white suggestion cards stacked over the blurred wallpaper of the lock screen, and the Siri confirmation balloon with a black question and two unfilled blue text buttons (sheet 0003, q0019 to q0022).
- Real system screens appear with a density of detail that the speech doesn't describe, for example the Reminders editing screen with each field on its own line separated by a thin divider, a colored icon on the left and a toggle or arrow on the right, with Cancel and Done at the top (sheet 0004, q0031 and q0032).
- The immersion criterion is judged on screen: the slide about customizing a watch face accumulates lines of text frame by frame next to the rich Apple Watch editing screen, and in the last frame an X appears in the corner that wasn't there before; next comes the slide about switching watch faces next to the simple screen listing already configured watch faces (sheet 0005, q0040 to q0044).
- An action's outputs get their own diagram: fan-shaped lines come out of the base of the get-tasks block toward small property icons, and across the frames both the property highlighted in green and the second block below change, going from archive to show notes to save attachment (sheet 0009, q0073 to q0076), with the real variable menu screen showing the property marked with a check (q0077).
- The recommended action types are revealed by accumulation, one label and one example block at a time, until the list is complete (sheet 0009, q0079 to q0081; sheet 0010, q0082 and q0083). The same progressive build device appears in the definition slides and in the principle icons.
- The comparison between touch and voice is done with the same scene twice: the calendar screen next to a HomePod mini with the corresponding phrase alongside. When the text is a question, the badge is a green check; when the label becomes a colon, the text on the calendar changes along with it and the badge switches to a gray X (sheet 0011, q0091 to q0093).
- Dynamic enumeration appears in the two moments of the same parameter: the device drop-down menu in the Mac editor, with a specific icon per device type and the variable and ask-each-time options, and then the voice resolution, with the task selection screen next to the Siri card asking which one to archive (sheet 0012, q0100 to q0107).
- Diagram annotation over a real interface: on iPad, a line connects the parameter summary label to the body of the action block and a vertical bracket groups the list of next-action suggestions under the action titles label, showing where each term applies on the screen (sheet 0014, q0119 to q0121).
- The summary writing rules appear as side-by-side pairs with a badge below, starting with a verb versus starting with the number, and with no punctuation or optional parameter, with the optional parameter reappearing, in the approved version, in a collapsed field below the block (sheet 0014, q0123 to q0126).
- The three states of a parameter are shown in the same block shape with an explanatory caption below: with no value, with the question spoken by the HomePod alongside; with a default value, with the speech already moving on; and with the ask-every-time option, with the numeric keypad and the question on the iPhone screen (sheet 0015, q0127 to q0129).
- The coffee order appears in two distinct snippets: the review card with store, item, options, subtotal, fee, total and change and confirm buttons, and then the success screen in solid green with large typography (sheet 0013, q0109 to q0111), plus the snippet with a shortcut header, a body with parameters in blue pills and a link to expand (q0114 and q0115).
- Discoverability is shown as a contrast between two screens of the same structure, phrase in quotes at the center, Siri icon above, explanatory text and main blue button at the bottom: one already set up, with a done button and a green "NEW" badge, and the other still asking to record the phrase, marked with a yellow alert (sheet 0016, q0138 to q0140).

Visual proportion: according to the notes, most of the frames are taken up by product screens, action blocks and slides, to the point that sheet 0002 is described as a sequence made up only of animated graphic elements with no presenter, while the presenters alone appear in short transition blocks between sections (sheet 0006, q0053 and q0054; sheet 0015, q0130 and q0131; sheet 0016, q0141 to q0144) and the video closes with two frames of the closing screen (sheet 0017).

Recorded divergences or limits: the notes flag frames with limited legibility, one almost entirely dark with only two faintly visible blocks (sheet 0008, q0065), a screen that is almost blank with a faint outline (sheet 0003, q0025) and a small, illegible list under the run-from-interface icon (sheet 0010, q0087). They also record readings marked as interpretation, the case of the particle explosion between the two app icons, described as some kind of transition or highlight effect (sheet 0008, q0070).
