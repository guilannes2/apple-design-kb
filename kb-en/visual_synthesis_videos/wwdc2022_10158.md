### What the images show
Basis: 14 of 14 frame sheets viewed, all codes checked.

- The session is structured by transition cards that name the blocks with two lines, a title faded in light gray above and a larger white subtitle below, sans-serif typography left-aligned over a solid dark background (sheet 0001, q0006 to q0009).
- The SF Symbols app's inspection panel is reused throughout almost the entire demonstration, always in the same vertical order of sections, rendering control at the top, color sample, background, and, when applicable, the layer list (sheets 0002, 0003, and 0007 to 0011). In the library navigation sheets, the symbol grid occupies the center and the selected cell appears with a blue outline (sheets 0002 and 0003).
- The mode selector is shown open, with the five dropdown menu items visible and one of them checked, overlaid on the symbol grid; in the next frame the menu closes and the four mode buttons, previously colored circles, switch to showing an arrow icon inside squares, which the notes read with a caveat as the selected symbol replicated in each mode (sheet 0002, q0015 to q0017).
- The effect of the percentage control is shown as a difference between frames on the same symbol: the speaker's wave goes from one bar to two while the percentage field in the right panel goes from 0% to 51% (sheet 0003, q0022 to q0023). Right after, a comparison card lines up the same symbol in the four modes, with black, gray, solid blue, and vibrant pink outlines side by side (q0026 to q0027).
- The didactic component that explains the thresholds is a segmented horizontal bar with numeric marks and a small indicator dot, always accompanied by an icon that lights up in parts: on the microphone, four vertical points that go from dark blue to light blue as the dot advances from 0% to the final range, and turn off again when it returns to zero, closing a demonstration cycle (sheet 0004, q0031 to q0036).
- The rounding case gets its own version of the same scale, now with the thresholds falling at 34% and 68% instead of round numbers, and an icon of three stacked layers lighting up from bottom to top as the value rises (sheet 0005, q0039 to q0041).
- The combinatorial explosion of variants is shown in three image stages: an isolated symbol, then four circles representing the rendering modes, and finally a grid of 27 identical icons in three rows of nine, illustrating weights crossed with scales (sheet 0006, q0046 to q0050).
- The annotation flow begins with the file being dragged into the app, and the change between frames records the import through the library counter going from no symbol to one, with the layer list gaining its first entry (sheet 0007, q0061 to q0063).
- The custom symbol's design is deliberately stylized, a solid black cube with a grid pattern on its faces, omitting details of the real object so it survives at small sizes (sheet 0007).
- The layer list visibly evolves as the mode changes, from a single entry to a different entry in monochrome, then to three entries named by hierarchical level with distinct texture thumbnails, and finally to three repeated entries with the same name in blue, each row with a square thumbnail, name, percentage field, and action icons on the right (sheet 0008, q0064 to q0071). The color names in the layer list only appear on the following sheet.
- In color mode, the front face appears sliced into one layer per square, with several rows of the same name stacked in the panel, and one frame shows several of these rows selected together in a blue block; when the control is moved to 41%, part of the squares turn light pink and part remain vivid red (sheet 0009, q0073 to q0080).
- Contrast problem shown as a concrete flaw: the version of the symbol inside a circle appears in monochrome as a black circle almost without detail, with the cube practically invisible, next to a comparison between the outline-only version and the solid filled version (sheet 0010, q0085 to q0089).
- The fix appears in the next frame of the same demonstration, with the cube in sharp white over the black circle and two eraser-type layers in the list, identified by a hatched texture thumbnail and shown in multiple selection (sheet 0011, q0091 to q0093).
- The summary cards use a constant reveal pattern: fixed title, green circular new badge in the upper right corner in most frames, and bullets that accumulate one per frame, sometimes restarting the list when the subtopic changes and swapping the title to announce the new template version (sheet 0011, q0097 to q0099; sheet 0012, q0100 to q0106).
- The closing contrasts two interface solutions for the same data, a screen with a large time number and an explanatory sentence next to the colored symbol, shown afterward fading and finally only with the symbol, without the text (sheet 0013, q0110 to q0113).

Visual proportion: by the notes, most of the frames are a capture of the SF Symbols app, an explanatory illustration on a black background, or a text card, with the presenter interspersed between the blocks, and only sheet 0014 is exclusively the closing card.

Recorded divergences or limits: the notes themselves flag uncertain or corrected readings, in sheet 0002 the four rendering buttons are described with a caveat ("they all seem to replicate the selected symbol"), in sheet 0005 the dot's position near 0% coexists with a layer already lit, with no recorded explanation, and in sheet 0013 the description corrects itself midway, pointing out that the text attributed to q0110 does not actually appear in that frame. In sheet 0008 the notes disagree about which mode the three repeated entries with the same name appear in, attributed to Palette in the interface construction line and to Multicolor in the change-between-frames line.
