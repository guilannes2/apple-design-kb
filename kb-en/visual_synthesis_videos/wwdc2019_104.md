### What the images show
Basis: 52 of 52 frame sheets viewed, all codes checked.

- The card system used throughout the ceremony is a single one and repeats from beginning to end: absolute black background, one word or phrase at a time in white, sans serif, large and centered, with no box or frame. The first title appears on sheet 0001 (q0009) and the same format returns with each new award, with "ELOH" on sheet 0032, "Butterfly iQ" on sheet 0036 and "HomeCourt" on sheet 0045. From sheet 0002 to sheet 0007 this background is accompanied by a row of luminous pedestals on the stage floor, which functions as a depth grid and as a path along which the presenters walk, until it turns into the row of dashed rectangles in q0062.
- Color functions as a category code, not as ornament: "Design Matters" and "Celebrate Excellence" appear in solid white, while "Technology" appears in pink neon outline and "Innovation" in blue neon outline (sheets 0003 and 0004, q0020 to q0034). The notes record that the narration does not mention any color at all in this section; the color differentiation exists only on screen.
- There is a progress indicator built over the same stage grid: first a row of eight rounded-corner rectangles with a dashed outline, empty (sheet 0007, q0062), then the same row receiving the icon of each awarded app, with five icons on sheet 0032 (q0283), six on sheet 0036 (q0319), seven on sheet 0040 (q0352), eight on sheet 0045 (q0399) and nine at the close (sheet 0050, q0447). It is a list that fills in, and the narration barely comments on it: the notes only record the narration returning to the list of previous winners on sheet 0040, with the icon scoreboard on screen.
- The interface never appears on its own: every device is shown in a real frame or in a mockup with a visible notch, resting on a pedestal or tilted in perspective, taking up almost the entire height of the frame (sheet 0006, q0046 to q0051, with the iPad showing a grid of app icons, the MacBook showing the desktop and the iPhone showing the "Celebrating the ADA Winners" card; then sheets 0019, 0020 and 0022 to 0025).
- Complete anatomy of a drawing app on iPad: three-column layout, with a color indicator on the narrow bar on the left, a blank drawing area in the center and a right column of stacked tools with an add button at the bottom, plus a top bar with back, grid, history, undo and redo (sheet 0013, q0112 to q0117). The color picker is a cross, vertical axis of tones and horizontal axis of dots, with a pen-shaped bar crossing the center and showing the name of the chosen color, which changes from "Dover Blue" to "Gates Blue" between two frames (sheet 0014, q0125 to q0127).
- The sequence of frames documents a drawing being built in discrete stages that the narration only describes in passing: circle in a thin stroke, then the same circle with shading texture, then the handwritten word "Pen." below it (sheet 0013, q0114 to q0117); and further on an isolated curved line, complete outline of the nib, forked barrel, blue stain at the tip and final signature (sheets 0015 and 0016, q0132 to q0136).
- Photo adjustment panel treated as a modular, layered system: fixed RGB histogram at the top, named sections (White Balance, Hue and Saturation, Lightness) each with its own toggle and sliders with a visible numeric value (sheets 0027 and 0029), with the "ML" label on these toggles recorded on sheet 0029. The change between frames proves the manipulation: Temperature goes from 9% to 15% and Tint from 5% to 8% (sheet 0027, q0242 to q0243), and the panel goes deeper into Highlights, Shadows, Brightness, Contrast, Black Point and Color Balance with "Color Wheels" and "3-Way Color" tabs (sheet 0029, q0259 to q0260) and, further on, to a "Selective Color" section with a "Master" color wheel (sheet 0031, q0272).
- Before-and-after pair recorded only by a label: the same photo appears with the "ORIGINAL" tag in the upper left corner and, in the next frame, without the tag and with visibly different color and light (sheet 0030, q0262 to q0264). This is all the visual evidence of the comparison that the narration describes as a long press on the image.
- Selected state marked by a highlight color: the crop bar shows a row of aspect ratio icons (square, 4:5, 16:9, 4:3, 5:4, 3:2, Custom) with the chosen one highlighted in orange, plus a dedicated "ML Crop" button; between q0249 and q0251 the photo goes from rectangular to square, confirming the applied crop (sheet 0028).
- Button hierarchy on a detail screen: secondary action "UPGRADES" in neutral gray against primary action "PLAY" in lime yellow and with a larger area, next to stat bars and a green "TOUCHDRIVE ON" toggle (sheet 0022, q0197). In the next frame the entire interface disappears and all that is left is the car in a rotating showroom, a product shot with no UI at all (q0198).
- A HUD that does not move while the scenery changes completely: speedometer, timer, top yellow bar and action icons keep an identical position across a night street, tunnel, wet highway and a red taillight area (sheet 0024), with the mini-map noted in the upper left corner already on sheet 0023. Speed varies from frame to frame (141, 179, 114, 118, 107, 130, 136, 134, 119 mph) and ephemeral achievement texts ("PERFECT NITRO", "KNOCKDOWN", "DRIFT", "AIR TIME") appear and disappear without shifting anything in the layout (sheets 0023 and 0024, q0199 to q0216).
- Clinical app in dark mode with explicit state hierarchy: black background, ultrasound image in the center, numbered depth ruler on the right, fixed bar of line icons at the bottom with the record button in red, the only colored one among the rest in white (sheet 0037, q0331). In the capture flow, the screen gathers the legal notice and the framing instruction at the top, and the "Not Measurable" quality indicator in red turns into a "0:03" timer with a white bar in the next frame (sheet 0038, q0335 to q0336); during measurement, editable blue anchor points over the ventricle outline change position between frames, with capsule-shaped "Deselect" and "End Diastolic Frame" buttons and a filmstrip of frame thumbnails at the bottom (q0337 to q0339).
- Full-screen color used as a progression signal: in Thumper the entire scene shifts hue between frames (red and orange, blue and purple, almost white with silver floral shapes, gold, light pink) always keeping the same central vanishing point and the character at the base of the composition (sheets 0041 to 0043, q0361 to q0385). The HUD is minimal and frameless: circular count badges, a "6,800" scoreboard and a "LEVEL 4-4" label in thin white typography in the corners. Between q0376 and q0377 the same device appears rotated from portrait to landscape with the central composition preserved.
- Interface over live video instead of a traditional app screen: in HomeCourt the initial screen is light, with a red "Start" button and a list of exercises with video thumbnails, but during capture, nearly full-screen colored cards appear ("Warm up!" in orange, "Dribble to start" in black), a countdown, a timer in one corner and a rep counter in the opposite one, and a summary with the large number "18" over a grid of four metrics and three action buttons (sheet 0046, q0406 to q0413). In two-player mode, body-tracking rectangles colored per player appear over the video, with a framing warning on a red card, then a screen split in half with its own timer and score on each half, going from "00:15" with a zeroed score to "24 24" and "64 63" (sheets 0048 and 0049, q0427 to q0434).
- Spatial data drawn instead of tabulated: the shot map uses a simplified half-court diagram with red dots for misses and orange for makes marking the position of each attempt, with an embedded video player at the top of the same screen, plus the "12/25" stat and the user's name (sheet 0047, q0418 to q0419).
- Each studio's credit is mostly visual: behind-the-scenes collages with hand-drawn sketches on paper, wireframes and grid thumbnails in a video-call style showing people working in physically different places (sheet 0021, q0182; sheet 0026, q0227; sheet 0035, q0308 and q0313), plus a photo of the team's real office (sheet 0025, q0225), added to a "lower third" caption with the app icon and the team's name (sheets 0031, 0035 and 0036, q0275, q0315 and q0316). When there is text, it comes on an attribution card with the app icon next to the app name and the studio name (sheets 0039, 0044 and 0049).

Visual proportion: of the 52 sheets, about 34 show app or game screens on a device or mockup, and the other 18 are stage, audience, title cards, behind-the-scenes collages and trophy presentation, meaning most of the video shows interface, even though it is a ceremony.

Recorded divergences or limits: on sheet 0010 the notes point out that the speech praises the accessibility mode for color blindness and the iPad game with iCloud sync, but the screen shows only the gameplay in the green universe, without showing either one. On sheet 0002, q0016 catches the text "Appearance and Func" cut off mid entrance animation, and only q0017 shows the full phrase. On sheet 0038 the notes contradict each other about the order of the elements at the top of the capture screen, sometimes with the instruction above the legal notice, sometimes the opposite, so here it is only recorded that both stay at the top. Sheet 0052 records that there is no speech in the interval, only the Apple logo in a thin outline between the two presenters.
