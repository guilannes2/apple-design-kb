### What the images show
Basis: 35 of 35 frame sheets viewed, all codes checked.
- The opening is built entirely with typography: a title card with a hexagonal frame and a gradient border in purple, yellow and cyan over a dark background, the same uppercase lettering inverting from black on white to white on black (sheet 0001, q0003 and q0004), abstract curved shapes serving as a transition (q0005 and q0006) and the same title overlaid on three completely different scenes of people (q0007 to q0009). The title card appears in the sheet's description with no frame code recorded.
- A pair of draft and final result shown explicitly: a hand-drawn wireframe with arrows pointing to each area of the screen (video frame, caption strip, exit button, participant list) and, in the next frame, that same wireframe placed next to an iPhone mockup with the interface already implemented (sheet 0013, q0115 and q0116). An earlier wireframe, of rectangles drawn by hand on a sheet of paper, covers the face of an interviewee (sheet 0002, q0013).
- Art direction boards with technical annotations overlaid in semi-transparency on the footage, changing subject with every frame: a study of stylized trees, village architecture with red roofs and a character study with notes about shadow and facial details (sheet 0009, q0076 to q0078).
- App screens with visible anatomy: a cup that fills partially with the value "8.0oz" and a rounded "+ WATER" button below (sheet 0014, q0118); ski run metrics in stacked cards in a vertical list with a pause button highlighted (sheet 0016, q0141); a transcription app with a time counter at the top, text coming in in real time and a full keyboard at the bottom (sheet 0015, q0135); a Nixie-style digital clock display showing "09:41:07" in orange on black with a small date label (sheet 0015, q0129); a map app card with a large "55" number, a traffic warning line and an action button (sheet 0011, q0098).
- Authoring tools filmed in use: a side palette of brushes with texture names in a scrollable list in a painting app (sheet 0004, q0034); a photo editing app on iPad with a crop and resize window, color wheel, per-channel adjustment sliders and a swatch palette (sheet 0021, q0185), and right after that a different tool, with team avatars at the top and a "Gradient Library" panel of named gradients, where only the background image changes between the two frames and the panel stays (q0186 and q0187); a Unity-style 3D game editor with an object hierarchy panel on the left and the rendered garden on the right (sheet 0024, q0214), followed by two modeling stills in which a gray wireframe cube gives way to a more elaborate yellow structure (q0215 and q0216); a thumbnail gallery on the left with the large painting open on the right (sheet 0022, q0190 and q0191).
- The overlap between creator and product is the video's most repeated device, and it appears in both arrangements: app screens, game illustrations and icons float over the person or replace the background behind them while keeping the physical foreground objects (sheet 0010, q0083 and q0084; sheet 0014, q0118 and q0119; sheet 0018, q0155 and q0156); in the reverse arrangement the product occupies the full screen and the interviewee stays in a small window on top (sheet 0004, q0033, q0035 and q0036). The pairs with and without the layer expose the editing itself: the same scene loses the decorative wave frame and the starry background (sheet 0003, q0022 and q0023), the icons disappear and only the plain footage remains (sheet 0010, q0084 and q0085) and the video call insert disappears while the message bubble changes content (sheet 0004, q0028 and q0029).
- The frame split in half vertically, half creator footage and half illustrated game scene, instead of cutting to full screen; the illustrated half changes scene from one frame to the next (sheet 0008, q0071 and q0072).
- A communication interface used as a graphic element over the video: a white message bubble with rounded corners with a video call thumbnail in the top right corner (sheet 0004, q0028), and chat and call app icons, with a rounded outline and solid color, floating enlarged over the real scene (sheet 0010, q0083 and q0084).
- An instructions modal in a 3 by 3 grid, each cell with a figure in a numbered pose and a red contact point marker, ending in a "READY!" warning; in the next frame the same person performs the movement in the same environment (sheet 0017, q0150 and q0151). On the same sheet, reward messages appear in colored blocks overlaid on the character (q0149).
- A language learning grid as a physical object rather than a screen: a wall of cards with one Chinese character and one illustration per cell, alternating background colors, uniform layout, reappearing as a backdrop behind the same interviewee on several sheets (sheet 0006 q0048, sheet 0030 q0267, sheet 0033 q0293). Sheet 0017 shows the same wall again, with no frame code recorded for it.
- A title card per product, with the name in white uppercase and author and city in small text in the lower corner (sheet 0022 q0192, sheet 0023 q0201, sheet 0025 q0219 and q0225, sheet 0027 q0236, sheet 0028 q0244). The background is almost always a still from the material itself, but not always: in q0201 it is an aerial photo of escalators and in q0236 it is the creators themselves playing instruments. The notes record that this card is usually the only place where the product name appears written.
- Closing in kinetic typography: an "APPLE DESIGN AWARD WINNERS" card in bold aligned to the right across three lines, followed by giant letters cropped by the frame, each with the name of a category in small text inside it, covering the award's six categories (sheet 0034, q0300 to q0306); next to the cards, a dark card with the apple logo takes the place previously used by the interpreter.
- Final summary screen: a grid of 4 columns by 3 rows of app icons with rounded corners in the iOS pattern, under a small centered title and with no app name written, followed by a plain light frame that closes the piece (sheet 0035, q0308 and q0309).
- Transitions treated as a piece of design rather than a hard cut: the image breaks down into gray and green rectangular blocks and then recomposes whole (sheet 0012, q0104 to q0106), white bars appear on the sides narrowing the frame (q0107 and q0108), a white frame thickens as if the camera were pulling back inside a card (sheet 0013, q0110 and q0111), a circular vignette crops the face and swaps the background behind it (sheet 0007, q0059 and q0060) while smaller circles with product illustrations float over the scene (q0061 and q0062), and purple ink strokes accumulate over the recording until the person disappears from the frame (sheet 0011, q0092 to q0094).
- On sheets 0019 to 0035 the app captures almost always appear without a device frame, in a full-screen crop or in a collage; the recorded exceptions are the vertical blocks of captures from the maps and transit app and the photos of a hand holding the physical iPhone (sheet 0028 q0247 to q0250, sheet 0030 q0266). Before this section the notes record an iPhone mockup and a real device in hand (sheet 0013 q0116, sheet 0014 q0121, sheet 0016 q0141).
Visual proportion: no sheet shows a presenter on stage with slides; the 35 sheets alternate testimonials filmed in the creators' real workspaces with product screens, illustrations, title cards and tool captures, and the last two sheets are only typography and an icon grid. Starting on sheet 0019 the notes record an ASL interpreter in a white frame next to the image.
Recorded divergences or limits: on sheet 0034 the notes point out that the large letter of each card generally does not match the small word written inside it, because the frames were extracted at different instants of the animation; the case of "US" with the subtitle "DELIGHT AND FUN" is noted as a fragment of a larger word, without certainty of which one. On sheet 0011 the reading of the purple streaks as a metaphor for the reported failure is marked as a possibility, not as a confirmed observation. The frame of the ASL interpreter only appears recorded in the notes starting from sheet 0019.
