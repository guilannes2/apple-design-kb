### What the images show
Basis: 15 of 15 frame sheets viewed, all codes checked.

- A side summary works as a progress marker for each new principle: the names appear stacked and left aligned, the current principle in bold (black or white, depending on the background) and the ones already seen in faded gray (sheets 0004, 0006, 0008, 0010, 0012 and 0014). The list grows from two items on sheet 0004 to seven on sheet 0014, and the speech only announces the topic change out loud, without mentioning the feature.
- The progress list and the site index shown at the end record seven names (Purpose, Agency, Responsibility, Familiarity, Flexibility, Simplicity, Craft), with no entry of their own for the remaining principles mentioned in the speech (sheets 0004 to 0014, and the page's side panel on sheet 0015, q0132).
- Central lines of speech become solid pastel color cards with a short phrase in typographic emphasis: "Offer forgiveness." in green, left aligned (sheet 0003, q0021), "Let people personalize." in pink, left aligned (sheet 0010, q0085) and "Maintain your design over time." in blue, this time centered (sheet 0014). The same template changes alignment between occurrences.
- Each principle carries an identity color that the speech never names: orange for Familiarity (sheet 0007), coral red for Flexibility (sheets 0009 and 0010), purple for Simplicity (sheets 0011 and 0012) and blue for Craft (sheets 0013 and 0014). The "Purpose" title card appears in two versions, light background with black text (sheet 0001) and dark background with white text (sheet 0002).
- A slider labeled "Literal" on the left and "Abstract" on the right serves as a graphic device to explain the literalness spectrum of a metaphor, and reappears with the indicator in different positions, first near the center and then shifted toward the abstract side, inside a panel called "Inspector" with two alternative icons for the same function, one organic and one geometric (sheet 0007, q0057 to q0058).
- The historical origin of the metaphor is shown through a contrast in finish: a trash can in low resolution pixelated style over an orange background and, on the same sheet, three modern thin line symbols aligned with equal spacing, a star, an arrow coming out of a box and a padlock (sheet 0007, q0055 and q0061).
- Nearly identical pairs serve as a didactic comparison with no text label at all, only the visual difference: two phone screens with the same bottom bar of three icons, one with a highlighted content block and the other uniform, and two desktop windows with a three dot title bar in the upper left corner (sheet 0008, q0065 to q0066 and q0068 to q0069).
- The difference between consecutive frames demonstrates interaction states: a blue pill shaped button with the text "Continue" switches to showing a loading spinner in place of the text (sheet 0013, q0111 to q0112), and an empty purple modal fills with a direct confirmation sentence and an explicitly named action button (sheet 0011, q0093 to q0094).
- Also through the difference between frames, an isolated circular pause button gives way to a pill shaped control with play and a partially filled progress bar, that is, the control gains context instead of losing elements (sheet 0012, q0104 to q0105).
- The quality of finish is shown by comparing typography and color between consecutive frames: an "Aa" block and a color circle in a faded, weak version give way to the same elements in a sharp, saturated version over a blue background (sheet 0013, q0115 to q0116), followed by an app icon labeled "SDK" as the technical basis.
- Opacity is the visual code for hierarchy throughout the piece: a list of self check questions has the first two in black and the third in light gray (sheet 0005, q0040 and q0041); an opaque laptop appears next to a semi transparent phone (sheet 0009, q0079); and the summary items already seen always stay faded.
- Abstract concepts are represented only by simple geometric shapes, with no text: an isolated black silhouette of a person that multiplies into a cloud of dots with a light dot highlighted in the middle, over a neutral background (sheet 0001, q0005 to q0006), and a diagram of hollow circles in a ring around a solid white circle, over a green background (sheet 0002, q0018).
- The mock-ups evolve from generic to specific within the same sheet: a grid of unbranded app icons becomes a single icon with a pizza slice and then a complete delivery screen with a photo, a map and an action bar at the bottom, all in shades of yellow and with well rounded corners (sheet 0004, q0033 to q0036).
- The only real Apple product interface capture in the entire video is the documentation page at developer.apple.com, with a filter sidebar on the left, a grid of four principle cards (colored monochrome icon, name in bold and a summary sentence) and a navigation index on the right, interspersed with a single frame between shots of the presenters (sheet 0015, q0132).

Visual proportion: the 15 sheets continuously alternate the two presenters in studio with graphic cards and generic vector mock-ups; practically all the interface examples are abstract illustrations, and real product capture appears in a single frame of the entire video (sheet 0015, q0132).

Recorded divergences or limits: the notes record constant set design that the speech never mentions, two colored cards on the table that do not change position in any of the 15 sheets, plus plain white frames interspersed as scene cuts, with no content (sheets 0003, 0005, 0008, 0011 and 0014); solid black frames appear recorded only in sheet 0003, two in a row (q0024 to q0025).
