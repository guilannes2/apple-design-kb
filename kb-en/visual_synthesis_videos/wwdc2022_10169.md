- A five-step progress card reappears throughout the whole talk, always as a vertical list aligned to the left with the current item in white and bold and the rest in faded gray, and the list grows as the script advances, gaining new items relative to the previous version (sheet 0002, q0016; sheet 0004, q0034; sheet 0009, q0074; sheet 0013, q0110 and q0111; sheet 0015, q0135).
