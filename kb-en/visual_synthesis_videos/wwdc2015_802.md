### What the images show
Basis: 40 of 40 frame sheets viewed, all codes checked.

- The title cards work as a summary that grows along with the talk, with the item under discussion in full white and the ones already covered dimmed in gray (sheets 0004, 0006, 0008, 0012, 0013, 0014 and 0017); the timestamped case is sheet 0013, q0113 to q0116, when "Not for critical-path actions" comes in. This opacity feature is not mentioned in the talk. In other cards, the notes only record the accumulation of items, without pointing out an opacity contrast between the current item and the previous ones (sheets 0025, 0032, 0034 and 0039).
- Explicit before-and-after pair for a list reordered by use: side-by-side watch faces labeled "First Time" and "Second Time", where "Outdoor Run" moves to the top and gets a larger card with distance and date of the last workout, while the remaining options stay as single-row buttons (sheet 0008, q0064 to q0067).
- Graphic mark of a disapproved practice: the "Continue Reading on iPhone" dialog box appears with a red circled X drawn outside the watch, an annotation that marks the explicit handoff button as dispensable (sheet 0031, q0278).
- Redundancy demonstrated through literal repetition: the four cards on the Activity screen each appear with the same button to change the goal, and the two consecutive frames are practically identical, which turns the verbal argument into on-screen evidence (sheet 0012, q0100 to q0101).
- The haptics get their own reusable diagram: thick magenta bars, representing the Taptic Engine's pulses, synced to a thin blue audio wave over a black background, with the shape changing by category, a decreasing triangle in notification and direction, an irregular block in retry, two vertical bars marking start and end in stop, and a single narrow bar in click, this one repeated almost identically in q0179 and q0180 (sheets 0018, 0019 and 0020). The talk covers sound and sensation, not the drawing.
- The Digital Crown's click is explained through an annotated diagram of the watch face, with small and large tick marks around the rim, a red arrow entering from outside to indicate rotation and a white vertical line marking the trigger point; the pink rectangles labeled "Click" are a didactic legend, not real watch interface (sheet 0021, q0183 to q0188).
- The loss of clicks during fast rotation becomes a visible count: the markers go from five stacked in a cascade to just two well spaced apart, while the number in the center of the dial changes (sheet 0022, q0190 to q0191 and q0192).
- The Force Touch menu appears as buttons with icon and label, never as a simple text list: radial over a darkened background, reaching four buttons in a 2x2 grid in the stopwatch styles menu (sheet 0010, q0085 to q0087), or as three stacked rectangular buttons with the icon on the left over the open conversation (sheet 0034, q0302 to q0303). Critical path actions stay on the main screen, in large full-width, high-contrast buttons, lime green for both save and discard (sheet 0014).
- In voicemail, the render rotating in perspective reveals, as it settles, two secondary circular icons, a green phone and a red trash can, below the inline player, showing the coexistence between the likely action and visible secondary actions (sheet 0014, q0121 to q0123).
- The watchOS 2 Pickers appear in three concrete forms, a single card with an image in stack style and three scrollable date columns with green highlight on the active column and confirm and skip buttons (sheet 0015), and a short-label picker with a green outline over a grid of dimmed buttons (sheet 0017). The outline concept is isolated separately, in rectangular text boxes, one of them with a bright green outline, on the same sheet as the two watches with Pickers rotating in perspective (sheet 0016, q0137 to q0139).
- Statistic slides with a huge centered number and the word "Seconds" small below anchor the interaction time targets, with two values in consecutive frames and a third one further on, always in the same layout (sheet 0023, q0204 to q0205; sheet 0029, q0260).
- The same screen hierarchy repeats across unrelated apps, large value in the center or at the top, small label below and context in even smaller text: temperature on the weather Glance above city and condition (sheet 0024, q0210), recipe timer countdown changing between two frames (sheet 0029, q0253 to q0254), running distance (sheet 0032, q0283) and workout elapsed time, which even changes color between frames (sheet 0033, q0292 to q0295).
- The interactive notification separates the primary action from the secondary one by color: colored header with the app name in capitals, sender in bold and, below, the reply button in solid green against the dismiss one in gray (sheet 0027, q0241 and q0242).
- The watch frame appearing semi-transparent over the scene is the editing device used to introduce a screen (sheet 0024, q0209; sheet 0026, q0233) and also to stage the handoff, with the article on iPhone and the summary on the watch overlapping before the watch comes into sharp focus in the foreground (sheet 0030, q0264 and q0265). In the illustrated character's app, the device switch happens between frames of a side-by-side composition, with no overlap, leaving only the instructional text on the watch (sheet 0037, q0330 to q0331).
- Animatable properties and state change appear as a labeled demonstration: the same element called "Label" appears with the word "Height" written next to the device and then larger and in dark green with "Background color" in the same position (sheet 0038, q0337 to q0338), while in the 2x2 effects grid a pink circle turns into a solid pink square, marking active state through shape and not just color (sheet 0036, q0320 to q0321).
- The interface guidelines page appears as a window overlaid on a capture of the developer website, with a side navigation menu, a watch illustration with a 30-degree angle annotated and three screen thumbnails labeled by the talk's three themes (sheet 0040, q0352).

Visual proportion: the 40 sheets alternate frames of the presenter alone on stage with watch face screens, title cards and diagrams, and screen material appears on practically every sheet; the notes do not record any live on-device demo segment, the examples appear as stills, renders rotating in perspective and side-by-side compositions.

Recorded divergences or limits: the notes explicitly mark elements that are annotation and not real interface, the pink rectangles of "Click" (sheet 0021), the yellow dotted line crossing the Glance screen, described as a video editing mark (sheet 0025, q0223), and the red X next to the handoff dialog (sheet 0031). Several pairs and sequences of frames are also recorded as having no perceptible change (sheets 0027 q0235 and q0236, 0028 q0250 and q0251, 0030 q0269 and q0270, 0036 q0316 and q0317, 0037 q0326 to q0329), and the second variation of the Activity screen is noted as apparently identical to the first (sheet 0012).
