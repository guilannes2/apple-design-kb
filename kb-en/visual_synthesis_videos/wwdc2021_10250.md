### What the images show
Basis: 19 of 19 frame sheets viewed, all codes checked.

- The screen carries the measurement that the speech only describes in words: over the folder icon with a plus sign, two blue vertical guide lines appear labeled with the margin and variant name, "left-margin Regular-M" and "right-margin Regular-M" (sheet 0003, q0024). In another frame of the same sheet, a block of technical text shows the 3.0 version of the template, the Xcode 13 requirement, the source symbol and the composition size in points (q0022).
- The same diagram device runs through almost the whole video: a comparison matrix with the letter "A" to the left of each row and the symbols aligned along the baseline to the right, sometimes with the title "Typographic alignment" overlaid and a focus circle on one of the icons (sheet 0002, q0014 and q0015; sheet 0003, q0021 and q0027; sheet 0009, q0079; sheet 0014, q0123 to q0125; sheet 0016, q0141 and q0142). The notes record that this device is not named in the speech, that is, it is the image that makes the alignment mechanism concrete.
- The foundation slides are black, with white text and very few elements: "Margins" shows only the symbol between vertical axis marks, "3 scales" shows the same symbol in three increasing sizes, and the next slide swaps the images for a list of system attributes with references to other sessions in the footer (sheet 0002, q0016 to q0018).
- The SF Symbols app always appears with the same three-column skeleton, categories on the left, symbol grid in the center, details panel on the right, with a blue frame marking the selected item and a separate "Custom Symbols" section at the bottom of the sidebar (sheet 0005, q0037 to q0045; sheet 0010, q0087 to q0089).
- The evolution of the file is visible sheet by sheet, not just narrated: the sidebar gains "custom.crown", then the item renamed to "queen.heart", then "queen.heart" and "teacup.circle" together (sheet 0005, q0040, q0042 and q0045), and the reimport flow appears with the standard macOS modal alert asking for confirmation to replace the template, with Cancel on the left and the blue action button on the right (sheet 0009, q0080 and q0081).
- The anatomy of the rendering panel is exposed: mode selector at the top with the four options in a dropdown menu, "Primary" color field with a percentage value, default background field and a "Layers" list in which each layer carries a color indicator, name and visibility and opacity controls (sheet 0011, q0091 to q0099). The system color menu is a vertical list with a square swatch next to each name and a check on the active option (q0094 to q0096).
- The vector drawing is shown in stages, with the anchor points as the mode indicator: crown silhouette with points at the tips, then the hollow shape closed by a straight central line, then the heart added while still being edited, then the final result in solid black with no visible points; the blue margin guides flank the drawing in three of these frames (sheet 0007, q0057, q0058, q0060 and q0063).
- The design app window appears from the inside: left panel with the variants named by weight and scale, from Ultralight to Black in sizes S, M and L, center area with the matrix of black crowns and right properties panel with fill, borders, shadows and blur (sheet 0006, q0054).
- The choice between a static and a variable setup is translated into a visible quantity: the point diagram goes from nine points per line, labeled static, to just three points per line, labeled variable, with the vertical guides repositioned (sheet 0006, q0049 to q0051).
- A fixed vocabulary of right and wrong organizes the construction rules: a red circle with an X for the practice to avoid and a green circle with a check for the recommended one, applied to live strokes versus converted fill, an open zigzag path, and a gradient versus a flat fill (sheet 0008, q0064 to q0069). In the editing of an already annotated symbol the trio of seals is different, a green circle with a dot, a green circle with a check and a yellow circle with an exclamation mark over the edited heart diagrams (sheet 0014, q0118 to q0120), and in the editing of an interpolable symbol the red circle and the green one with a check return (sheet 0017, q0146 to q0148).
- The path order problem gets a visual demonstration: the "Path count" and "Path order" slides show the two folder icons with a caption of points numbered 1 to 4 and, in the last frames, the icons filled in blue and green show internal outlines of different colors, a sign that the paths do not match between the two drawings (sheet 0009, q0073 to q0078).
- The interaction between layers gets an abstract diagram of three overlapping circles comparing the default behavior, which blends where there is overlap, with the mode that clears what is behind, and this diagram is overlaid on the icon itself before and after being isolated in full screen (sheet 0012, q0106 to q0108). In the previous frames the symbol goes through the corresponding layer states: olive crown with a red heart and reduced opacity in the panel, then the hierarchical version in shades of gray with the primary and secondary groups listed (q0100 to q0105).
- The difference between hierarchical and palette is built by accumulation: one circle with the icon in the center and callout lines labeled "Primary" and "Tertiary", then two circles, then three, each example with a row of swatches below indicating how many colors are in use (sheet 0013, q0112 to q0115), while the app panel in palette mode lists the named colors with a percentage value (q0110 and q0111).
- The interpolation requirements appear as three distinct screens: the three design sources labeled Ultralight-S, Regular-S and Black-S side by side, the pairs of crowns with each path in a different color and a caption numbered 1 to 9, and the red and blue heart outlines overlaid with thin lines connecting each point to its counterpart (sheet 0015, q0127 to q0135).
- The closing is decisive rather than descriptive: the template 2.0 and 3.0 slides list attributes next to the icon with the margin guides, and the distribution flowchart uses blue requirement boxes pointing to green template boxes, with a column labeled "iOS14+" that points to both templates and two others, "iOS15+" and the sharing one, that point only to 3.0 (sheet 0017, q0151 to q0153; sheet 0018, q0154 to q0159).

Visual proportion: according to the notes, the large majority of frames carry a slide, technical diagram or a capture of the SF Symbols app and the vector editor, and the presenter appears alone in a smaller portion. In part of these moments the chapter index stays in the background, marking the current item in white against the rest in gray (sheet 0001, q0004 to q0007; sheet 0003, q0020; sheet 0013, q0117; sheet 0014, q0122).

Recorded divergences or limits: the notes mark some readings as inference rather than certainty, for example the opening with silhouettes that change position described as "probable animated opening" (sheet 0001, q0001 and q0002) and the small context menu over the yellow layer, whose two icons are described as probably duplicate and remove (sheet 0011, q0098).
