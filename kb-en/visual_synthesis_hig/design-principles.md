### What the illustrations show
Basis: 2 illustration sheets viewed (2 of 2), all codes checked; no video listed for the page.
- The eight images form a rigorously uniform pictogram system: the same green gradient, from light to dark pulling toward teal, the same thick rounded stroke or solid fill, the same relative size and the same centered position at the left of the frame (img 0425 to 0432).
- No illustration carries text, caption, photograph or interface screen; each principle is communicated only by a single isolated pictogram (img 0425 to 0432).
- The opening of the page uses a target of three concentric circles on a white background, with the center darker and the edges lighter (img 0425).
- The first three principles are figured with people and shelter: a human figure with arms and legs open for Purpose (img 0426), two simplified figures with hands joined at the center for Agency (img 0427) and a house seen from the front with a door or square window cut out in white for Responsibility (img 0428).
- The following principles turn into objects and signs of common use: arrows crossed in four directions for Familiarity (img 0429), open scissors for Flexibility (img 0430), a technical drawing compass with a dry point and graphite for Simplicity (img 0431) and a solid heart, with no outline or internal detail, for Craft (img 0432).
- The scissors carry the same cut-out circles at the finger holes that appear in the other compositions (img 0430).
- The sheets carry only the light version of each illustration; there is no variant for dark mode in the material (sheets 0001 and 0002).
- The recorded sequence goes from the opening to Craft; the sheets do not carry an image of its own for Delight, the eighth principle of the text (sheets 0001 and 0002).
