### What the illustrations show
Basis: 5 illustration sheets viewed (5 of 5), all codes checked, with 20 images, all in light version; no video listed for the page.
- The opening icon keeps the green composition with grid and guide circle, but swaps the single rectangle of the other devices for a vertical rectangle with a second flap coming out of the top right edge, like an open cover, and a small filled circle in the top right corner (img 0436).
- The Home Screen changes density between the screens: the outer one, narrow, has two widgets at the top and a grid of four columns by four rows; the inner one, nearly square, moves to six columns by four rows. The shortcuts column (phone, Safari, Messages, Music) stays fixed on the right edge, outside the grid, on both screens (img 0437, img 0438).
- The anatomy diagrams use black outline with labels: closed, the camera is a circle in the top right corner and the hinge a vertical line on the left (img 0439); on the inner screen, the hinge becomes a light blue vertical band in the center and the camera sits in the top right quadrant, near the fold (img 0440).
- The poses appear as six silhouettes in thin black line, side by side and with no content drawn: closed vertically, half open in a V like a propped-up book, open at nearly 180 degrees, book standing up, closed again, and laptop position with the fold at the base (img 0441).
- In Mail, the outer screen shows a single open email with the icon bar stacked on the right edge (back, arrows, trash, folder, reply, compose) (img 0442); on the inner screen, the same email shares the screen with the Inbox list on the left, adding a level of hierarchy (img 0443).
- The reserved regions are marked by color and label: dark blue circle "Outer camera region" in the corner of the outer screen (img 0444); on the inner screen, light blue band "Folding region" in the center with the "Inner camera region" next to it, that is, the inner camera sits inside the folding region (img 0445).
- In Notes, with the device fully open the list column is noticeably narrower than the content one (img 0446); partly folded, the two columns become the same width (img 0447).
- The arrangement views are drawn in two colors: in split, half light blue "Secondary view" and half light purple "Primary view", with no overlap (img 0448); in overlay, the secondary one takes up the whole screen and the primary one is a smaller rectangle centered on top, without touching the edges (img 0449).
- On the outer screen, the right edge stacks, from top to bottom, Dynamic Island, status bar, toolbar with two round buttons (back and more options), and tab bar with three icons, each one pointed to by a callout line (img 0450).
- The controls stay attached to the content they serve: in Split View, each app uses a light blue band of controls on its own outer edge, with no shared border in the center (img 0451); in Mail, the leading pane carries its controls at the top and the trailing one on the right vertical edge (img 0453).
- The Calculator redistributes the same buttons: four columns by five rows on the regular iPhone, five columns by four rows on the outer screen, wider and shorter (img 0452).
- In landscape on the outer screen, the toolbar and tab bar never appear full at the same time: either the toolbar items collapse into an ellipsis button below the back button and the tab bar stays complete (img 0454), or the toolbar appears expanded and the tab bar collapses into a single button (img 0455).
