### What the illustrations show
Basis: 1 illustration sheet viewed (1 of 1), all codes checked; no video listed for the page.
- The opening keeps the composition common to the platform pages: green rectangle, dotted grid and guide circle (img 0458).
- Apple Vision Pro is drawn as a dark green outlined stroke, thicker than that of the other opening sheets (img 0458).
- The shape is a continuous curve resembling diving goggles: two upper arcs joined together and a central indentation below, in place of the nose bridge (img 0458).
- The whole visor fits inside the guide circle and occupies practically its entire diameter, more than the rectangular icons of the other devices (img 0458).
- Among the openings recorded in the batch, it is the only icon with an organic shape, with no right angles, that starts from the device's physical form rather than from a rectangular screen (img 0458).
- The sheet carries only the light version of the illustration (img 0458).
