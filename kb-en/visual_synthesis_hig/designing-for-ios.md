### What the illustrations show
Basis: 1 illustration sheet viewed (1 of 1), all codes checked; no video listed for the page.
- The only image is a section-opening symbol, not a real screen: a rectangle with well-rounded corners in a gradient from green to lime green, with no text or caption (img 0434).
- Over the background there is a dotted construction grid, made of straight lines and a central circle, all aligned to the center of the composition (img 0434).
- The iPhone is drawn in a thick, dark green stroke, seen from the front, reduced to three elements: a rounded vertical frame, a small horizontal notch at the top for the camera and sensors and a horizontal bar at the bottom as the home indicator (img 0434).
- The device sits exactly on the crossing of the grid lines and inscribed in the guide circle, which shows the proportion of the outline is derived from a combined circular and rectangular grid, and not from a free drawing (img 0434).
- The green of the background corresponds, according to the official alt text recorded in the notes, to one of the six colors of the Apple logo; the sheet carries only the light version (img 0434).
