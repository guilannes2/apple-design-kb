# Visual essence, batch ess-02 (HIG, from game-controls to playing-video)

## The opening of each section is always the same chassis: card with rounded corners, thematic gradient, symbol in a single solid color and dotted construction grid left visible
- Evidence: generative-ai (img 0527, dark blue pencil with three stars over a rectangular and circular grid)
- Evidence: gyro-and-accelerometer (img 0537, hollow rings with a fine grid of horizontals, verticals and a pair of diagonals plus concentric circles)
- Evidence: icloud (img 0560, hollow cloud with a dashed circle tangent to the base dictating the curvature of the lobe)
- Evidence: onboarding (img 0824, waving hand in a dark red stroke with a guide circle centered on the palm)
- Evidence: playing-audio (img 0850, speaker in a thick stroke with a guide circle centered on the body)
- What this teaches about building interfaces: system identity comes from repeating the container, the stroke weight and the construction method, varying one parameter at a time. Leaving the grid visible turns the cover into a signature of the process, not just decoration.

## The gradient color changes by section and works as a subject code, with the artwork always in a single ink
- Evidence: healthkit, homekit, icloud, nfc and photo-editing use a blue gradient (img 0538, 0544, 0560, 0807, 0840)
- Evidence: launching, managing-accounts, managing-notifications, loading and playing-audio use an orange gradient (img 0679, 0751, 0752, 0732, 0850)
- Evidence: images, immersive-experiences, inclusion, motion and materials use a yellow gradient (img 0630, 0638, 0667, 0800, 0770)
- Evidence: keyboards uses purple and the synthesis itself compares the same construction scheme in blue and yellow (img 0671 against img 0644 and img 0667)
- What this teaches about building interfaces: a palette assigned by area gives orientation without requiring a label, as long as the shape and structure stay constant and only the hue changes.

## In dark mode the luminosity inverts, but hue, geometry and the position of the markings do not move
- Evidence: icons (img 0561, the stroke turns cream over darkened gold and the grid stays visible, more discreet)
- Evidence: icons (img 0562 and 0563, the band turns wine and the glyphs cream, with the guides in exactly the same positions; img 0564 to 0566, discs and glyph invert color and the measurements do not move; img 0623, the margin turns wine with the mark in the same place)
- Evidence: materials (img 0770, the background turns olive gold and the outlines lighten without abandoning the yellow hue)
- Evidence: icons (img 0567, the blue of the selected item is identical in both modes and only the ellipsis switches from black to white)
- What this teaches about building interfaces: dark mode is a swap of luminosity, not a redesign. The accent color and the dimensions must be the same in both themes.

## A good portion of the covers abandons the sketch and becomes a specification diagram, with dimensions and attributes named on top of the component

- What this teaches about building interfaces: the first image of a component can teach its anatomy. Naming the font, the role of the text and the system color in the artwork keeps the specification from living only in written documentation.

## The dimension notation is always the same: double horizontal arrow above for width, double vertical arrow to the right for height
- Evidence: image-views (img 0628, width above and height to the right of the glyph inside the frame)
- Evidence: ornaments (img 0825, horizontal arrow above the pill and vertical to the right)
- Evidence: panels (img 0835, horizontal below for width and vertical to the right for height)
- Evidence: lockups (img 0733) and gauges (img 0523) repeat the same pair of arrows
- What this teaches about building interfaces: annotation convention is part of the design system. Whoever reads the second plate already knows how to read the third without a legend.

## Red is the annotation color and pink is the empty-zone color (margin, padding, reserved spacing)
- Evidence: layout (img 0686, pink band of the tvOS safe area with the measurements written in red; img 0687, pink bands filling the padding between focusable cards)
- Evidence: icons (img 0623, outer pink square marking the margin with the measurement bracket; img 0564, pink bars marking the geometric center)
- Evidence: lockups (img 0734, vertical pink bands as reserved slack between columns)
- Evidence: playing-video (img 0855 and 0859, the built-in padding always appears as a pink band glued to the edges of the video)
- Evidence: live-activities (img 0711 and 0722, thin red border as a margin guide and dashed red line marking an empty band)
- What this teaches about building interfaces: separate the color that measures from the color that fills. Negative space becomes visible when it gets a color of its own, and that is how you discuss spacing without citing a number.

## Almost nothing is dimensioned with a numeric value: the guides mark relation and proportion, not absolute measurement
- Evidence: icons (img 0623, the 10% margin is described as the only numeric measurement on a page with 29 sheets)
- Evidence: layout (img 0686, 60 at the top and bottom and 80 on the sides are the only numbers on the page)
- Evidence: image-views (img 0628), lockups (img 0733), ornaments (img 0825), panels (img 0835), page-controls (img 0827) and outline-views (img 0826) carry measurement arrows with no value written
- What this teaches about building interfaces: the system is proportional before it is metric. Fixing a number too early stiffens the layout; marking the relation lets the same design scale across sizes and platforms.

## Right and wrong are marked by minimal, textless seals: white check in a green circle, white X in a gray circle
- Evidence: homekit (img 0545 and 0546)
- Evidence: menus (img 0791 to 0794, the X on the menu with arbitrary icons and the check on the text-only menu)
- Evidence: offering-help (img 0817 to 0820, 2x2 grid with the marker next to each variant)
- Evidence: materials (img 0774 to 0777, X on the gray symbol over material and check on the vibrant symbol, in both modes)
- Evidence: page-controls (img 0828 to 0831) and playing-video (img 0854 and 0856)
- What this teaches about building interfaces: the verdict must be discreet and always in the same place, so attention stays on the example and not on the judgment. Filled green approves, gray disapproves, and red stays reserved for annotation.

## The right-and-wrong pair changes only one variable and freezes the rest of the frame
- Evidence: menus (img 0791 to 0794, same day-of-the-week menu, only the presence of icons changes)
- Evidence: materials (img 0774 to 0777, same button and same background, only the color of the symbol changes)
- Evidence: page-controls (img 0828 and 0830, same bar from the Weather app with the same circular buttons, only the content of the pill changes)
- Evidence: live-activities (img 0720 and 0721, same compact, only the distance between the camera pill and the text block changes)
- Evidence: lists-and-tables (img 0698 and 0699, same row, only the trailing-edge element changes)
- What this teaches about building interfaces: comparison only teaches when it is controlled. If two examples differ in three things, nobody learns which one caused the problem.

## Variation series follow the same discipline: one dimension at a time, fixed container
- Evidence: layout (img 0682 to 0685, the size classes grow in width and height independently, over the same background)
- Evidence: imessage-apps-and-stickers (img 0635 to 0637, the iMessage bar and the category panel stay identical and the grid drops from four to three to two columns as the sticker grows)
- Evidence: materials (img 0778 to 0781, same icon and same background in an opacity progression from ultraThin to thick)
- Evidence: gauges (img 0524 compared with img 0525 and with img 0526, continuous against discrete and single color against color bands)
- Evidence: immersive-experiences (img 0639 to 0643, the glass panel stays in the same position and proportion and only how much of the real environment is replaced changes)
- What this teaches about building interfaces: variation is an axis, not a collection. Defining which parameter varies and locking the rest is what turns options into a system.

## Active state is marked by solid fill against outline or neutral, without changing the artwork
- Evidence: icons (img 0567, active button with the whole circle filled blue against a neutral ellipsis; img 0604 to 0607, the focused layer stands out only through solid fill, keeping the shape)
- Evidence: menus (img 0790, active submenu item with solid red fill)
- Evidence: outline-views (img 0826, selected row in solid red with white text, inverting the contrast)
- Evidence: in-app-purchase (img 0651, annual plan with a filled red circle and monthly with an empty circle)
- Evidence: page-controls (img 0832 to 0834, the current page dot is the only one filled, black on light in iOS and white on dark in watchOS)
- What this teaches about building interfaces: change weight and fill, never the silhouette. Swapping the icon between states forces the eye to re-identify the element with every change.

## Momentary highlight and persistent choice use marks of different purpose
- Evidence: menus (img 0790 with solid fill for hover or momentary selection against img 0796, in which a toggleable state is marked only by a discreet checkmark on the left margin)
- Evidence: pickers (img 0845 and 0846, green outline marking the focus on the wheel, against img 0849, in which the chosen item carries a green check on the right)
- Evidence: in-app-purchase (img 0648, chosen reason marked with a blue check in a list with only dividers)
- What this teaches about building interfaces: focus and selection are distinct things and need two visual languages. Using the same mark for both makes the user think they have already chosen when they are only navigating.

## Focus on tvOS is elevation: the item grows, lightens to white, gains a shadow and rises a layer, and the layout reserves the slack
- Evidence: lockups (img 0734 to 0738, the focused item turns white among gray neighbors, with shadow and larger size; the focused poster rises a layer)
- Evidence: layout (img 0687, larger central card with shadow, with pink bands filling the padding between it and its neighbors)
- Evidence: materials (img 0782, the active tab turns into an opaque white pill over the translucent material)
- What this teaches about building interfaces: if focus enlarges the element, the spacing needs to be sized for the larger state, not the resting state.

## Translucent material has no color of its own: it takes on the tone of what's behind it, and it is the content that has to guarantee contrast
- Evidence: materials (img 0771 and 0772, the same circle turns dark gray over a starry sky and whitish over a beach photo; img 0773, the clear variant blurs less and leaves the brick pattern recognizable)
- Evidence: materials (video 015, sheets 0002 and 0003, the room darkens and the window darkens along with it, becoming grayer and more opaque without the text losing contrast)
- Evidence: keyboards (video 014, a narrow, dark brown, translucent bar floating over the wooden table, with white text)
- Evidence: multitasking (video 016, two translucent visionOS windows coexisting over a real scene that changes behind them)
- What this teaches about building interfaces: material is a layer of context, not of legibility. Contrast has to come from the color and weight of the content, because the background will change without warning.

## Difficult legibility is solved by removing content and increasing weight, not by adding a box
- Evidence: live-activities (img 0707 versus img 0709, two small lines with a label and a code are replaced by a single larger, heavier code, with the error and success badges in img 0708 and 0710)
- Evidence: materials (img 0774 to 0777, the symbol in systemGray3 disappears into the material, and the same symbol in a vibrant color resolves without changing the button)
- Evidence: live-photos (img 0729 and 0730, a semi-transparent white badge over a dark nighttime photo, with the text-free variant reduced to a small circle)
- What this teaches about building interfaces: when space is minimal, cut information before shrinking typography, and solve contrast in the symbol's color before inventing a new background.

## Transition between states is always gradual and local, never a hard cut
- Evidence: gestures (video 010, sheet 0001, q001 to q005, the background of the heart button progressively lightens from translucent gray to solid white without anything changing position or size)
- Evidence: immersive-experiences (video 013, sheet 0002, q014 to q018, the inner scene fades in transparently over the landscape and grows frame by frame, with a visible diagonal transition line)
- Evidence: multitasking (video 016, sheet 0002, q012, q016 and q018, a light vertical stripe appears only at the border between the two windows, a localized and subtle effect)
- Evidence: game-controls (img 0517 and 0518, the same thumbstick goes from opaque white with a direction arrow to translucent dark gray at rest)
- What this teaches about building interfaces: animate the property that carries meaning (opacity, tone, border) and leave position and size still. Localized change communicates without reorganizing the screen.

## Direct input and indirect input call for different types of response
- Evidence: gestures (video 010, in the indirect gesture the button highlights before contact and then turns solid white, versus video 011, where the direct touch has no highlight or halo and the response is physical, with blocks spinning and falling)
- Evidence: game-controls (img 0519, the same button distinguishes a simple tap with a full ring from a prolonged touch with a partial ring, as a progress indicator)
- What this teaches about building interfaces: when the finger touches the object, physics is already the feedback; when the aim is remote, the interface has to show beforehand what's going to be hit.

## Action hierarchy by shape: filled, then outlined, then plain text with no background
- Evidence: machine-learning (img 0748, a purple filled button, an outlined button and a third action in text only, in the same column)
- Evidence: healthkit (img 0542, a white filled pill button to sync and the skip option right below it as plain text with no background)
- Evidence: in-app-purchase (img 0654, plans as green pill buttons and code redemption as a text link; img 0657, manage, restore and redeem as blue links stacked outside the block; img 0660, cancellation as a centered red link)
- What this teaches about building interfaces: the secondary action loses its background before it loses its color, and the tertiary action loses both the background and the prominent position. This keeps a single obvious action per screen.

## The list row is a fixed template, and the slot at the trailing edge declares the intent
- Evidence: lists-and-tables (img 0698 with a circular blue "i" icon to reveal information, versus img 0699, the same structure with a chevron and a secondary gray value to navigate)
- Evidence: healthkit (img 0540, each category row with a colored icon, name and a green toggle on the right)
- Evidence: homekit (img 0558, a row with an icon on the left, title, simulated secondary text and a chevron on the right)
- Evidence: home-screen-quick-actions (img 0543, four rows with a geometric symbol at the leading edge and a label on the right)
- What this teaches about building interfaces: keep the row's skeleton and swap only the trailing element. The user learns to predict the result of the tap from the symbol on the right.

## A grouped list is a card inside a card, with thin dividers and a header or footer outside the card
- Evidence: lists-and-tables (img 0697, a header above and a footer below the card, three rows inside; img 0698 and 0699, a light gray card containing an inner white card)
- Evidence: healthkit (img 0540, write and read in separate sections with a gray uppercase label outside the block)
- Evidence: in-app-purchase (img 0646, five options in a single capsule with thin dividers and chevrons; img 0666, a single card with a gray uppercase title and a green check on the chosen option)
- What this teaches about building interfaces: the card delimits the group and the label explains the group from outside, which allows stacking sections without creating a frame inside a frame.

## Deliberate placeholder: blind shapes, gray wireframe, dashed icon and blurred text when the lesson is about layout
- Evidence: live-activities (img 0701 to 0703, black shapes labeled by callout lines instead of real content; img 0718 and 0719, content deliberately blurred inside a dashed red box)
- Evidence: in-app-purchase (img 0658, the redemption screen as a wireframe inside an iPhone frame, with a dashed icon area and an empty field, with no brand color)

- Evidence: machine-learning (img 0743, a standard context menu over a gray iPhone with no app color, with four generic rows above the divider)
- What this teaches about building interfaces: erasing the content is the way to prove that the structure works on its own. Attractive content hides layout problems.

## Blur and dimming direct the eye within the image itself, reducing the surroundings instead of inflating the target
- Evidence: home-screen-quick-actions (img 0543, the neighboring icons become blurred and only the origin icon stays sharp)
- Evidence: in-app-purchase (img 0653, the same onboarding screen reappears with the button highlighted by an outline and the rest dimmed; img 0656, the redeem link gains a solid black capsule while the green buttons are dimmed)

- What this teaches about building interfaces: an overlay that dims the background keeps context and still creates focus. Pushing the content is the alternative when the context needs to stay legible.

## Every ephemeral component is born anchored to the element that originated it, and the origin stays marked
- Evidence: home-screen-quick-actions (img 0543, the menu card sits right above the origin app icon)
- Evidence: menus (img 0798, the visionOS menu emerges below the more-options button, which appears selected in solid white)
- Evidence: maps (img 0759, the compact place card stays anchored right above the pin; img 0760, the caption reduces everything to the label under the same pin)

- What this teaches about building interfaces: the anchor explains where the panel came from and where it goes back to. Without it, the element seems to have appeared out of nowhere and the user loses the context reference.

## A cut at the edge is the standard cue that there is more content

## A system glyph is the same shape in black on white and white on black, isolated, with no card and no grid
- Evidence: icons (img 0570 to 0610, standard actions presented as a single centered glyph, with an identical shape in both contrasts)

## Opposite meanings come from inverting the same drawing, never from two different drawings
- Evidence: icons (img 0576 and 0577, undo and redo are the same curved arrow mirrored; img 0602 and 0603, like and dislike use the same outlined hand with the thumb inverted)

## Growing concentric waves from one side only is the motif reserved for communication at a distance
- Evidence: nfc (img 0807, three growing concentric arcs, the same motif as nearby-interactions without the central circle)
- Evidence: nearby-interactions (img 0806, two concentric curves arriving from only one side of the circle, with direction communicated by the asymmetry)

## When reduced in size, the art loses elements instead of shrinking everything together
- Evidence: icons (img 0618, 0620, 0621 and 0622, the grid loses lines, the electrocardiogram stroke thickens, the text becomes illegible and at the minimum size only the heart remains)
- Evidence: mac-catalyst (img 0740 and 0741, the same pixelated icon gains margin and smaller pixel blocks in the Mac idiom, giving an impression of more sharpness)
- Evidence: images (img 0631 to 0633, the same shape over grids of 10, 20 and 30 pixels per side, with a jagged edge and intermediate gray pixels at the smallest)
- What this teaches about building interfaces: define what survives at each size before drawing the largest one. Detail that turns into noise at small size should disappear, not thin out.
